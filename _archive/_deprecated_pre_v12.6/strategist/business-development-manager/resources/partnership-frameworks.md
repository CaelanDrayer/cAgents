# Partnership Frameworks

## Partnership Proposal Template

```markdown
# Partnership Proposal: [Partner]

**Opportunity**: [What we can do together]

**Strategic Rationale**:
- Market: [Shared opportunity]
- Gap: [Problem we solve together]
- Value: [Benefit quantified]

**Value Proposition**:
- **For Us**: [What we gain]
- **For Partner**: [What they gain]
- **For Customers**: [What customers gain]

**Deal Structure**:
- [Revenue model]
- [Investment required]
- [Timeline]

**Success Metrics**:
- Year 1: [Targets]
- Year 2: [Targets]
- Year 3: [Targets]

**Risks**: [Key risks and mitigation]
```

## Partnership Evaluation Scorecard

| Criteria | Weight | Score (1-5) | Weighted |
|----------|--------|-------------|----------|
| Strategic Fit | 30% | [1-5] | - |
| Market Opportunity | 25% | [1-5] | - |
| Value Proposition | 20% | [1-5] | - |
| Execution Capability | 15% | [1-5] | - |
| Cultural Fit | 10% | [1-5] | - |
| **Total** | 100% | - | [Score] |

**Decision**: Proceed (≥4.0), Reconsider (3.0-3.9), Reject (<3.0)

## Partnership Lifecycle

1. **Prospect**: Identify potential partners
2. **Qualify**: Evaluate fit and opportunity
3. **Negotiate**: Structure deal and terms
4. **Launch**: Execute and go-to-market
5. **Grow**: Scale partnership value
6. **Optimize**: Improve or exit

## Deal Structures

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Revenue Share | % of revenue from partnership |
| Referral Fee | Fee per lead or sale |
| Co-Marketing | Shared marketing costs |
| Equity | Investment or JV |
| Licensing | License technology/IP |
