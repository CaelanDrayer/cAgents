# Fantasy Tropes and Conventions

## Classic Tropes
- The Chosen One
- Dark Lord antagonist
- Wise mentor figure
- Band of diverse companions
- Magical artifacts/MacGuffins
- Hidden heritage revealed
- Coming of age journey

## Magic System Types
- **Hard Magic**: Clear rules, limitations
- **Soft Magic**: Mysterious, wonder-based
- **Hybrid**: Known and unknown elements

## Fantasy Subgenres
- Epic/High Fantasy
- Sword and Sorcery
- Urban Fantasy
- Dark Fantasy
- Cozy Fantasy
- Grimdark
- Portal Fantasy

## Reader Expectations
- Immersive worldbuilding
- Escapism and wonder
- Clear stakes
- Satisfying resolution
- Character growth
- Magical elements

## Pitfalls to Avoid
- Overcomplicated magic
- Info-dumping worldbuilding
- Inconsistent rules
- Derivative settings
- Weak female characters
