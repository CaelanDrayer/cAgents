# Employment Law Frameworks

## Investigation Protocol

### Phase 1: Intake
- Receive complaint/report
- Document initial allegations
- Assess urgency and interim measures
- Assign investigator

### Phase 2: Planning
- Define scope and issues
- Identify witnesses and documents
- Create investigation timeline
- Prepare interview questions

### Phase 3: Investigation
- Conduct witness interviews
- Collect and review documents
- Maintain confidentiality
- Document all evidence

### Phase 4: Analysis
- Evaluate credibility
- Apply legal standards
- Assess policy violations
- Develop findings

### Phase 5: Conclusion
- Draft investigation report
- Recommend corrective actions
- Present to decision-makers
- Follow up on implementation

## Compliance Checklist

### Wage and Hour
- [ ] Exempt/non-exempt classification
- [ ] Minimum wage compliance
- [ ] Overtime calculations
- [ ] Meal and rest breaks
- [ ] Timekeeping practices

### Discrimination Prevention
- [ ] EEO policies current
- [ ] Anti-harassment training
- [ ] Reasonable accommodations process
- [ ] Complaint procedures

### Employment Documentation
- [ ] Offer letters
- [ ] Employment agreements
- [ ] Handbook acknowledgments
- [ ] Performance documentation
- [ ] Termination records

## Risk Assessment Matrix

| Risk Area | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|-----------|------------|--------|------------|
| Classification | Medium | High | Regular audits |
| Harassment | Medium | High | Training, reporting |
| Termination | High | Medium | Documentation, review |
| Discrimination | Low | High | Policies, training |

## Key Deadlines

- EEOC charge response: 30 days
- FMLA certification: 15 days
- I-9 completion: 3 days
- WARN notice: 60 days

## Collaboration

- HR: Policy implementation, training
- General Counsel: Litigation strategy
- External Counsel: Complex matters, trials
