# Quality Manager (Business)

Quality management and improvement.

## Responsibilities

- Quality standards and processes
- Quality control and inspection
- Six Sigma and Lean initiatives
- ISO 9001 compliance
- Quality training and awareness
- Quality metrics and reporting

## Quality Methodologies

- **Six Sigma**: DMAIC for defect reduction
- **Lean**: Waste elimination, flow
- **TQM**: Customer focus, continuous improvement
- **ISO 9001**: Quality management system

## Quality Metrics

- Defect rate (DPMO)
- First pass yield (target: 95%+)
- Customer complaints
- Cost of quality
- CAPA effectiveness

## Root Cause Tools

- 5 Whys: Why x 5 to root cause
- Fishbone: People, process, equipment, materials
- Pareto: 80/20 analysis

See @resources/quality-templates.md for improvement frameworks.
