# Best Practices: Facilities Manager

> Design principles, patterns, and frameworks that guide high-quality facilities operations, space planning, and workplace management work.

## Design Principles

- **Proactive Maintenance Over Reactive Repair**: Planned preventive maintenance costs a fraction of emergency repairs and avoids productivity disruption — always invest in prevention.
- **Space Supports Culture**: Physical environment shapes how people work and collaborate; space decisions are culture decisions, not just real estate decisions.
- **Safety Is Non-Negotiable**: No cost constraint justifies compromising life-safety systems, fire suppression, emergency egress, or OSHA compliance — safety is the floor, not a variable.
- **Vendor Relationships Are Strategic Assets**: High-performing vendor relationships require investment in communication, fair contracts, and performance accountability — not just lowest-bid selection.
- **Data-Driven Space Utilization**: Decisions about space allocation, lease renewals, and expansions must be grounded in utilization data, not anecdote or seniority-based entitlement.
- **Sustainability as Operations Standard**: Energy efficiency, waste reduction, and environmental compliance reduce cost and legal exposure while meeting growing employee and regulatory expectations.
- **Service Mentality**: Internal customers (employees) judge the workplace experience by every detail — responsiveness, cleanliness, temperature, and functionality all drive productivity and retention.

## Key Patterns & Frameworks

- **Preventive Maintenance Schedule (PMS)**: Calendar-driven maintenance tasks for each asset class (HVAC, electrical, plumbing, elevators) to extend asset life and prevent failures.
- **Space Utilization Analysis**: Sensor or badge data measuring how often and how intensively spaces are used. Apply before lease renewals or space reconfigurations to eliminate underutilized real estate.
- **Activity-Based Working (ABW)**: Workplace design providing a variety of settings (focus zones, collaboration spaces, phone booths, lounges) matched to different work modes rather than assigned desks.
- **IWMS (Integrated Workplace Management System)**: Software platform managing space, maintenance, moves, and environmental data. Provides data foundation for facilities decision-making.
- **Total Cost of Occupancy (TCO)**: Complete cost per square foot including rent, utilities, maintenance, security, and cleaning — enables true comparison across locations and space strategies.
- **Vendor Scorecard**: Structured quarterly assessment of vendor performance against contracted SLAs (response time, quality, safety compliance, customer satisfaction). Drives accountability.
- **Emergency Response Plan (ERP)**: Documented procedures for fire, medical emergencies, severe weather, and building failures. Must be tested annually and communicated to all occupants.
- **Lease Abstraction**: Summary of critical lease terms (expiration, renewal options, tenant improvement allowances, rent escalations) enabling proactive lease management.
- **Move/Add/Change (MAC) Process**: Standardized workflow for managing employee relocations, new hires, and workspace changes — prevents ad-hoc moves that disrupt neighbors and waste resources.
- **Green Building Certification (LEED / BREEAM)**: Environmental performance certification frameworks guiding energy, water, materials, and indoor air quality standards for sustainable facilities.

## Domain Concepts & Terminology

### Space Management
- **Gross Square Footage (GSF)**: Total floor area including walls, mechanical rooms, and common areas
- **Net Usable Square Footage (NUSF)**: Space actually available for occupancy, excluding structural and mechanical elements
- **Density Ratio**: Square feet per person — measures how intensively space is used (typical: 150-250 SF/person for modern offices)
- **Hoteling / Hot Desking**: Shared, unassigned workstations reserved on-demand rather than permanently assigned
- **Seat Ratio**: Number of desks divided by total headcount — ratios below 1.0 require hoteling or shift-based scheduling
- **Churn Rate**: % of employees changing seats annually — high churn (>40%) signals poor space planning or rapid growth

### Maintenance & Operations
- **Preventive Maintenance (PM)**: Scheduled maintenance performed before failure to extend asset life
- **Corrective Maintenance**: Reactive repair after a failure occurs — ideally minimized through strong PM programs
- **MTBF (Mean Time Between Failures)**: Average time a system operates between failures — key metric for planning PM intervals
- **MTTR (Mean Time To Repair)**: Average time to restore a failed system to operation — measure of maintenance response effectiveness
- **Critical Asset**: Facility asset whose failure directly impacts business operations (HVAC in data centers, elevators in multi-story buildings)
- **Capital Expenditure (CapEx) vs. Operational Expenditure (OpEx)**: Distinction between improvement investments (CapEx) and ongoing operating costs (OpEx) — important for budget planning and approval processes

### Compliance & Safety
- **OSHA (Occupational Safety and Health Administration)**: US regulatory agency setting workplace safety standards
- **Certificate of Occupancy (CO)**: Official approval confirming a building meets code requirements for its intended use
- **ADA (Americans with Disabilities Act)**: Accessibility standards for public and commercial buildings
- **Fire Life Safety (FLS)**: System of equipment (sprinklers, alarms, exits) and procedures ensuring occupant safety in fire events
- **Environmental Health & Safety (EHS)**: Integrated function covering workplace safety, environmental compliance, and health programs

### Vendor & Contracts
- **SLA (Service Level Agreement)**: Contractual commitment to response time and quality metrics for vendor services
- **Preferred Vendor Program**: Pre-approved vendor list with negotiated rates, ensuring quality and efficiency at scale
- **RFP (Request for Proposal)**: Competitive bidding process for significant facility services or capital projects

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- **Run-to-Failure Maintenance**: Waiting for equipment to break before servicing it. Fix: implement preventive maintenance schedules for all critical assets; the cost difference is 3-5x in emergency repair costs.
- **Space Allocated by Seniority**: Assigning the most space to most senior employees without regard to actual utilization or work needs. Fix: use utilization data and work-mode analysis to right-size all spaces.
- **Single-Vendor Dependency**: Relying on one vendor for critical services without backup options. Fix: maintain at least two qualified vendors for each critical service category.
- **Deferred Maintenance Accumulation**: Postponing maintenance investments to meet short-term budget targets. Fix: track deferred maintenance backlog; deferred costs compound with interest in emergency repairs.
- **Missing Emergency Plan**: Operating without tested emergency procedures because "nothing has gone wrong yet." Fix: treat emergency preparedness as non-negotiable; test annually.
- **Lease Surprise**: Missing lease expirations, renewal options, or rent escalation clauses due to poor lease tracking. Fix: maintain lease abstracts with 18-month forward visibility and calendar alerts.
- **Reactive Service Model**: Waiting for employees to report problems rather than proactively inspecting. Fix: implement inspection rounds and predictive sensor monitoring to surface issues before they become complaints.

## Quality Indicators

- **Preventive Maintenance Completion Rate**: % of scheduled PM tasks completed on time (target: >95%) — leading indicator of equipment reliability.
- **Work Order Response Time**: Average time from request submission to work order completion by priority level (critical: <4 hours; standard: <48 hours).
- **Space Utilization Rate**: % of available workstations occupied during peak hours (target: 70-80% for hoteling environments) — below 60% signals over-provisioning.
- **Facilities Satisfaction Score**: Employee rating of the workplace environment (comfort, cleanliness, functionality) in periodic surveys (target: >4.0/5.0).
- **Vendor SLA Compliance Rate**: % of vendor deliverables meeting contracted SLA commitments (target: >95%).
- **Energy Cost per Square Foot**: Utility spend divided by occupied square footage — benchmark against industry standards to identify optimization opportunities.
- **Safety Incident Rate**: Number of workplace safety incidents per 100 employees — regulatory compliance threshold and cultural health signal.

## Collaboration Touchpoints

- **With Operations Manager**: Quality looks like facilities metrics integrated into operational dashboards, space and maintenance plans aligned to operational needs, and escalation paths defined for critical facility failures.
- **With HR / People Team**: Quality looks like space planning aligned to headcount forecasts, onboarding space ready before start dates, and return-to-office or hybrid work policies translated into space design.
- **With Finance Manager**: Quality looks like facilities budget with accurate TCO calculations, CapEx/OpEx separation for planning purposes, and lease commitments visible in long-range financial forecasts.
- **With Vendors**: Quality looks like quarterly SLA reviews with documented performance data, clear escalation paths, and contract terms that incentivize quality rather than just lowest cost.
