# Product Owner Decision Framework

## Priority Decisions

Use value vs. effort matrix:

| Value | Effort | Action |
|-------|--------|--------|
| High | Low | **Do first** - Quick wins that deliver maximum value |
| High | High | **Strategic investment** - Plan carefully, deliver in phases |
| Low | Low | **Nice-to-have** - Do if capacity allows after high-value work |
| Low | High | **Don't do** - Poor ROI, reject or defer indefinitely |

## Scope Decisions

When scope must be cut:

1. **Preserve core business value** (MVP) - What's the minimum that delivers value?
2. **Defer nice-to-have features** - What can wait for a future release?
3. **Simplify implementation** - Can we achieve the value with a simpler approach?
4. **Communicate rationale** - Explain why to stakeholders clearly

## Go/No-Go Decisions

Release when ALL criteria met:

- ✅ **Business value is delivered** - Core functionality works as intended
- ✅ **Acceptance criteria met** - All success metrics achieved
- ✅ **Critical bugs resolved** - No blockers or high-severity issues
- ✅ **Stakeholder acceptance obtained** - Business approves the release
- ✅ **Release timing aligns** - Market timing and business needs align

## Success Metrics

Track Product Owner effectiveness:

- Business value delivered per iteration
- Stakeholder satisfaction with priorities
- Clarity of requirements and acceptance criteria
- Speed of decision-making
- Alignment between delivered work and product vision
- Feature adoption and user satisfaction
