# OKR Best Practices

## Good Objectives
- Ambitious and inspirational
- Qualitative (not metrics)
- Action-oriented
- Clear and concise
- 3-5 per quarter

## Good Key Results
- Measurable and quantifiable
- Specific and time-bound
- Outcome-focused (not output)
- Challenging but achievable
- 2-4 per objective

## Example
**Objective**: Become the market leader in customer satisfaction

**Key Results**:
- KR1: Increase NPS from 40 to 60
- KR2: Reduce average support response time from 24h to 4h
- KR3: Achieve 95% customer retention rate

## Grading Scale
- 0.0-0.3: Failed to achieve
- 0.4-0.6: Made progress
- 0.7-1.0: Fully achieved (or exceeded)

Target: 60-70% achievement (stretch goals)

## Common Mistakes
- Too many OKRs
- Output-focused KRs
- Not ambitious enough
- Not reviewing regularly
- Not connected to strategy
