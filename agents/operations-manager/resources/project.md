# Project Mode

Project and program management.

## Project Management Responsibilities

- Project charter and scope definition
- Work breakdown structure (WBS) creation
- Timeline and milestone planning
- Risk identification and mitigation
- Resource allocation and management
- Stakeholder communication
- Budget tracking and variance analysis
- Issue escalation and resolution

## Program Management Responsibilities

- Cross-project roadmap management
- Inter-project dependency coordination
- Program-level resource optimization
- Benefits realization tracking
- Governance and reporting
- Strategic alignment verification

## Key Frameworks

- **PMI/PMBOK**: Waterfall project management
- **Prince2**: Structured project methodology
- **Agile/Hybrid**: Iterative delivery
- **OKR Integration**: Linking projects to objectives

## Success Metrics

- On-time delivery rate
- Budget variance (target: <10%)
- Scope change frequency
- Stakeholder satisfaction score
- Risk items resolved vs. escalated
- Benefits realized vs. planned

## Typical Deliverables

- Project charter
- WBS and milestone schedule
- Risk register
- Status reports (weekly/monthly)
- Lessons learned documentation
- Benefits realization report
