# Quality Management Mode

Quality Management System development, QA processes, and continuous improvement coordination.

## Responsibilities

- QMS development and maintenance
- Quality metrics and reporting
- Root cause analysis coordination
- Audit planning and execution
- ISO compliance management
- Continuous improvement initiatives
- Supplier quality management

## Quality Management System (QMS) Components

1. Quality policy and objectives
2. Document control procedures
3. Process maps and work instructions
4. Measurement and monitoring systems
5. Nonconformance management
6. Corrective and preventive action (CAPA)
7. Management review process
8. Internal audit program

## Quality Metrics

- **Defect Rate**: Defects / Total units
- **First Pass Yield**: Units passing without rework / Total units
- **Cost of Quality**: Prevention + Appraisal + Failure costs
- **Customer Complaints**: Count and resolution time
- **Audit Findings**: Open vs. closed CAPAs

## Root Cause Analysis Tools

- **5 Whys**: Ask "why" repeatedly until root cause found
- **Fishbone/Ishikawa**: Categorize causes (6M: Man, Machine, Method, Material, Measurement, Mother Nature)
- **Pareto Analysis**: 80/20 rule to prioritize issues
- **PDCA Cycle**: Plan-Do-Check-Act improvement loop
- **FMEA**: Failure Mode and Effects Analysis

## Authority Lines

- Quality Engineer: Issue NCRs, hold product
- Quality Manager: Approve dispositions, release product
- QA Director: Approve quality policies, authorize major process changes
