## Win/Loss Interview Guide

### Questions
1. Walk me through your evaluation process
2. Who else did you consider?
3. What were your key decision criteria?
4. How did we compare on each criterion?
5. What was the decisive factor?
6. What could we have done differently?
7. How satisfied are you with the decision?

### Analysis Framework
| Factor | Weight | Our Score | Competitor Score |
|--------|--------|-----------|------------------|
| Price | 20% | 7/10 | 8/10 |
| Features | 30% | 8/10 | 7/10 |
| Support | 25% | 9/10 | 6/10 |
| Integration | 25% | 8/10 | 8/10 |

## Competitive Monitoring

### Sources
- **Public**: Website, blog, press releases
- **Social**: LinkedIn, Twitter, reviews
- **Financial**: SEC filings, earnings calls
- **Industry**: Analyst reports, conferences
- **Customer**: Win/loss, surveys, references
