## Market Sizing

### TAM/SAM/SOM Framework
| Level | Definition | Calculation |
|-------|------------|-------------|
| TAM | Total Addressable Market | All potential customers * avg revenue |
| SAM | Serviceable Addressable Market | TAM * % we can serve |
| SOM | Serviceable Obtainable Market | SAM * realistic market share |

### Approaches
- **Top-Down**: Industry data -> segments -> target
- **Bottom-Up**: Unit economics -> customer count -> market
- **Triangulation**: Use both, reconcile differences

## Survey Design

### Question Types
| Type | Use For | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| Multiple Choice | Categorical data | "Which product do you use?" |
| Likert Scale | Attitudes, satisfaction | "Rate 1-5: Ease of use" |
| Open-Ended | Exploration, quotes | "What would you improve?" |
| Ranking | Priorities | "Rank features by importance" |

### Survey Best Practices
- Keep surveys under 10 minutes
- Start with easy questions
- Group related questions
- Avoid leading questions
- Include validation questions
- Test with pilot group

## Interview Guide Template

```markdown
# Interview Guide: [Topic]

## Introduction (5 min)
- Thank you for your time
- Purpose of research
- Confidentiality notice
- Permission to record

## Warm-up (5 min)
- Role and background
- Company overview

## Core Questions (40 min)
### Topic 1: [Current State]
- Tell me about...
- What challenges do you face?
- Walk me through your process...

### Topic 2: [Needs/Pain Points]
- What would make your job easier?
- If you could change one thing...

### Topic 3: [Evaluation Criteria]
- What do you look for in a solution?
- How do you make decisions?

## Wrap-up (5 min)
- Anything else to add?
- Can we follow up?
- Referrals to others?
```

## Industry Analysis

### Porter's Five Forces
| Force | Questions |
|-------|-----------|
| Rivalry | How intense? Price competition? |
| New Entrants | Barriers to entry? Threat level? |
| Substitutes | Alternative solutions? Switching costs? |
| Buyer Power | Concentration? Price sensitivity? |
| Supplier Power | Dependencies? Switching costs? |

### PEST Analysis
- **Political**: Regulations, trade, government policy
- **Economic**: Growth, interest rates, exchange rates
- **Social**: Demographics, culture, trends
- **Technological**: Innovation, disruption, adoption

## Research Synthesis

### Insight Development
1. **Pattern Recognition**: Themes across data sources
2. **Interpretation**: What does it mean?
3. **Implications**: So what for the business?
4. **Recommendations**: What should we do?

### Presentation Structure
1. Executive Summary (1 page)
2. Research Objectives & Methodology
3. Key Findings (with evidence)
4. Insights & Implications
5. Recommendations
6. Appendix (detailed data)
