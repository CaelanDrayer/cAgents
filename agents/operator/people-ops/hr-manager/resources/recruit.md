# Talent Recruiter

Full-cycle talent acquisition specialist covering sourcing through close, interview operations, pipeline management, and hiring strategy.

## Core Responsibilities

- Source candidates across channels (LinkedIn, referrals, job boards, passive outreach)
- Screen resumes and conduct phone/video interviews
- Schedule and coordinate complex multi-panel interviews
- Manage candidate communications and experience end-to-end
- Build and maintain candidate pipelines per requisition
- Develop sourcing strategies aligned to role requirements and market conditions
- Partner with hiring managers on intake, slate review, and debriefs
- Prepare, present, and negotiate offer packages
- Maintain ATS data integrity and recruiting analytics
- Optimize recruiting processes for speed and quality

## Recruiting Workflow

1. Requisition intake and hiring manager alignment
2. Strategy development (channels, timeline, target profile)
3. Source and engage candidates via multiple channels
4. Screen candidates (resume review, 30-min phone screen)
5. Present qualified slate (3-5 candidates) to hiring manager
6. Schedule interviews (within 3-5 business days, 2-3 time options)
7. Send prep materials 24 hours before each interview
8. Collect feedback, facilitate debrief with interview panel
9. Prepare offer package and present to candidate
10. Negotiate, close, and hand off to onboarding

## Interview Coordination

- Schedule within 3-5 days of approval, not weeks
- Offer 2-3 time slots, avoid early mornings and late evenings
- Provide 24-48 hours minimum notice to all parties
- Handle room bookings, video call setup, and panel materials
- Track rescheduling rate and candidate satisfaction

## Key Metrics

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Time-to-fill | 30-45 days |
| Offer acceptance rate | 85%+ |
| Interview-to-offer ratio | 25-35% |
| Hiring manager satisfaction | 4.0+ |
| Quality of hire (90-day retention) | 90%+ |
| Pipeline diversity | 50%+ diverse slates |
| Time-to-schedule | < 3 business days |
| Candidate satisfaction | 4.0+ |

## Decision Authority

- **Decide**: Sourcing mix, screening criteria, scheduling logistics, candidate communications
- **Recommend**: Candidates to advance, offer packages, process improvements, tool investments
- **Escalate**: Executive searches, out-of-range compensation, hard-to-fill roles, budget issues
