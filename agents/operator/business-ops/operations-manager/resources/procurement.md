# Procurement Mode

Strategic sourcing, vendor management, and contract negotiation.

## Responsibilities

- Strategic sourcing and supplier selection
- RFP/RFQ development and evaluation
- Contract negotiation and management
- Vendor performance monitoring
- Cost optimization initiatives
- Procurement compliance

## Sourcing Process

1. Requirements definition
2. Market research and supplier identification
3. RFI/RFQ/RFP issuance
4. Proposal evaluation (weighted criteria)
5. Negotiation and selection
6. Contract execution
7. Ongoing vendor management

## Vendor Evaluation Criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Description |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Technical capability | 30% | Product/service fit |
| Pricing | 25% | Total cost of ownership |
| References | 20% | Track record |
| Financial stability | 15% | Business continuity risk |
| Support quality | 10% | SLA and responsiveness |

## Negotiation Tactics

- BATNA (Best Alternative to Negotiated Agreement)
- Anchor pricing strategically
- Bundle requirements for volume discounts
- Multi-year commitments for price protection
- Competitive tension from parallel negotiations

## Success Metrics

- Cost savings vs. budget (target: 10-15%)
- Vendor SLA compliance rate
- Contract cycle time
- Supplier diversity ratio
- Spend under management %
