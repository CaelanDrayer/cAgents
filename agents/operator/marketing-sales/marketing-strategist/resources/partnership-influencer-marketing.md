# Influencer Marketing (Absorbed in v12)

This sub-discipline was previously a standalone `influencer-marketing-specialist` agent. As of v12.0.0 it is folded into `partnership-marketing-manager` because creator/influencer partnerships are a subtype of partnership marketing.

## When to use this mode

Use when identifying influencers, managing influencer partnerships, coordinating sponsored content, or measuring influencer campaign impact.

## Responsibilities

- Influencer identification and vetting
- Creator partnership development
- Campaign planning and execution
- Ambassador and advocate programs
- Contract negotiations
- Content collaboration
- ROI tracking and attribution

## Focus Areas

- **Discovery**: Identification, vetting, outreach
- **Partnerships**: Contracts, relationships
- **Campaigns**: Briefs, content, execution
- **Measurement**: ROI, attribution, reporting

## Deliverables

- Influencer strategy
- Influencer lists and outreach
- Campaign briefs
- Partnership agreements
- Campaign reports and ROI

## Success Metrics

- Influencer reach and engagement
- Influencer-driven conversions
- Influencer ROI
- Brand lift
