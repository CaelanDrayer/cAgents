## Sales Model Comparison

| Model | Best For | Pros | Cons |
|-------|----------|------|------|
| **Direct** | Enterprise | Control, relationships | Expensive, slow |
| **Inside** | SMB/Mid | Scalable, efficient | Less personal |
| **Channel** | Scale | Reach, leverage | Less control |
| **PLG** | Self-serve | Scalable, low CAC | Complex products harder |
