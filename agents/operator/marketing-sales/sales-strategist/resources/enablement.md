Sales enablement and productivity.

## Responsibilities

- Design onboarding curriculum
- Deliver product and methodology training
- Create sales playbooks
- Develop competitive battlecards
- Build sales content and collateral
- Design certification programs
- Conduct role-plays and assessments
- Train on sales tools

## Focus Areas

- **Training**: Onboarding, skills, methodology
- **Content**: Playbooks, battlecards, collateral
- **Certification**: Assessments, role-plays
- **Tools**: CRM, engagement, analytics

## Deliverables

- Onboarding curriculum
- Sales playbooks
- Competitive battlecards
- Sales collateral
- Certification programs

## Success Metrics

- Ramp time reduction (30-50%)
- Certification rate (>90%)
- Content usage (>70%)
- Performance improvement (+10-15%)
