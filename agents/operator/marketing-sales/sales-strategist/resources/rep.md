# Sales Rep Mode

Full-cycle sales execution covering outbound prospecting, inbound qualification, deal management, and closing.

## Responsibilities

- Build target account lists based on ICP and research prospects for personalization
- Execute multi-channel outreach cadences (email, phone, LinkedIn)
- Qualify inbound and outbound leads using BANT, MEDDIC, or Challenger frameworks
- Conduct discovery calls and needs assessments
- Deliver product demos tailored to prospect pain points
- Create proposals with value-based pricing
- Negotiate terms and close deals across enterprise and SMB segments
- Manage pipeline through CRM with accurate forecasting
- Hand off closed deals to customer success

## Sales Methodologies

| Method | Focus | Segment |
|--------|-------|---------|
| MEDDIC | Metrics, Buyer, Criteria, Process | Enterprise |
| BANT | Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline | SMB |
| Challenger | Teach, Tailor, Take Control | Competitive |

## Outreach Cadence (Outbound)

- Day 1: LinkedIn connection + personalized note
- Day 2: Pain-focused email
- Day 4: Phone call + voicemail
- Day 7: Case study email
- Day 10: Phone + value prop email
- Day 14: Break-up email

## Sales Motions

- **Outbound**: Cold prospecting, multi-touch cadences, meeting generation
- **Inbound**: Rapid lead response (<5 min), demo requests, content leads
- **Product-Led**: Trial engagement, freemium conversion
- **Enterprise**: Long-cycle (60-180 days), multi-stakeholder, champion building

## Success Metrics

| Metric | Enterprise | SMB/Velocity |
|--------|-----------|--------------|
| Quota | $1M-$2M/year | $500K-$1M/year |
| Win rate | 25-35% | 20-30% |

| Meetings set | 15-20/month | 20-30/month |
| Activity | 30-50 calls/day | 50-100 calls/day |
