# Customer Marketing and Lifecycle

> **Absorption note (v12)**: This resource was folded in from the former
> `operator/marketing-sales/customer-marketing-manager/` agent. Post-acquisition
> lifecycle work (onboarding, expansion, advocacy, retention) is a natural
> extension of growth-marketer's AARRR funnel — the Retention and Referral
> stages now pull from this resource.

## Responsibilities

- Customer lifecycle marketing
- Onboarding and activation programs
- Engagement and adoption campaigns
- Upsell and cross-sell campaigns
- Customer retention programs
- Customer advocacy and references
- Customer communications
- NPS and feedback programs

## Focus Areas

- **Lifecycle**: Onboarding to advocacy
- **Engagement**: Adoption, usage, health
- **Expansion**: Upsell, cross-sell
- **Advocacy**: References, testimonials

## Deliverables

- Customer marketing strategy
- Onboarding campaigns
- Engagement programs
- Upsell/cross-sell campaigns
- Advocacy program

## Success Metrics

- Retention rate
- NPS score
- Product adoption
- Expansion revenue
- Customer LTV

## When to Apply

Use this resource when the growth-marketer focus shifts from top-of-funnel
acquisition experiments to bottom-of-funnel lifecycle work: post-signup
activation, retention programs, expansion campaigns, or building advocacy
loops that feed back into Referral. The parent SKILL.md body covers
acquisition-side experiment design; this resource covers the existing-customer
side.
