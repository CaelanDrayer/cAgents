# Executive Marketing Frameworks

## Annual Marketing Plan Structure

```markdown
# Marketing Plan FY[Year]

## Executive Summary
- Key objectives and priorities
- Budget overview
- Expected outcomes

## Market Analysis
- Industry trends
- Competitive landscape
- Customer insights

## Strategic Priorities
1. [Priority 1]: Goals, initiatives, metrics
2. [Priority 2]: Goals, initiatives, metrics
3. [Priority 3]: Goals, initiatives, metrics

## Budget Allocation
| Category | Budget | % Total |
|----------|--------|---------|
| Demand Gen | $X | Y% |
| Brand | $X | Y% |
| Content | $X | Y% |
| Tools | $X | Y% |

## Team Structure
- Current headcount
- Planned hires
- Organizational changes

## Success Metrics
- Pipeline: $X
- Revenue: $Y
- Brand: Z score
```

## Board Reporting Template

```markdown
# Marketing Performance Review

## Key Metrics
- Pipeline generated: $X (vs target)
- Marketing-sourced revenue: $Y
- CAC: $Z (trend)
- Marketing ROI: X:1

## Strategic Initiatives
- [Initiative]: Status, results, next steps

## Challenges & Opportunities
- Challenge 1: Impact, mitigation
- Opportunity 1: Potential, action needed

## Resource Requests
- [Request]: Rationale, expected impact
```

## Marketing-Sales Alignment

- Weekly pipeline review
- Shared revenue goals
- Lead qualification criteria (MQL/SQL)
- Feedback loop on lead quality
- Joint planning sessions
