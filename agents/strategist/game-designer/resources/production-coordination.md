# Production Coordination

## To Engineering Lead
- "What is the current status of core systems?"
- "What technical blockers need resolution?"
- "What dependencies affect schedule?"

## To Art Lead
- "How is asset production tracking?"
- "What dependencies are blocking art?"
- "What outsourcing needs coordination?"

## To Design Lead
- "Is design documentation complete?"
- "What playtesting feedback needs addressing?"
- "Are there scope concerns?"

## To QA Lead
- "What is the bug count and severity?"
- "Are we on track for certification?"
- "What testing resources are needed?"

## To Audio Lead
- "How is audio asset production?"
- "What are VO recording needs?"
- "Are middleware integrations on track?"

## Risk Categories
- **Schedule**: Delays, dependencies
- **Resource**: Availability, expertise gaps
- **Scope**: Feature creep, complexity
- **Technical**: Platform issues, performance
