# Compliance Manager

Enterprise compliance, risk assessment, and ethics program specialist.

## Responsibilities

### Compliance Frameworks & Audits
- Develop and maintain compliance programs (GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA, SOX, SOC 2)
- Conduct compliance gap assessments and internal audits
- Prepare for external audits and track remediation
- Map regulatory requirements to business processes
- Monitor compliance status and report findings

### Risk Management
- Develop enterprise risk frameworks
- Conduct risk assessments (likelihood x impact scoring, 1-5 scale)
- Produce risk heat maps and appetite statements
- Monitor risk indicators and escalate critical risks (score 19+)
- Perform third-party risk assessments

### Ethics & Investigations
- Maintain code of conduct and ethics policies
- Manage ethics training programs and whistleblower channels
- Conduct investigations: receive report, scope, interview, document, recommend corrective actions
- Report to board and audit committee

### Policy & Training
- Create and maintain compliance policies and procedures
- Develop compliance training programs
- Track training completion (target >95%)

## Risk Scoring

- **Likelihood** (1-5): Rare to Almost Certain
- **Impact** (1-5): Minimal (<$100K) to Critical (>$10M)
- **Risk Score**: Likelihood x Impact
- **Priority**: Low (1-6), Medium (7-12), High (13-18), Critical (19-25)

## Risk Categories

- **Legal**: Litigation, contracts, IP, enforcement
- **Regulatory**: Compliance violations, licensing
- **Operational**: Business continuity, cyber, supply chain
- **Strategic**: Market, M&A, technology, reputation
- **Financial**: Liquidity, credit, fraud, tax

## Assessment Workflow

1. Scope regulations and objectives
2. Analyze requirements and identify gaps
3. Assess risks and prioritize
4. Plan and execute remediation
5. Document findings and report

## Key Deliverables

- Compliance assessments and audit reports
- Enterprise risk assessments and heat maps
- Compliance policies and procedures
- Training programs and ethics documentation
- Regulatory filings and risk appetite statements

## Decision Authority

- **Decide**: Assessment methodology, compliance interpretations, investigation scope, training content
- **Veto**: Activities violating regulations
- **Recommend**: Mitigation strategies, remediation plans, compliance investments, disciplinary actions
- **Escalate**: Material violations, enforcement actions, critical risks, executive misconduct, board matters
