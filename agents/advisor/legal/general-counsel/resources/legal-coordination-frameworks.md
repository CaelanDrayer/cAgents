# Legal Coordination Frameworks

## Matter Management

### Matter Types
- Corporate/Governance
- Employment
- Intellectual Property
- Commercial/Contracts
- Litigation
- Regulatory
- M&A/Transactions

### Matter Intake Process
1. Receive request from stakeholder
2. Classify matter type and complexity
3. Assign to appropriate attorney
4. Set timeline and milestones
5. Track to completion

### Priority Matrix

| Priority | Criteria | Response |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Critical | Board, regulatory, material | Same day |
| High | Executive, significant risk | 24-48 hours |
| Standard | Business operations | 5 business days |
| Low | Planning, advisory | 2 weeks |

## Delegation Patterns

### Corporate Counsel
**Delegates**:
- Entity formation and maintenance
- M&A transactions
- Securities compliance
- Board and governance matters

**Questions Asked**:
- What is the transaction structure?
- What are the securities implications?
- What governance approvals are needed?

### Employment Attorney
**Delegates**:
- Workplace investigations
- Employment disputes
- Policy development
- Regulatory compliance

**Questions Asked**:
- What is the legal exposure?
- What are the applicable laws?
- What remediation is needed?

### IP Attorney
**Delegates**:
- Patent prosecution
- Trademark management
- IP licensing
- Trade secret protection

**Questions Asked**:
- What IP assets are involved?
- What is the protection strategy?
- What are the licensing terms?

### Litigation Manager
**Delegates**:
- Active lawsuits
- Pre-litigation disputes
- Alternative dispute resolution
- Claims management

**Questions Asked**:
- What is the case status and strategy?
- What is the liability exposure?
- What are the settlement options?

## Outside Counsel Management

### Selection Criteria
- Subject matter expertise
- Industry experience
- Cost-effectiveness
- Diversity
- Relationship history

### Budget Guidelines

| Matter Type | Expected Range |
|-------------|----------------|
| Routine contract review | $2K-10K |
| Employment matter | $10K-50K |
| IP prosecution | $15K-30K |
| Litigation defense | $50K-500K+ |
| M&A transaction | $100K-1M+ |

### Performance Metrics
- Budget adherence
- Matter outcomes
- Responsiveness
- Invoice accuracy
- Value delivered

## Executive/Board Reporting

### Monthly Legal Report
- Active matters summary
- Budget vs. actual
- Key risks and issues
- Outside counsel spend
- Team updates

### Board Legal Report (Quarterly)
- Material litigation
- Regulatory matters
- Significant contracts
- Governance items
- Risk assessment
