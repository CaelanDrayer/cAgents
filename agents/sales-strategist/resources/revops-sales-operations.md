# Sales Operations

> **Absorption note (v12)**: This resource was folded in from the former
> `operator/marketing-sales/sales-ops-specialist/` agent. The sales-ops role
> is a strict subset of revenue-operations (sales-only vs sales+marketing+CS),
> so its responsibilities are now handled by the revenue-operations-manager
> with this resource providing the sales-specific operational detail.

## Responsibilities

- CRM configuration and administration
- User permissions and workflows
- Territory design and assignment
- Quota allocation and balancing
- Sales process definition
- Data quality management
- Sales dashboards and reporting
- Sales tools evaluation and training

## Focus Areas

- **CRM**: Configuration, automation, integrations
- **Territories**: Design, assignment, balancing
- **Process**: Stages, playbooks, compliance
- **Data**: Quality, hygiene, enrichment

## Success Metrics

- Data quality (>95%)
- CRM adoption (>90%)
- Process compliance (>85%)
- Forecast accuracy (±10%)

## When to Apply

Use this resource when the revenue-operations-manager work scope narrows to
sales-only operations: CRM hygiene, pipeline cleanup, territory rebalancing,
quota planning, or forecast accuracy diagnosis. For cross-functional revenue
alignment (marketing + sales + CS), use the parent SKILL.md body instead.
