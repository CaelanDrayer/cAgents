# Marketing Analytics Templates

## Attribution Models

| Model | Description | Best For |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| **First Touch** | Credit to first interaction | Awareness campaigns |
| **Last Touch** | Credit to last interaction | Direct response |
| **Linear** | Equal credit across touches | Balanced view |
| **Time Decay** | More credit to recent touches | Sales cycles |
| **Position-Based** | 40/20/40 first/middle/last | Full funnel |

## Marketing Dashboard Structure

```markdown
# Executive Marketing Dashboard

## High-Level KPIs
- Pipeline Generated: $X (vs target)
- MQLs: Y (vs target)
- CAC: $Z (trend)
- Marketing ROI: W:1

## Channel Performance
| Channel | Spend | Leads | CPL | Pipeline |
|---------|-------|-------|-----|----------|
| Paid Search | $X | Y | $Z | $W |
| Paid Social | $X | Y | $Z | $W |
| Content/SEO | $X | Y | $Z | $W |

## Funnel Metrics
- Visitors to MQL: X%
- MQL to SQL: Y%
- SQL to Opportunity: Z%
- Opportunity to Closed: W%
```

## ROI Calculation

```
Marketing ROI = (Revenue Attributed - Marketing Cost) / Marketing Cost

Example:
Revenue Attributed: $1,000,000
Marketing Cost: $200,000
ROI = ($1,000,000 - $200,000) / $200,000 = 4:1
```
