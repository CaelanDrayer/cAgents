# Change Management

Reference for planning and executing organizational changes.

## Change Management Framework

### ADKAR Model

| Phase | Focus | Key Activities |
|-------|-------|---------------|
| **A**wareness | Why change is needed | Communicate business case, share data |
| **D**esire | Personal motivation to change | Address WIIFM, involve champions |
| **K**nowledge | How to change | Training, documentation, coaching |
| **A**bility | Demonstrated capability | Practice, support, remove barriers |
| **R**einforcement | Sustain the change | Celebrate wins, measure adoption |

### Change Impact Assessment

**Impact Dimensions**:
| Dimension | Low Impact | Medium Impact | High Impact |
|-----------|-----------|---------------|-------------|
| Process | Minor workflow tweak | New steps or tools | Fundamentally new process |
| People | Same roles, slight adjustment | Role expansion or reduction | New roles, restructuring |
| Technology | Config change | New tool adoption | Platform migration |
| Culture | Reinforces existing norms | Shifts some behaviors | Transforms core values |

**Stakeholder Impact Matrix**:
```
             Low Influence  |  High Influence
High Impact  Keep Satisfied  |  Manage Closely
             (Regular updates)|  (Active partnership)

Low Impact   Monitor         |  Keep Informed
             (Periodic check) |  (Regular communication)
```

## Communication Plan

### Communication Cadence

| Audience | Channel | Frequency | Owner |
|----------|---------|-----------|-------|
| All employees | Town hall | Launch + monthly | CEO/CHRO |
| Managers | Manager briefing | Weekly during rollout | HR |
| Affected teams | Team meetings | Weekly | Direct manager |
| Leadership | Steering committee | Bi-weekly | Change lead |
| External (if applicable) | Press/customer comms | As needed | Comms |

### Message Framework

**Before Change** (Awareness):
- Why: Business rationale with data
- What: Clear description of what will change
- When: Timeline and key milestones
- Who: Who is affected and how

**During Change** (Knowledge + Ability):
- How: Step-by-step guidance and training
- Support: Where to get help, FAQs
- Progress: What has been accomplished
- Feedback: How to share concerns

**After Change** (Reinforcement):
- Wins: Celebrate early successes
- Metrics: Show measurable improvements
- Recognition: Acknowledge change champions
- Next: What comes next in the journey

## Resistance Management

### Common Resistance Patterns

| Type | Signs | Response |
|------|-------|----------|
| Fear of unknown | Questions, anxiety, avoidance | Provide clarity, allow Q&A |
| Loss of control | Pushback, negativity | Involve in design, give choices |
| Competence concerns | "I can't do this" | Training, coaching, patience |
| Change fatigue | Disengagement, "another initiative" | Simplify, acknowledge fatigue |
| Philosophical disagreement | "This is wrong direction" | Listen, address concerns, data |

### Resistance Response Protocol

1. **Listen**: Acknowledge the concern without judgment
2. **Empathize**: Validate the emotion behind the resistance
3. **Clarify**: Provide missing information or correct misunderstandings
4. **Involve**: Offer a role in shaping the change
5. **Support**: Provide resources to build capability
6. **Follow up**: Check back on progress and remaining concerns

## Change Readiness Assessment

### Readiness Survey Questions

Rate 1-5 (Strongly Disagree to Strongly Agree):
1. I understand why this change is necessary
2. I believe this change will benefit the organization
3. I have the skills needed to work in the new way
4. My manager supports this change
5. I have adequate resources and support
6. I am confident the organization can execute this successfully

### Readiness Score Interpretation

| Score | Readiness | Action |
|-------|-----------|--------|
| 4.0-5.0 | High | Proceed, leverage as champions |
| 3.0-3.9 | Moderate | Address gaps, increase communication |
| 2.0-2.9 | Low | Pause, address concerns, rebuild trust |
| 1.0-1.9 | Critical | Fundamental rethink of approach needed |

## Reorganization Playbook

### Timeline Template

| Week | Activity | Owner |
|------|----------|-------|
| T-6 | Design new structure, validate with leadership | HRBP + CEO |
| T-4 | Manager notification and alignment | HRBP |
| T-3 | Legal review (if applicable, RIF considerations) | Legal + HR |
| T-2 | Prepare communication materials | HRBP + Comms |
| T-1 | Manager coaching sessions | HRBP |
| T-0 | Announce to all affected employees | Leadership |
| T+1 | Individual conversations, FAQ sessions | Managers |
| T+2 | Begin transition activities | All |
| T+4 | Check-in survey | HRBP |
| T+8 | Effectiveness review | HRBP + Leadership |

### Checklist

- [ ] Business case documented and approved
- [ ] Legal review completed (if headcount changes)
- [ ] New org chart finalized
- [ ] Individual impact assessments done
- [ ] Manager talking points prepared
- [ ] FAQ document created
- [ ] Communication timeline set
- [ ] Support resources identified (EAP, career services)
- [ ] Transition plan for each affected individual
- [ ] Success metrics defined
