# HR Operations Framework

Comprehensive framework for HR process management, compliance, and service delivery.

## HR Service Delivery Model

### Service Tiers

Organize HR services into tiers for efficient delivery:

**Tier 0 - Self-Service**
- Employee handbook and policy lookup
- Benefits enrollment and changes
- PTO balance and requests
- Payroll information and pay stubs
- Address and personal info updates

**Tier 1 - HR Generalist Support**
- Policy interpretation questions
- Benefits eligibility inquiries
- Leave of absence coordination
- Employment verification requests
- Basic onboarding/offboarding tasks

**Tier 2 - HR Specialist Escalation**
- Complex employee relations issues
- Accommodation requests (ADA, religious, etc.)
- Compensation exception requests
- Cross-functional workforce planning
- Performance improvement plan guidance

**Tier 3 - HR Leadership Decision**
- Termination approvals
- Organizational restructuring
- Policy creation or major revisions
- Legal escalations
- Executive compensation decisions

### Service Level Agreements

| Request Type | Response Time | Resolution Time |
|-------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Urgent (safety, harassment) | 2 hours | 24 hours initial action |
| High (termination, legal) | 4 hours | 3 business days |
| Standard (policy, benefits) | 1 business day | 5 business days |
| Low (info requests, updates) | 2 business days | 10 business days |

## Core HR Processes

### Onboarding Process

**Pre-Start (2 weeks before)**

| Task | Owner | Timeline |
|------|-------|----------|
| Send offer letter and collect signed docs | Recruiter | Day of offer |
| Initiate background check | HR Specialist | Within 24 hours of acceptance |
| Create employee record in HRIS | HR Ops | 5 days before start |
| Provision equipment and accounts | IT | 3 days before start |
| Send welcome packet and first-day details | HR Manager | 3 days before start |
| Assign onboarding buddy | Hiring Manager | 2 days before start |

**First Day**

| Task | Owner | Timeline |
|------|-------|----------|
| Welcome and orientation session | HR | 9:00 - 10:00 AM |
| Complete I-9 verification | HR | Before end of day 1 |
| Benefits enrollment overview | Benefits team | 10:00 - 11:00 AM |
| Team introduction and workspace setup | Hiring Manager | 11:00 AM - 12:00 PM |
| Systems access verification | IT | 1:00 - 2:00 PM |
| Role expectations and 30/60/90 plan | Hiring Manager | 2:00 - 3:00 PM |

**First 90 Days**

| Milestone | Check-in Type | Owner |
|-----------|--------------|-------|
| Day 7 | Informal check-in | Onboarding buddy |
| Day 14 | HR check-in (settling in) | HR Manager |
| Day 30 | First formal review | Hiring Manager + HR |
| Day 60 | Progress review | Hiring Manager |
| Day 90 | Probation review and confirmation | Hiring Manager + HR |

### Offboarding Process

**Voluntary Resignation**

| Task | Owner | Timeline |
|------|-------|----------|
| Receive and acknowledge resignation | Manager | Day of notice |
| Notify HR and initiate offboarding | Manager | Same day |
| Schedule exit interview | HR Manager | Within 3 days |
| Knowledge transfer plan | Manager | Within first week |
| Revoke system access (schedule) | IT | Last day |
| Final paycheck and benefits info | Payroll/HR | Last day |
| Conduct exit interview | HR Manager | Last week |
| Collect company property | Manager/IT | Last day |
| Process final documentation | HR Ops | Within 5 days of departure |

**Involuntary Termination**

| Task | Owner | Timeline |
|------|-------|----------|
| Review termination decision with HR | Manager + HR | Before notification |
| Legal review (if needed) | Legal/HR | Before notification |
| Prepare separation agreement | HR/Legal | Before notification |
| Conduct termination meeting | Manager + HR | Scheduled meeting |
| Immediately revoke system access | IT | During meeting |
| Provide final pay and COBRA info | HR/Payroll | At meeting or within 24 hours |
| Collect company property | HR/Manager | At meeting |
| Team communication plan | Manager + HR | Within 24 hours |

## Policy Development Framework

### Policy Lifecycle

```
Identify Need -> Draft Policy -> Legal Review -> Stakeholder Review ->
Approval -> Communication -> Training -> Implementation -> Monitor -> Review
```

### Policy Document Template

Every HR policy must contain:

1. **Purpose**: Why this policy exists
2. **Scope**: Who it applies to (all employees, specific groups, locations)
3. **Definitions**: Key terms defined clearly
4. **Policy Statement**: The actual rules and guidelines
5. **Procedures**: Step-by-step process for compliance
6. **Responsibilities**: Who is responsible for what
7. **Exceptions**: How to request an exception and who approves
8. **Related Policies**: Cross-references to related policies
9. **Revision History**: Date, version, author, changes made
10. **Effective Date**: When the policy takes effect

### Policy Review Schedule

| Policy Category | Review Frequency | Trigger Events |
|----------------|-----------------|----------------|
| Compliance/Legal | Annually | Law changes, audit findings |
| Safety/Security | Annually | Incidents, regulation changes |
| Benefits/Compensation | Annually | Market data updates, open enrollment |
| Code of Conduct | Every 2 years | Ethics complaints, culture shifts |
| Remote/Hybrid Work | Annually | Workforce changes, technology updates |
| Leave Policies | Annually | State/federal law changes |

## Compliance Calendar

### Monthly Tasks

- [ ] Review new hire I-9 completion (3-day deadline)
- [ ] Audit PTO accruals and balances
- [ ] Review open employee relations cases
- [ ] Update headcount and org chart

### Quarterly Tasks

- [ ] EEO data review and reporting
- [ ] Benefits utilization review
- [ ] Training completion audit
- [ ] Manager compliance check-in
- [ ] Workers compensation review

### Annual Tasks

- [ ] Affirmative Action Plan update (if applicable)
- [ ] OSHA 300 log posting (February)
- [ ] EEO-1 report filing
- [ ] Benefits plan renewal and open enrollment
- [ ] Compensation market analysis
- [ ] Employee handbook review and update
- [ ] Required training deployment (harassment, safety, etc.)
- [ ] Policy audit and updates
- [ ] Employment law update review

## HR Metrics Dashboard

### Key Metrics to Track

| Metric | Formula | Target | Frequency |
|--------|---------|--------|-----------|
| Turnover Rate | Departures / Avg Headcount | < 15% annual | Monthly |
| Time to Fill | Days from req open to offer accept | < 45 days | Per role |
| Offer Acceptance Rate | Offers accepted / Offers made | > 85% | Quarterly |
| Onboarding Satisfaction | Survey score (1-5) | > 4.0 | Per cohort |
| HR Case Resolution Time | Avg days to close | < 10 days | Monthly |
| Training Completion | Completed / Assigned | > 95% | Quarterly |
| Benefits Enrollment | Enrolled / Eligible | > 90% | Annual |
| Employee Satisfaction (eNPS) | Promoters - Detractors | > 30 | Quarterly |

### Reporting Cadence

- **Weekly**: Open cases, new hires/departures, urgent items
- **Monthly**: Headcount, turnover, time-to-fill, case metrics
- **Quarterly**: Full HR dashboard, trends, strategic recommendations
- **Annually**: Workforce planning report, compensation analysis, compliance audit
