# IP Management Frameworks

## Patent Prosecution Timeline

### Provisional Application
- **Purpose**: Establish priority date
- **Duration**: 12 months
- **Cost**: $1,500-3,000

### Non-Provisional Application
- **Filing**: Within 12 months of provisional
- **Examination**: 12-24 months
- **Cost**: $10,000-20,000

### Prosecution
- Office actions (2-4 typical)
- Amendments and arguments
- Allowance and issue
- **Timeline**: 2-4 years total

### Maintenance
- 3.5 year fee
- 7.5 year fee
- 11.5 year fee
- **Total life**: 20 years from filing

## Trademark Management

### Registration Process
1. Clearance search
2. File application (ITU or use-based)
3. Examination (3-6 months)
4. Publication (30 days)
5. Registration/Statement of Use
6. **Timeline**: 8-14 months

### Maintenance Schedule
| Filing | Deadline | Action |
|--------|----------|--------|
| Section 8 | Year 5-6 | Declaration of use |
| Section 9 | Year 9-10 | Renewal |
| Combined 8 & 9 | Every 10 years | Renewal + use |

### Monitoring Program
- Watch services for similar marks
- Domain monitoring
- Marketplace monitoring
- Social media monitoring

## Portfolio Management

### IP Inventory Categories
- Patents (pending, issued, maintained)
- Trademarks (pending, registered, common law)
- Copyrights (registered, unregistered)
- Trade secrets (documented, protected)
- Domain names

### Valuation Factors
- Revenue attributed to IP
- Competitive advantage
- Licensing potential
- Enforcement history
- Market position

### Portfolio Review (Annual)
1. Inventory current assets
2. Assess strategic value
3. Identify gaps
4. Prioritize filings
5. Budget planning
6. Abandonment decisions

## Licensing Framework

### License Types
| Type | Rights Granted | Exclusivity |
|------|---------------|-------------|
| Exclusive | All rights | Yes |
| Sole | Rights to one party | Licensor retains |
| Non-exclusive | Limited rights | No |
| Cross-license | Mutual exchange | Varies |

### Key License Terms
- Grant clause (scope, field, territory)
- Consideration (royalty, lump sum)
- Term and termination
- Representations and warranties
- Indemnification
- Audit rights

### Royalty Structures
- Running royalty (% of revenue)
- Per-unit royalty
- Minimum guarantees
- Milestone payments
- Hybrid models

## Enforcement Protocol

### Infringement Response
1. Detect potential infringement
2. Gather evidence
3. Legal analysis
4. Business assessment
5. Enforcement decision
6. Execute strategy

### Enforcement Options
| Option | When to Use | Cost |
|--------|-------------|------|
| Cease and desist | First notice, clear infringement | Low |
| UDRP (domains) | Cybersquatting | $1,500-5,000 |
| ITC complaint | Import infringement | High |
| Federal litigation | Significant harm | Very high |
