# Risk and Compliance Frameworks

## Enterprise Risk Assessment Template

```markdown
# Enterprise Risk Assessment

## Executive Summary
- Top risks (rank, category, score, trend)
- Total risk exposure
- Key recommendations

## Risk Analysis
For each top risk:
- Description
- Likelihood (1-5) + rationale
- Impact (financial, operational, reputational)
- Current controls and gaps
- Mitigation plan (phases, costs, timeline)

## Risk Heat Map
[Likelihood vs Impact matrix]

## Risk Appetite Statement
- Appetite by category
- Mitigation thresholds
- Escalation criteria

## Monitoring and Reporting
- Key risk indicators
- Reporting cadence
```

## Risk Scoring Methodology

### Likelihood Scale
| Score | Rating | Probability |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Rare | <10% |
| 2 | Unlikely | 10-25% |
| 3 | Possible | 25-50% |
| 4 | Likely | 50-75% |
| 5 | Almost Certain | >75% |

### Impact Scale
| Score | Rating | Financial Impact |
|-------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Minimal | <$100K |
| 2 | Minor | $100K-$500K |
| 3 | Moderate | $500K-$2M |
| 4 | Major | $2M-$10M |
| 5 | Critical | >$10M |

### Priority Matrix
- **Low (1-6)**: Monitor, no immediate action
- **Medium (7-12)**: Implement controls
- **High (13-18)**: Priority mitigation
- **Critical (19-25)**: Immediate action, board escalation

## Compliance Program Structure

### Governance
- Board oversight (Audit Committee)
- Executive leadership (CCO, Steering Committee)
- Operational execution (Compliance Champions)

### Components
1. Regulatory inventory (quarterly update)
2. Policies and procedures (annual review)
3. Controls (preventive, detective, corrective)
4. Training and awareness
5. Monitoring and testing
6. Incident management
7. Metrics and reporting

## Risk Appetite Template

**Legal and Regulatory**:
- Low appetite for violations
- Threshold: Score ≥12 requires mitigation
- Escalation: Score ≥16 to GC and Board

**Operational**:
- Moderate if mitigated
- Threshold: Score ≥13
- Escalation: Score ≥18 to COO

**Strategic and Financial**:
- Moderate if growth-aligned
- Threshold: Impact >$5M
- Escalation: Impact >$10M to CEO

## Third-Party Risk Assessment

1. **Identification**: Inventory all vendors/partners
2. **Classification**: Risk tier by data access, criticality
3. **Due Diligence**: Questionnaires, audits, certifications
4. **Contracting**: DPAs, security terms, audit rights
5. **Monitoring**: Ongoing performance, incident tracking
