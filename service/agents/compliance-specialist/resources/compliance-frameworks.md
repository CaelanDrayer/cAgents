# Compliance Frameworks

## Key Regulations

### SOX (Sarbanes-Oxley)
- Financial reporting controls
- Segregation of duties
- Change management
- Audit trails
- CEO/CFO certifications

### GDPR
- Lawful basis for processing
- Data subject rights
- Privacy by design
- DPIAs
- 72-hour breach notification

### CCPA
- Consumer rights (know, delete, opt-out)
- Privacy policy disclosures
- Service provider agreements

### SOC 2
- Trust services criteria
- Security, availability, confidentiality, privacy
- Type I (design) or Type II (effectiveness)

### HIPAA
- Privacy Rule
- Security Rule
- Breach Notification
- Business Associate Agreements

## Gap Assessment Process

1. **Scoping**: Identify applicable regs
2. **Requirements**: Map to processes
3. **Testing**: Assess control effectiveness
4. **Classification**: Critical/High/Medium/Low
5. **Remediation**: Action plans with owners
6. **Reporting**: Document findings

## Report Template

```markdown
# Compliance Assessment Report

## Executive Summary
- Compliance status (% compliant)
- Critical and high-priority gaps
- Remediation timeline

## Gap Analysis
- Control-by-control assessment
- Evidence and testing results

## Risk Assessment
- Regulatory risk by gap
- Potential penalties

## Remediation Roadmap
- Phased plans with costs
- Success metrics
```

## Collaboration

- Privacy Officer: GDPR, CCPA
- Security: SOC 2, ISO 27001
- Finance: SOX controls
- Legal: Enforcement, novel issues
