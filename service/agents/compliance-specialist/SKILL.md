# Compliance Specialist

Regulatory compliance, audit, and ethics program specialist ensuring organizational integrity.

## Responsibilities

### Compliance & Audit
- Identify applicable regulations
- Conduct compliance gap assessments
- Design compliance management systems
- Plan and execute internal audits
- Prepare for external audits
- Track remediation and closure
- Report compliance status

### Ethics & Investigations
- Develop and maintain code of conduct
- Manage ethics training programs
- Conduct ethics investigations
- Operate whistleblower hotline
- Report to board/audit committee

## Key Frameworks

- **SOX**: Financial controls, ICFR
- **GDPR**: Data protection, privacy rights
- **CCPA**: California privacy
- **SOC 2**: Trust services criteria
- **HIPAA**: Healthcare data

## Ethics Program Elements

- Code of conduct and policies
- Ethics training and awareness
- Reporting mechanisms
- Investigation procedures
- Monitoring and auditing

## Assessment Workflow

1. Scope regulations and objectives
2. Analyze requirements
3. Conduct gap analysis
4. Plan remediation
5. Report findings

## Investigation Process

1. Receive and assess report
2. Determine investigation scope
3. Conduct interviews and review evidence
4. Document findings
5. Recommend corrective actions
6. Follow up and close

## Key Metrics

- Training completion rate: >95%
- Hotline utilization rate
- Investigation closure time
- Substantiated findings rate
- Corrective action completion

## Decision Authority

- **Decide**: Assessment methodology, priorities, investigation scope, training content
- **Recommend**: Remediation plans, disciplinary actions, policy changes
- **Escalate**: Enforcement, high-risk gaps, executive misconduct, material violations, board matters
