# Best Practices: Paralegal

> Design principles, patterns, and frameworks that guide high-quality legal document preparation, research, and matter support work.

## Design Principles

- **Precision in Document Preparation**: Legal documents must be exact; a wrong party name, incorrect date, or missing exhibit can invalidate a filing or create liability
- **Deadline Awareness is Non-Negotiable**: Missed legal deadlines can abandon rights permanently; maintain a master deadline calendar with reminders at 30, 14, and 7 days for every critical date
- **Scope Discipline**: Paralegals prepare and support; they do not provide legal conclusions or advice; know precisely where that line is and never cross it
- **Organized Case Files Enable Efficient Work**: A well-organized matter file is the foundation of efficient legal work; time spent on organization pays back tenfold in time saved searching during crises
- **Attorney Review Before Filing**: No document with legal consequences should leave the organization without attorney review; paralegals prepare, attorneys review and authorize
- **Research Methodology Rigor**: Legal research findings must cite specific authorities with accurate citations; unreliable research creates misinformed legal advice
- **Confidentiality as Baseline**: Everything touched in the legal department is attorney-client privileged and/or confidential; treat all matter information with absolute discretion

## Key Patterns & Frameworks

- **Matter File Organization Standard**: Consistent file structure for every matter type (correspondence folder, pleadings folder, discovery folder, research folder, contracts folder, billing folder); enables any team member to find any document immediately
- **Deadline Docketing System**: Master calendar of all legal deadlines — court filing dates, statute of limitations, response deadlines, contract renewals, regulatory filing dates; populated at matter inception and reviewed weekly
- **Document Proofreading Protocol**: Multi-pass review — first for content accuracy, second for formatting and citations, third for party names and defined terms, final for completeness; each pass has a specific focus
- **Legal Research Methodology**: Issue identification → jurisdiction determination → secondary sources for overview → primary sources for authorities → update verification (Shepardize/KeyCite) → citation formatting
- **Contract Summary Template**: Standardized format for abstracting key contract terms (parties, effective date, term, renewal, payment, key obligations, termination rights, governing law, dispute resolution) for business stakeholder consumption
- **Deposition Preparation Support Package**: Organize deposition exhibits, prepare exhibit list with descriptions, create witness binder with key documents, coordinate court reporter, prepare deposition notice
- **Corporate Records Maintenance Checklist**: Annual review of board minutes, officer certificates, shareholder records, state registrations, and filing compliance; maintains good standing and corporate validity
- **Discovery Management Workflow**: Hold notice → custodian interviews → collection protocol → chain of custody documentation → processing instructions → privilege log preparation

## Domain Concepts & Terminology

### Document Preparation
- **Pleading**: Court filing initiating or responding to litigation (complaint, answer, counterclaim)
- **Motion**: Written request to the court for a ruling or order
- **Brief**: Written argument submitted to a court supporting a legal position
- **Declaration**: Sworn written statement of facts by a person (similar to affidavit)
- **Exhibit**: Document or item attached to and referenced by a legal filing
- **Service of Process**: Formal delivery of legal documents to required parties
- **Proof of Service**: Document confirming that service was properly completed

### Legal Research
- **Primary Authority**: Binding legal authority — constitutions, statutes, regulations, court decisions
- **Secondary Authority**: Persuasive but non-binding sources — treatises, law review articles, practice guides
- **Case Law**: Judicial decisions that interpret statutes and create precedent
- **Shepardizing/KeyCiting**: Verifying that a cited case or statute is still valid law and hasn't been overruled
- **Legal Memorandum**: Structured written analysis of a legal issue; format: question presented, brief answer, facts, discussion, conclusion
- **Annotated Code**: Published compilation of statutes with editorial notes and case citations

### Corporate Records
- **Minute Book**: Official record of all corporate meetings and actions; required to be maintained and current
- **Board Resolution**: Formal documentation of board-approved decisions and authorizations
- **Written Consent**: Approval of corporate action obtained through signatures rather than a meeting
- **Certificate of Good Standing**: State-issued document confirming an entity is validly registered and in compliance
- **Annual Report**: Required state filing confirming entity status and officers; failure creates lapse of registration

### Litigation Support
- **Discovery**: Exchange of evidence between parties in litigation
- **Privilege Log**: Document listing items withheld from discovery due to attorney-client or work product privilege
- **Deposition**: Pre-trial sworn testimony of a witness
- **Legal Hold**: Suspension of document destruction when litigation is anticipated
- **Chain of Custody**: Documentation of who handled evidence and when; required to authenticate documents in court

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- **Providing Legal Conclusions**: Answering substantive legal questions or advising on the legal meaning of documents without attorney involvement; this is unauthorized practice of law
- **Casual Deadline Management**: Tracking court and contract deadlines informally without a docketing system; the volume and consequence of legal deadlines requires systematic tracking
- **Filing Without Proofreading**: Submitting legal documents without thorough review for accuracy, completeness, and formatting; errors in filed documents are public record and can be embarrassing or legally damaging
- **Disorganized Matter Files**: Allowing case files to accumulate documents without consistent organization; disorganized files waste attorney time and create risk of missing documents during litigation
- **Unsupervised Research Distribution**: Sharing legal research directly with business stakeholders without attorney review; paralegal research informs attorney advice, not the other way around
- **Citation Without Verification**: Including case citations without verifying current validity; overruled cases undermine the legal arguments they're supposed to support
- **Privilege Carelessness**: Forwarding privileged documents to parties outside the privilege relationship without attorney guidance; waives privilege and can expose the client's legal strategy

## Quality Indicators

- **Deadline Compliance**: Zero missed legal deadlines with docketing system fully populated
- **Document Accuracy Rate**: All filed documents with correct party names, dates, and exhibits confirmed through multi-pass review
- **Research Citation Validity**: All case citations verified as current and good law before being included in submissions
- **Matter File Organization**: All matter files organized to standard template with documents filed consistently
- **Turnaround Time**: Documents delivered to attorneys within committed timelines
- **Corporate Records Currency**: All minute books, resolutions, and state filings current
- **Privilege Log Completeness**: All withheld documents properly logged with privilege assertion and description

## Collaboration Touchpoints

- **With Litigation Manager**: Provide discovery support, deposition preparation, exhibit organization, and deadline management; the litigation manager sets strategy while the paralegal executes the supporting work
- **With Legal Operations Manager**: Receive workflow standards, templates, and technology guidance; report on matter support capacity and process improvement opportunities
- **With Contracts Manager**: Support contract review process with proofreading, execution coordination, and filing in CLM system; paralegal handles administrative steps freeing contracts manager for legal analysis
- **With Corporate Counsel**: Maintain corporate records, coordinate board meeting logistics, support entity compliance filings; corporate paralegal work is high-volume and requires precision
