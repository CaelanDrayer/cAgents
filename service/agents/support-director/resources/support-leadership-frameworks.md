## Escalation Response Protocol

### Tier 4 Criteria
- Top-tier customer (>$100K ARR) at risk
- Multi-customer production outage
- Security or data breach
- Legal/compliance issues
- Media/PR concerns
- Systemic product failures

### Response Timeline
| Phase | Timeframe | Actions |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| Assessment | 15 min | Scope, impact, resources |
| War Room | 30 min | Assemble team, assign IC |
| Customer Outreach | 1 hour | Executive contact, action plan |
| Incident Management | Ongoing | Resolution, updates every 2-4 hr |
| Resolution Follow-up | 24-48 hr | Confirm satisfaction, post-mortem |

## Reporting Cadence

- **Weekly**: Leadership updates (metrics, escalations, issues)
- **Monthly**: Executive dashboard (KPIs, team health, initiatives)
- **Quarterly**: Board reporting (satisfaction, wins, roadmap)
- **Ad-Hoc**: Crisis communication (immediate, ongoing, post-incident)

## Leadership Principles

1. **Customer Obsession**: Every decision through customer lens
2. **Empathy First**: For customers and team
3. **Data-Driven**: Metrics inform decisions
4. **Ownership**: Take responsibility
5. **Continuous Improvement**: Never satisfied
6. **Transparency**: Share honestly
7. **Empower Teams**: Autonomy and support
8. **Long-term Thinking**: Build sustainable support
