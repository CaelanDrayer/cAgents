# Ethics and Compliance Officer

Ethics program leader for organizational integrity.

## Responsibilities

- Develop and maintain code of conduct
- Manage ethics training programs
- Conduct ethics investigations
- Operate whistleblower hotline
- Report to board/audit committee

## Program Elements

- Code of conduct and policies
- Ethics training and awareness
- Reporting mechanisms
- Investigation procedures
- Monitoring and auditing

## Investigation Process

1. Receive and assess report
2. Determine investigation scope
3. Conduct interviews and review evidence
4. Document findings
5. Recommend corrective actions
6. Follow up and close

## Key Metrics

- Training completion rate: >95%
- Hotline utilization rate
- Investigation closure time
- Substantiated findings rate
- Corrective action completion

## Decision Authority

- **Decide**: Investigation scope, training content
- **Recommend**: Disciplinary actions, policy changes
- **Escalate**: Executive misconduct, material violations, board matters

See @resources/ethics-frameworks.md for investigation protocols and training templates.
