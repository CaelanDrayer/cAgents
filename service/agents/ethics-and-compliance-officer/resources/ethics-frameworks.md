# Ethics Program Frameworks

## Investigation Protocol

### Phase 1: Report Intake
- Log report in case management system
- Assess initial credibility
- Determine investigation scope
- Identify conflicts of interest
- Assign investigator

### Phase 2: Investigation Planning
- Define specific allegations
- Identify witnesses and documents
- Establish timeline
- Plan interview sequence
- Prepare interview guides

### Phase 3: Evidence Collection
- Conduct witness interviews
- Collect documents and records
- Preserve electronic evidence
- Maintain chain of custody
- Document all activities

### Phase 4: Analysis
- Evaluate witness credibility
- Corroborate or refute allegations
- Apply policy standards
- Assess severity
- Develop findings

### Phase 5: Conclusion
- Draft investigation report
- Recommend corrective actions
- Present to appropriate authority
- Track remediation
- Close case

## Training Program Structure

### Annual Ethics Training
**Duration**: 60 minutes
**Topics**:
- Code of conduct overview
- Key policy areas
- Reporting mechanisms
- Case studies/scenarios
- Certification

### Role-Based Training

| Role | Additional Training |
|------|---------------------|
| Managers | Leadership ethics, investigation response |
| Finance | Anti-bribery, financial integrity |
| Sales | Gift policies, competitive practices |
| Procurement | Vendor ethics, conflict of interest |

### New Hire Training
**Timing**: Within 30 days of hire
**Topics**:
- Code of conduct
- Reporting hotline
- Key policies
- Acknowledgment

## Hotline Management

### Report Categories
- Harassment/discrimination
- Financial irregularities
- Conflicts of interest
- Safety concerns
- Policy violations
- Retaliation

### Triage Matrix

| Urgency | Response Time | Examples |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| Critical | 24 hours | Safety, ongoing harm |
| High | 3 days | Executive, financial |
| Standard | 7 days | Policy violations |
| Low | 14 days | Questions, minor |

## Metrics Dashboard

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Training completion | >95% | Quarterly |
| Hotline awareness | >80% | Annual survey |
| Investigation cycle time | <60 days | Monthly |
| Corrective action completion | 100% | Monthly |
| Substantiation rate | Track trend | Quarterly |

## Board Reporting Template

1. **Program Status**: Training, awareness, resources
2. **Hotline Activity**: Volume, categories, trends
3. **Investigation Summary**: Open, closed, findings
4. **Key Issues**: Material matters, emerging risks
5. **Benchmarking**: Industry comparisons
6. **Recommendations**: Program enhancements
