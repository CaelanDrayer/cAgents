# Community Management Frameworks

## Community Code of Conduct

**Be Respectful**: Courtesy, no attacks, assume good intent
**Be Helpful**: Share knowledge, constructive feedback
**Stay On Topic**: Relevant, appropriate categories
**Protect Privacy**: No sharing personal info

**Consequences**: Warning -> Suspension -> Ban

## Engagement Strategies

### Weekly Prompts
- Monday: Share your biggest win
- Tuesday: Favorite hidden feature
- Wednesday: What you wish you knew
- Thursday: Your use case story
- Friday: Feature you can't live without

### Programs
- Member spotlights (monthly)
- Challenges and contests
- Expert AMAs
- Product roadmap sneak peeks

## Moderation Tasks

### Daily (2 hours)
- Review flagged content
- Welcome new members
- Answer urgent questions
- Monitor for spam

### Weekly (3 hours)
- Surface unanswered questions
- Recognize top contributors
- Archive solved discussions
- Plan next week's activities

## Handling Violations

| Type | Action |
|------|--------|
| Spam | Remove, ban repeat |
| Inappropriate | Remove, warn, escalate if severe |
| Conflicts | De-escalate, remind guidelines |
| Misinformation | Correct politely, link official |

## Ambassador Program

**Role**: Answer questions, welcome members, contribute content

**Benefits**: Swag, early access, product team access, recognition

**Selection Criteria**:
- Active 3+ months
- Helpful, accurate
- Product expertise
- Positive tone

## Success Metrics

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Active members | Growing monthly |
| Engagement rate | >15% |
| Peer answer rate | >70% |
| Support deflection | >1000 tickets/mo |
| Community NPS | >50 |
