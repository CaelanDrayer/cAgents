# Best Practices: Account Manager

> Design principles, patterns, and frameworks that guide high-quality account management work.

## Design Principles

- **Proactive Engagement**: Anticipate needs and risks before customers raise them; never let silence become churn
- **Value-Centered Communication**: Every touchpoint should reinforce or demonstrate value delivered, not just check in
- **Mutual Success Framing**: Position every negotiation and renewal as a joint outcome, not a vendor win
- **Data-Driven Decisions**: Use health scores, usage data, and engagement signals to prioritize account actions
- **Executive Relationship Depth**: Maintain relationships at multiple levels; single-threaded accounts are fragile
- **Strategic Account Planning**: Treat each key account as a business within a business with its own growth plan
- **Expansion Mindset**: Understand the customer's roadmap deeply enough to surface the right opportunities at the right time

## Key Patterns & Frameworks

- **Account Health Score**: Composite metric combining product usage, support tickets, NPS, engagement, and contract value to signal overall account risk and opportunity
- **MEDDICC for Expansions**: Metrics, Economic Buyer, Decision Criteria, Decision Process, Identify Pain, Champion, Competition — apply the sales qualification framework to every upsell motion
- **Executive Business Review (EBR)**: Structured quarterly meeting reviewing ROI realized, roadmap alignment, and mutual goals; builds executive relationships and uncovers expansion opportunities
- **Success Plan Framework**: Documented plan with customer goals, success metrics, milestones, and owner assignments; created at onboarding and updated quarterly
- **Land and Expand Model**: Start with initial use case, prove value, then systematically expand to adjacent teams, use cases, and products
- **Risk Mitigation Playbooks**: Predefined response actions for red-flag signals like low engagement, executive departure, or missed milestones
- **Net Revenue Retention (NRR) Driver Analysis**: Decompose NRR into renewal rate, expansion rate, and contraction/churn to identify highest-leverage improvement areas
- **Renewal Timeline Management**: Begin renewal conversations 90-120 days before expiration; never let renewal come as a surprise negotiation
- **Stakeholder Mapping**: Map all key stakeholders by role, influence, and sentiment; identify champions, blockers, and uninvolved decision-makers
- **Mutual Action Plan (MAP)**: Shared, time-bound checklist co-owned with the customer to drive toward specific outcomes or milestones

## Domain Concepts & Terminology

### Account Health & Risk
- **Health Score**: Weighted composite of usage, engagement, support, NPS, and commercial signals
- **Churn Risk**: Probability a customer will not renew based on health and behavioral signals
- **At-Risk Account**: Account with health score below threshold requiring proactive intervention
- **Single-Threaded Risk**: Account where the relationship depends on one internal champion
- **Executive Sponsor**: Senior internal champion who advocates for the account's success

### Revenue Metrics
- **Net Revenue Retention (NRR)**: Revenue retained plus expansion minus contraction/churn, expressed as a percentage
- **Gross Revenue Retention (GRR)**: Revenue retained without counting expansion; pure renewal rate
- **ACV (Annual Contract Value)**: Annualized recurring revenue from a customer contract
- **ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue)**: Total predictable recurring revenue run-rate
- **Expansion Revenue**: New revenue from existing customers (upsell, cross-sell, seat adds)
- **Contraction**: Reduction in existing contract value (downgrade, seat removal)

### Engagement Models
- **High-Touch**: Dedicated CSM with frequent personal engagement; typical for enterprise or high-ACV accounts
- **Low-Touch**: Digital-led engagement with minimal direct contact; typical for SMB or low-ACV
- **Tech-Touch**: Fully automated engagement via email sequences, in-product nudges, and self-service
- **Segmentation Model**: Tiering accounts by ACV, strategic value, or growth potential to allocate CSM attention

### Planning & Strategy
- **Account Plan**: Strategic document outlining account history, stakeholders, goals, risks, and growth opportunities
- **Whitespace Analysis**: Assessment of untapped expansion potential within an existing account
- **Renewal Playbook**: Standardized set of actions, timelines, and escalation paths for renewal management
- **EBR (Executive Business Review)**: Quarterly strategic review meeting with customer leadership

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- **Reactive Account Management**: Waiting for customers to signal problems before engaging; by the time they reach out, churn risk is already elevated
- **Single-Level Relationships**: Knowing only one contact per account leaves the relationship fragile when champions leave or change roles
- **Value Amnesia**: Failing to regularly document and communicate ROI delivered; customers forget what they bought and why they're paying
- **Renewal Surprise**: Treating renewal as a one-time event rather than an ongoing process; rushed renewals signal distrust and invite competitor evaluations
- **Generic Success Plans**: Using template goals that don't reflect the customer's actual business objectives; customers disengage from plans that feel like paperwork
- **Expansion Without Readiness**: Attempting to upsell accounts that haven't realized value from their current investment; expansion on a shaky foundation churns faster
- **Ignoring Champion Signals**: Missing early signs that an internal champion is leaving, losing influence, or becoming dissatisfied; champion departure is a leading churn indicator

## Quality Indicators

- **NRR Above 110%**: Expansion revenue more than offsets churn; indicates strong value delivery and account health
- **EBR Attendance Rate**: Percentage of accounts with executive-level participation in quarterly reviews; signals relationship depth
- **Renewal Forecast Accuracy**: Predictive accuracy of renewal pipeline 90 days out; measures health signal reliability
- **Time-to-First-Value**: Days from contract close to customer achieving first measurable outcome; shorter is better
- **At-Risk Account Recovery Rate**: Percentage of flagged at-risk accounts that renew or expand; measures intervention effectiveness
- **Account Plan Coverage**: Percentage of strategic accounts with current, documented account plans; indicates proactive management discipline
- **Multi-threaded Relationships**: Average number of executive contacts per account; higher count reduces single-threaded risk

## Collaboration Touchpoints

- **With Customer Success Manager**: Share health signals and adoption blockers; CSM owns product adoption while Account Manager owns commercial relationship and expansion
- **With Sales/Account Executive**: Clean handoff at deal close with full context on buyer motivations, success criteria, and risk factors; collaborate on expansion deals
- **With Support Director**: Escalate support patterns that threaten renewal; ensure critical issues receive priority treatment for strategic accounts
- **With Product**: Surface customer feature requests and roadmap needs; strong product advocacy builds trust and reduces churn risk
- **With Finance**: Align on renewal forecasting methodology and ARR recognition; coordinate on billing disputes or contract restructuring
