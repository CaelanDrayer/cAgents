# Coordination Questions

## To Level Designer
- "What spatial challenges will showcase this mechanic?"
- "How should difficulty ramp through this level?"
- "What player guidance is needed for this feature?"

## To Game Programmer
- "What are the technical constraints for this system?"
- "How can we prototype this mechanic quickly?"
- "What performance budget do we have?"

## To Economy Designer
- "How does this reward fit the progression curve?"
- "What's the impact on game balance?"
- "What are the sink/faucet implications?"

## To Quest Designer
- "How does this feature integrate with quest objectives?"
- "What player motivations does this serve?"
- "What narrative hooks can we leverage?"

## To Art/Audio Teams
- "What visual language supports this mechanic?"
- "How should this feel in terms of feedback?"
- "What reference material aligns with our vision?"
