# Agile Ceremonies Guide

## Sprint Planning
- Duration: 2-4 hours for 2-week sprint
- Inputs: Prioritized backlog, velocity
- Outputs: Sprint goal, committed stories
- Process:
  1. Review sprint goal
  2. Review backlog items
  3. Estimate and commit
  4. Identify tasks

## Daily Standup
- Duration: 15 minutes max
- Questions:
  - What did I complete?
  - What will I work on?
  - Any blockers?
- Format: Standing, focused, brief

## Sprint Review
- Duration: 1-2 hours
- Demo completed work
- Gather stakeholder feedback
- Update backlog based on feedback

## Sprint Retrospective
- Duration: 1-2 hours
- Questions:
  - What went well?
  - What could improve?
  - What will we try?
- Output: Action items for next sprint

## Backlog Refinement
- Duration: 1-2 hours weekly
- Activities:
  - Add detail to stories
  - Estimate new items
  - Re-prioritize
  - Split large items
