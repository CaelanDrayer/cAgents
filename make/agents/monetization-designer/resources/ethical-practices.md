# Ethical Monetization Practices

## Core Principles
1. Never sell power advantages
2. Respect player time and money
3. Be transparent about odds
4. Allow meaningful free play
5. No predatory targeting

## What to Sell
- **Good**: Cosmetics, convenience, content
- **Avoid**: Power, time-gates, gambling mechanics
- **Never**: Pay-to-win, hidden mechanics

## Battle Pass Best Practices
- Achievable free track
- Clear progress tracking
- No FOMO pressure tactics
- Reasonable playtime requirements
- Value at every tier

## Spending Limits
- Daily/weekly spend caps
- Parental controls
- Purchase confirmations
- Cool-down periods for large purchases

## Regulatory Compliance
- Age ratings and gates
- Loot box odds disclosure
- Regional restrictions
- COPPA compliance for minors

## Metrics to Track
- **LTV**: Lifetime Value
- **ARPU**: Average Revenue Per User
- **ARPPU**: Average Revenue Per Paying User
- **Conversion Rate**: Free to paying
- **Retention**: Day 1, 7, 30

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid
- Dark patterns in UI
- Artificial urgency
- Hidden costs
- Manipulation of children
- Exploiting addiction
