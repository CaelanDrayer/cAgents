# Agenda Design Patterns for Planning Sessions

Templates and design principles for structuring planning meetings and workshops that produce clear outcomes.

## Agenda Design Principles

### Principle 1: Start with the Outcome

Before writing a single agenda item, define:
- What specific decisions must be made by the end of this session?
- What artifacts will participants leave with?
- What is the single most important outcome?

Write the desired outcome at the top of the agenda. Every activity must connect to it.

### Principle 2: Energy Management

Participant energy peaks early, dips mid-session, and fades at the end. Design accordingly:
- Place the hardest cognitive work in the first 90 minutes
- Schedule breaks every 60-90 minutes
- Use energizing activities after lunch (small group work, physical movement)
- Save lightweight wrap-up and commitment activities for the end

### Principle 3: Alternate Modes

Vary the interaction mode across the session to maintain engagement:

| Mode | Description | Energy Level | Duration |
|------|-------------|-------------|----------|
| Plenary input | Facilitator or speaker presents context | Low (passive) | 10-20 min max |
| Individual work | Silent writing, reflection, or sketching | Medium | 5-10 min |
| Pair discussion | Two-person conversations | Medium-High | 10-15 min |
| Small group work | Groups of 3-5 collaborate on a task | High | 15-30 min |
| Full group discussion | Facilitated dialogue with the whole room | Medium-High | 15-25 min |
| Movement activity | Gallery walk, stand-up voting, room mapping | High | 10-15 min |

Rule of thumb: never stay in one mode for more than 30 minutes.

### Principle 4: Progressive Depth

Structure the agenda to move from broad to specific:

1. **Context**: Where are we? What do we know? (10-15% of time)
2. **Exploration**: What are our options? What could we do? (25-30% of time)
3. **Evaluation**: Which options are best? What are the trade-offs? (25-30% of time)
4. **Decision**: What will we commit to? Who owns what? (20-25% of time)
5. **Close**: Summarize, confirm next steps, gather feedback (5-10% of time)

## Agenda Templates

**Outcome**: Approved quarterly plan with priorities, owners, and milestones.

| 2 | 09:30-10:15 | Previous quarter retrospective | Small groups then plenary | Retro board |
| 3 | 10:15-10:30 | Break | | |
| 4 | 10:30-11:15 | Strategic priorities review and alignment | Full group discussion | Strategy doc |
| 5 | 11:15-12:00 | Initiative brainstorm per priority | Silent brainstorm + clustering | Sticky notes |
| 6 | 12:00-13:00 | Lunch | | |
| 7 | 13:00-13:45 | Initiative prioritization | Impact-effort matrix | Wall chart |
| 8 | 13:45-14:30 | Resource allocation and dependency mapping | Small groups | Planning template |
| 9 | 14:30-14:45 | Break | | |
| 10 | 14:45-15:30 | Milestone and timeline drafting | Small groups | Timeline template |
| 11 | 15:30-16:15 | Plan review and risk identification | Gallery walk + dot voting | Printed plans |
| 12 | 16:15-16:45 | Commitment and consensus check | Fist of five per initiative | |
| 13 | 16:45-17:00 | Close, next steps, feedback | Plenary | Feedback form |

## Pre-Session Checklist

- [ ] Outcome statement defined and shared with participants in advance
- [ ] Agenda distributed at least 24 hours before the session
- [ ] Pre-read materials sent (if any) at least 48 hours before
- [ ] Required participants confirmed (decline follow-up completed)
- [ ] Room or virtual setup tested (whiteboard, sticky notes, markers, timer, projector)
- [ ] Facilitation materials prepared (templates, boards, voting dots)
- [ ] Roles assigned: facilitator, timekeeper, note-taker
- [ ] Backup plan for remote participants (if hybrid)

## Post-Session Protocol

- **Within 1 hour**: Photograph physical artifacts and secure digital artifacts
- **Within 24 hours**: Distribute session notes with decisions, action items (each with owner and deadline), and parking lot assignments
- **Within 1 week**: Follow up on action items, schedule follow-up sessions, archive materials

## Common Agenda Mistakes

- **No stated outcome**: Participants do not know what "done" looks like
- **Too much plenary input**: More than 30% presentation time kills engagement
- **No breaks**: Cognitive performance drops sharply after 90 minutes
- **Overpacked agenda**: No buffer time means falling behind is guaranteed
- **Missing decision mechanism**: Hoping consensus will "emerge" without a structured process
- **Wrong people in the room**: Missing a key stakeholder means decisions get revisited later
- **Skipping the close**: Ending without confirmed action items wastes the session
