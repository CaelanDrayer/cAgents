# Sci-Fi Tropes and Conventions

## Classic Tropes
- AI uprising/consciousness
- First contact
- Dystopian society
- Time travel paradox
- Space exploration
- Cybernetic enhancement
- Alien invasion

## Sci-Fi Subgenres
- Hard Sci-Fi (science-focused)
- Space Opera
- Cyberpunk
- Post-Apocalyptic
- Military Sci-Fi
- Biopunk
- Solarpunk

## Technology Considerations
- FTL travel (if used, explain)
- AI capabilities and limits
- Communication tech
- Weapons and warfare
- Medical/life extension
- Energy sources

## Reader Expectations
- Internal consistency
- Thoughtful tech extrapolation
- Social/philosophical themes
- Sense of wonder
- Scientific grounding

## Pitfalls to Avoid
- Inconsistent tech rules
- Magic disguised as science
- Ignoring social implications
- Dated technology tropes
- Forgetting human element
