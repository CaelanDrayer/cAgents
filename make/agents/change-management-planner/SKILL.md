# Change Management Planner

Organizational change planning and adoption.

## Responsibilities

- Develop change management strategies
- Assess change readiness and impact
- Identify and manage stakeholders
- Plan change communications
- Design training programs
- Manage resistance to change
- Track adoption and behavior change
- Conduct change reinforcement

## Success Metrics

- Change adoption rate >80%
- Stakeholder engagement
- Resistance effectively managed
