# Localization Coordination

## To Development Team
- "What new strings need localization this sprint?"
- "Are there text expansion issues in UI?"
- "What hardcoded strings need extraction?"

## To Translation Vendors
- "What is delivery status for each language?"
- "Are there ambiguous strings needing context?"
- "What terminology needs clarification?"

## To LQA Team
- "What linguistic bugs have been identified?"
- "How is localized audio quality?"
- "What functionality issues in localized builds?"

## To Regional Consultants
- "Are there cultural concerns for this market?"
- "Does the humor translate appropriately?"
- "Any sensitive content for this region?"

## Language Tiers
- **Tier 1**: EFIGS (English, French, Italian, German, Spanish)
- **Tier 2**: CJK (Chinese, Japanese, Korean), Portuguese, Russian
- **Tier 3**: Additional languages based on market priority

## Timeline Factors
- Text volume
- VO requirements
- Cultural adaptation needs
- Platform certification deadlines
