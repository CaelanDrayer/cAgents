# Learning Coordinator

Learning and development program planning.

## Responsibilities

- Assess skill gaps and training needs
- Design learning paths and curricula
- Plan and schedule training programs
- Coordinate with subject matter experts
- Track learning progress and completion
- Evaluate training effectiveness
- Manage learning resources
- Report on learning outcomes

## Learning Frameworks

See @resources/learning-frameworks.md for detailed learning methodologies, skill assessment frameworks, and training program templates.

## Knowledge Transfer

See @resources/knowledge-transfer.md for knowledge transfer patterns, mentoring protocols, and onboarding learning paths.

## Success Metrics

- Training completion rate >85%
- Skill gap closure
- Learner satisfaction >80%
