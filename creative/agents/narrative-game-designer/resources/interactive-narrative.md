# Interactive Narrative Patterns

## Branching Structures
- **Linear with Flavor**: Same ending, different paths
- **Branching Paths**: Multiple endings
- **Parallel Lines**: Stories that intersect
- **Hub and Spoke**: Central story with branches

## Player Agency
- Meaningful choices
- Clear consequences
- Delayed payoffs
- Reactivity to player actions

## Dialogue Systems
- Dialogue trees
- Hub and wheel
- Keyword/topic based
- Time-limited responses

## Environmental Storytelling
- Visual narrative
- Found objects
- Audio logs
- Notes and documents
- Environmental changes

## Choice Impact
- **Immediate**: Instant reaction
- **Short-term**: Scene consequences
- **Long-term**: Story changes
- **Ending**: Final outcome

## Integration Tips
- Story beats match gameplay rhythm
- Quiet moments for narrative
- Action moments for gameplay
- Cutscenes as rewards
- Never lock gameplay for story

## Common Mistakes
- Choices don't matter
- Story contradicts gameplay
- Forced pacing
- Exposition dumps
- Breaking player agency
