# Dialogue Techniques

## Voice Differentiation
- Vocabulary level
- Sentence length
- Speech patterns
- Regional expressions
- Professional jargon
- Personality markers

## Subtext Patterns
- Say opposite of meaning
- Avoid the real topic
- Physical contradiction
- Loaded silences
- Deflection and evasion

## Dialogue Tags
- "Said" is invisible (use mostly)
- Action beats instead of tags
- Vary placement (before/after)
- Minimize adverbs

## Bad Dialogue Habits
- "As you know, Bob..." (exposition)
- On-the-nose (saying exactly what they mean)
- All characters sound same
- Long speeches
- Formal speech in casual setting

## Conflict in Dialogue
- Different goals
- Misunderstanding
- Power dynamics
- Hidden agendas
- Emotional stakes

## Rhythm Tips
- Short lines: Tension, conflict
- Long lines: Explanation, emotion
- Interruptions: Urgency
- Trailing off: Uncertainty
- Overlapping: Chaos
