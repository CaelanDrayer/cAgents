## Filing Types by Agency

### SEC (Public Companies)
- **10-K**: Annual report (60 days after FYE)
- **10-Q**: Quarterly report (40 days)
- **8-K**: Material events (4 business days)
- **Proxy**: Shareholder meeting (before meeting)
- **Form 4**: Insider trading (2 business days)

### FDA (Medical Devices)
- **510(k)**: Substantial equivalence (~90 days review)
- **PMA**: High-risk devices (180+ days)
- **IDE**: Clinical trial authorization
- **MDR**: Adverse event reporting (30 days)

### FCC (Telecommunications)
- Equipment authorization
- Spectrum licenses
- Universal Service Fund reports
- Annual operational reports

## Agency Communication Guidelines

**Before Contact**:
- Research similar filings/decisions
- Prepare clear questions
- Document call/meeting objectives

**During Contact**:
- Take detailed notes
- Ask clarifying questions
- Confirm next steps

**After Contact**:
- Send follow-up summary
- Update filing team
- Document in file

## Compliance Calendar

Monthly review of:
- Upcoming filing deadlines
- Renewal dates
- Agency correspondence due dates
- Regulatory changes affecting filings
