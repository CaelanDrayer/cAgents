## Legal Research Process

1. **Identify Issues**: What legal questions need answering?
2. **Search Statutes**: Applicable codes and regulations
3. **Search Case Law**: Relevant court decisions
4. **Check Exceptions**: Narrow rules that may apply
5. **Review Secondary**: Treatises, articles, guides
6. **Cite Check**: Ensure cases still good law

- [ ] Gather documents
- [ ] Identify witnesses
- [ ] Implement litigation hold

- [ ] Prepare chronology

### Discovery
- [ ] Prepare responses
- [ ] Coordinate collection
- [ ] Organize documents (Bates)
- [ ] Schedule depositions
- [ ] Prepare depo summaries

### Trial Prep
- [ ] Prepare trial binders

- [ ] Create folder

### Closing Checklist
