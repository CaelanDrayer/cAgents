# Best Practices: Events Coordinator

> Design principles, patterns, and frameworks that guide high-quality event planning, logistics, and ROI measurement work.

## Design Principles

- **Event Objective Before Venue**: Define what success looks like (pipeline sourced, MQLs generated, brand impressions, customer retention) before selecting format or venue
- **Attendee Experience is the Brand**: Every logistical detail — registration, check-in, catering, AV — is an expression of brand quality
- **Pre-Event Marketing Drives Attendance, Post-Event Marketing Drives Pipeline**: Both deserve equal investment
- **Lead Capture is Non-Negotiable**: Every in-person event must have a defined process for capturing attendee data and routing to the appropriate follow-up
- **Build Reproducible Systems**: Event runbooks, vendor rosters, and logistics checklists should be documented so every event improves on the last
- **Contingency Planning is Part of the Plan**: Events have infinite failure modes; identifying the top 10 risks and their mitigations is core planning work
- **Sponsor and Speaker Experience Matters**: Sponsors who feel valued renew; speakers who are well-supported become brand advocates

## Key Patterns & Frameworks

- **Event Brief Template**: Objective, target audience, format, date/venue, budget, KPIs, marketing plan, logistics lead, and success criteria — the governing document before any vendor engagement
- **Event ROI Framework**: Direct costs (venue, catering, AV, staffing) vs. attributed pipeline and closed revenue, plus brand value and NPS of attendees — measured at 30 and 90 days post-event
- **Pre-Event Communication Sequence**: Save-the-date (8 weeks out) → Registration open (6 weeks) → Speaker/agenda announcement (4 weeks) → Early-bird close (2 weeks) → Last-chance email (3 days before) → Day-of logistics (morning of)
- **Webinar Production Checklist**: Platform test → speaker tech check → slides uploaded → polls configured → recording enabled → moderator briefed → backup plan for speaker drop → Q&A moderation plan
- **Badge and Check-In Protocol**: Pre-printed badges with QR codes, self-service kiosks for large events, VIP lane for speakers/sponsors — design for zero-queue check-in experience
- **On-Site Lead Capture System**: Badge scanning app + manual collection form + business card bowl as backup — all feeding into CRM within 24 hours of event close
- **Post-Event Follow-Up Architecture**: Hot leads (spoke with sales rep, requested demo) → immediate same-day follow-up; warm leads → personalized email within 48 hours; cold leads → nurture enrollment within 72 hours
- **Sponsor/Exhibitor Fulfillment Tracker**: Spreadsheet tracking every deliverable committed to each sponsor — logo placement, email mentions, on-site signage, speaking slots — with completion status
- **Venue Selection Scorecard**: Capacity, AV capabilities, catering quality, parking/transit access, room configuration flexibility, WiFi reliability, past event references — evaluated before committing
- **Retrospective Template**: Event goals vs. actuals, what worked, what didn't, budget variance, key lessons, and recommendations for next event — completed within 2 weeks

## Domain Concepts & Terminology

### Event Types
- **Hosted Event**: Company-organized event where you control content, audience, and experience (annual conference, executive dinner, user group)
- **Sponsored Event**: Paying to be present at or associated with an industry event organized by a third party (trade show, industry conference)
- **Webinar**: Virtual event format — lower cost than in-person, accessible globally, well-suited for thought leadership and lead generation
- **Field Event**: Small-format, regional event hosted in a target market — dinner, roundtable, workshop
- **Virtual Summit**: Multi-session online conference, often free to attend, designed for large-scale lead generation

### Logistics
- **Run of Show (RoS)**: Minute-by-minute script for event day covering every activity, person responsible, and timing
- **Production Schedule**: Week-by-week planning document covering all pre-event tasks, owners, and deadlines
- **Vendor Management**: Coordinating AV, catering, printing, security, registration tech, and other service providers
- **Force Majeure Clause**: Contract provision covering unforeseeable events (weather, venue fire) that prevent event delivery
- **BEO (Banquet Event Order)**: Contract between venue and organizer specifying food, beverage, setup, and service requirements

### Measurement
- **Event ROI**: Revenue (pipeline + closed/won) attributable to event activities divided by total event costs
- **Attendee NPS**: Post-event survey measuring attendee satisfaction and likelihood to recommend or attend again
- **Pipeline Sourced**: Opportunities first created as a result of event participation
- **Cost Per Attendee**: Total event cost divided by number of attendees — benchmarks across event types
- **Session Engagement**: For virtual events: average watch time, poll participation rate, and Q&A submissions

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- **No Post-Event Follow-Up Plan**: Collecting leads at an event and not having a structured follow-up process within 48 hours wastes the investment
- **Under-Estimating Logistics Lead Time**: Venue contracts, speaker agreements, and promotion campaigns have minimum lead times — starting late compresses quality
- **Over-Engineering Small Events**: Applying enterprise event logistics to a 20-person field dinner adds cost and complexity without proportional benefit
- **Ignoring AV Quality**: Poor audio and video at speaking events damages speaker credibility and attendee experience
- **No Contingency Budget**: Events reliably run over budget in unexpected areas; a 10–15% contingency reserve is standard practice
- **Sponsor Overpromise**: Committing to sponsor deliverables without confirming feasibility with the operations team creates relationship damage when deliverables miss
- **Measuring Attendance Only**: Reporting registration and attendance numbers without connecting to pipeline contribution gives leadership an incomplete picture of ROI

## Quality Indicators

- **Attendance Rate**: Registrants who actually attend (typical benchmark: 40–60% for free events, 70–80%+ for paid)
- **Pipeline Sourced per Event Dollar**: Dollar of pipeline generated per dollar of event investment — primary ROI metric
- **Lead Capture Rate**: Percentage of attendees whose contact information was captured and entered into CRM
- **Post-Event Follow-Up SLA Adherence**: What percentage of hot leads received same-day follow-up?
- **Attendee NPS**: Average score and trend across events of the same type
- **Budget Variance**: Actual vs. planned spend; consistent over-runs indicate planning or scope discipline issues
- **Sponsor Retention Rate**: Percentage of sponsors from last year's event who renewed — a signal of sponsor satisfaction

## Collaboration Touchpoints

- **With Campaign Manager**: Events are part of the broader marketing calendar — coordinate timing, messaging, and audience targeting with campaign strategy
- **With Field Marketing Manager**: Field events are co-owned; field manager defines regional strategy and audience while coordinator handles logistics and execution
- **With Demand Generation Manager**: Event leads must flow into the appropriate nurture sequence and lead scoring workflow — define the routing before the event, not after
- **With Copywriter**: Event landing pages, speaker bios, invitation emails, and post-event recaps all require copy that matches the event's brand and audience
- **With Marketing Analyst**: Post-event ROI analysis requires pre-agreed measurement methodology and access to pipeline data — establish the measurement plan during event planning
