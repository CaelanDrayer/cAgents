# Training Frameworks

## New Hire Onboarding Curriculum

### Week 1: Foundation
| Day | Topics | Hours | Assessment |
|-----|--------|-------|------------|
| 1 | Culture, philosophy, tools, meet team | 6 | - |
| 2-3 | Product basics, core features, exercises | 16 | Product quiz (80%) |
| 4-5 | Support processes, ticketing, KB usage | 16 | Tools check |

### Week 2: Skill Building
| Day | Topics | Hours | Assessment |
|-----|--------|-------|------------|
| 6-7 | Communication, de-escalation, empathy | 16 | Role-play |
| 8-9 | Troubleshooting, common issues, shadowing | 16 | Scenario test |
| 10 | Full-day shadowing experienced agent | 8 | Feedback |

### Week 3: Supervised Practice
- Handle real tickets with mentor review
- All responses reviewed before sending
- Daily check-ins
- Gradual complexity increase

### Week 4: Increasing Independence
- Handle tickets independently
- Mentor spot-checks 30%
- Complex issues introduction
- Final certification assessment

### Certification Criteria
- Product knowledge: >85% exam
- Quality scores: Average >80 on 10 tickets
- SLA compliance: 100% during supervised period
- CSAT: >90% positive ratings
- Manager approval

## Training Materials Checklist

- [ ] Slide deck (one concept per slide)
- [ ] Hands-on exercises
- [ ] Knowledge check quiz (80% passing)
- [ ] Job aids / quick reference
- [ ] Recording for self-paced

## Proficiency Levels

| Level | Experience | Capabilities |
|-------|------------|--------------|
| Foundation | 0-3 months | Core knowledge, tier 1 |
| Proficient | 3-12 months | Complex troubleshooting, tier 2, mentors |
| Expert | 12+ months | Deep expertise, tier 3, creates content |
| Specialist | 18+ months | SME, leads projects |

## Measuring Effectiveness

### Kirkpatrick Model
| Level | Measure | Target | When |
|-------|---------|--------|------|
| Reaction | Post-training survey | >85% positive | Immediately |
| Learning | Quiz/assessment | >85% passing | During/post |
| Behavior | Quality scores | Improvement | 30-60 days |
| Results | CSAT, resolution time | Positive trend | Quarterly |

### Training ROI
```yaml
costs:
  - Trainer time × rate
  - Agent time × agents × rate
  - Materials and platform

benefits:
  - Productivity gain
  - Quality improvement
  - Escalation reduction
  - Self-service improvement

roi: (Benefits - Costs) / Costs × 100%
```

## Ongoing Training Schedule

- **Monthly** (60 min): Product updates
- **Quarterly** (half-day): Skills workshops
- **Weekly** (30 min): Knowledge share Fridays
- **Self-Paced**: Video library, e-learning, certifications
