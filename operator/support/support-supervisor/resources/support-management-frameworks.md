# Support Management Frameworks

## Coaching Framework

### Weekly 1-on-1 Structure (30 min)
1. **Agent-Led** (10 min): Challenges, questions, career goals
2. **Performance Review** (10 min): Metrics, recent tickets, quality
3. **Skill Development** (5 min): Training, stretch assignments
4. **Support Needed** (5 min): Resources, tools, process

### Topics to Cover
- Recent high-quality interactions
- Specific improvement areas with examples
- Progress on development goals
- Career aspirations and path
- Well-being and job satisfaction

### Performance Improvement Plan
**When**: 3+ consecutive weeks below target
**Components**:
- Document specific gaps
- Set measurable goals
- Weekly check-ins
- Track over 30-60-90 days
- Recognize improvement or take action

## Escalation Protocols

### When to Take Over Ticket
- Customer requests manager
- Agent unable to resolve after attempts
- Customer threatening to churn
- Policy exception needed
- Cross-team coordination required

### Escalate to Escalation Manager
- Technical beyond support scope
- Multiple customers affected
- Potential product bug
- Customer demanding engineering

### Escalate to VP
- Top-tier customer at risk
- Legal/compliance concerns
- Media/PR issues
- Team resource crisis

## De-escalation Techniques

1. Listen actively, acknowledge frustration
2. Apologize sincerely for impact
3. Take ownership, don't deflect
4. Provide clear action plan
5. Offer value-added resolution
6. Follow through personally

## Resource Allocation

### Ticket Assignment Strategy
- Match complexity to skill level
- Distribute evenly
- Assign VIPs to senior agents
- Give new agents mentored assignments
- Balance ticket types

### Workload Management
- Monitor individual ticket loads
- Prevent cherry-picking
- Identify bottlenecks
- Redistribute when struggling
- Escalate capacity issues

## Quality Review Process

### Weekly Review (per agent)
Evaluate sample of 5-10 tickets:
- Professionalism and empathy
- Accuracy of information
- Completeness of resolution
- Timeliness
- Proper use of tools
- KB article usage

### Feedback Approach
- Specific examples of what was done well
- Concrete improvement suggestions
- Resources for gaps
- Recognition for exceptional work

## Daily Workflow

**Morning**:
- Review overnight tickets/escalations
- Check team attendance
- Brief team on known issues
- Assign priority tickets
- Set daily focus

**During Day**:
- Monitor queue and response times
- Jump in on difficult tickets
- Provide real-time coaching
- Handle escalations

**End of Day**:
- Review metrics
- Identify trends
- Update escalation manager
- Plan tomorrow
- Recognize wins
