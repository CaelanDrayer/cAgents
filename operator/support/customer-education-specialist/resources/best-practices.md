# Best Practices: Customer Education Specialist

> Design principles, patterns, and frameworks that guide high-quality customer training and education program work.

## Design Principles

- **Outcome-Centered Design**: Design learning experiences around specific behavioral outcomes (what the customer will be able to do), not content coverage (what topics will be covered)
- **Adult Learning Respect**: Adult learners are self-directed and need to understand why content is relevant before engaging; connect every learning module to a concrete job or goal they care about
- **Progressive Disclosure**: Layer complexity — basic concepts before advanced, conceptual before procedural; don't overwhelm customers with everything at once
- **Active Over Passive Learning**: Customers learn and retain more from doing than watching; design exercises, simulations, and practice opportunities into every program
- **Measurement at All Levels**: Track completion, comprehension, behavioral change, and business impact; training that only measures completion misses what actually matters
- **Tiered Learning Paths**: Different customer roles need different content; an end-user path differs from an administrator path or an executive path — treat them as separate programs
- **Self-Service Scaling**: One-to-many education (on-demand courses, certifications, documentation) scales infinitely; one-to-one training does not — design programs that can grow without proportional headcount

## Key Patterns & Frameworks

- **ADDIE Instructional Design Model**: Analysis → Design → Development → Implementation → Evaluation; systematic framework for building education programs that deliver intended outcomes
- **Kirkpatrick Evaluation Model**: Level 1 (Reaction: did they like it?), Level 2 (Learning: did they gain knowledge?), Level 3 (Behavior: did they apply it?), Level 4 (Results: did it impact the business?); measure at all four levels
- **Bloom's Taxonomy**: Knowledge → Comprehension → Application → Analysis → Synthesis → Evaluation; design learning objectives that advance customers up this hierarchy rather than staying at basic recall
- **Learning Path Architecture**: Map customer roles to specific sequences of modules with prerequisite gates; structured paths prevent overwhelm and guide customers toward mastery
- **Onboarding Academy Model**: Structured first-30-day curriculum covering account setup, core use cases, and initial value milestones; reduces time-to-value and improves retention
- **Certification Program Design**: Define competency levels (Foundation, Practitioner, Expert), map content to each level, create assessments with passing thresholds, issue credentials with expiration/renewal cycles
- **Train-the-Trainer Program**: Build a program to certify customer administrators and power users as internal trainers; multiplies reach without multiplying headcount
- **Webinar Engagement Optimization**: Pre-work to set expectations, polls and Q&A to maintain engagement, post-webinar resources to extend learning, recordings to serve asynchronous learners

## Domain Concepts & Terminology

### Instructional Design
- **Learning Objective**: Specific, measurable statement of what learners will be able to do after completing a module
- **Prerequisite**: Knowledge or skill a learner must have before beginning a module; prevents struggling with advanced content before basics are mastered
- **Scaffolding**: Temporary support structures in learning design that help learners build toward independent mastery
- **Formative Assessment**: Low-stakes check during learning (quizzes, exercises) to gauge comprehension and identify gaps
- **Summative Assessment**: End-of-module or end-of-course evaluation of whether learning objectives were achieved
- **Cognitive Load**: Mental effort required to process information; good design minimizes extraneous load while focusing attention on essential content
- **Spaced Repetition**: Revisiting content at increasing intervals to improve long-term retention

### Program Types
- **On-Demand Course**: Self-paced video and interactive content available anytime; scalable and accessible
- **Live Webinar**: Scheduled instructor-led session with real-time interaction; higher engagement than pre-recorded
- **Instructor-Led Training (ILT)**: In-person or virtual classroom training with synchronous instruction
- **Certification**: Credential awarded upon demonstrated mastery; creates customer commitment and engagement
- **Microlearning**: Short, focused learning modules (3-7 minutes) targeting a single concept or skill
- **Learning Path**: Curated sequence of modules designed to develop a specific competency or complete a role-based curriculum

### Measurement
- **Enrollment Rate**: Percentage of customer contacts registered for education programs; target >60%
- **Completion Rate**: Percentage of enrolled customers who complete programs; target >70%
- **Assessment Pass Rate**: Percentage of learners achieving passing scores on knowledge assessments
- **Satisfaction Score**: Post-training learner rating; target >4.5/5
- **Adoption Impact**: Comparison of product usage metrics between trained and untrained customers; quantifies training ROI
- **Time-to-Value**: Days from contract close to first value milestone; training accelerates this metric

### Platform & Delivery
- **LMS (Learning Management System)**: Platform for hosting, delivering, and tracking online courses and certifications
- **SCORM**: Standard for e-learning content packaging and tracking; ensures compatibility across LMS platforms
- **xAPI (Tin Can)**: Modern standard for tracking learning across systems and experiences beyond the LMS
- **Webinar Platform**: Tool for live virtual training (e.g., Zoom, GoToWebinar, Crowdcast)

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- **Content Dumping**: Designing courses by converting all available product documentation into slides; creates passive, overwhelming learning experiences with poor retention
- **Measuring Only Completion**: Reporting training completion rates to leadership without any measurement of behavioral change or business impact; completion measures effort, not effectiveness
- **One-Size-Fits-All Paths**: Building a single training program for all customer roles and levels; a beginner user needs completely different content than an enterprise administrator
- **Waiting for Perfection**: Delaying program launch until every piece of content is polished; imperfect content available now is more valuable than perfect content available later
- **Training as Support Alternative**: Positioning training primarily as a way to reduce support tickets rather than as an investment in customer success; this framing produces inferior educational experiences
- **Ignoring Webinar Drop-Off**: Running live webinars without analyzing attendance and drop-off patterns; declining attendance signals content relevance problems
- **Certification Without Value**: Creating certifications that customers don't perceive as worth having; a credential must signal meaningful competency to be motivating

## Quality Indicators

- **Enrollment Rate >60%**: Majority of customer contacts actively participating in education programs
- **Completion Rate >70%**: Most enrolled customers finishing the programs they start
- **Satisfaction >4.5/5**: Learners rate the experience highly
- **Trained vs. Untrained Adoption Gap**: Measurable difference in product usage between trained and untrained customer populations
- **Certification Program Cohort Growth**: Increasing number of customers holding active certifications year over year
- **Content Freshness**: All course content reviewed and updated within 90 days of major product releases
- **NPS Correlation**: Trained customers show measurably higher NPS than untrained customers in the same cohort

## Collaboration Touchpoints

- **With Customer Success Manager**: Align training content to success milestones; CSM triggers training recommendations at key onboarding stages
- **With Knowledge Base Manager**: Coordinate on content overlap between training courses and KB articles; training should extend and reference KB content, not duplicate it
- **With Product**: Receive product release notes and roadmap visibility early enough to update training content before features launch
- **With Support Operations Manager**: Share training data on common misunderstandings; high-volume support topics indicate gaps in the education curriculum
