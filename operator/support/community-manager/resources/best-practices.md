# Best Practices: Community Manager

> Design principles, patterns, and frameworks that guide high-quality community building and management work.

## Design Principles

- **Community Before Content**: A thriving community creates its own content; focus on member relationships and participation structures, not just publishing cadence
- **Peer Power**: Design for peer-to-peer value exchange; a community where members help each other scales better than one that depends on the company
- **Psychological Safety First**: Members contribute only when they feel safe from judgment, mockery, or dismissal; establish and enforce norms relentlessly
- **Recognition Drives Retention**: Publicly acknowledge contributions regularly; recognition is the highest-leverage engagement lever at zero cost
- **Moderation as Culture**: Every moderation decision signals what behavior is acceptable; be consistent and transparent about enforcement
- **Quality Over Quantity**: An engaged community of 1,000 active members outperforms a dormant community of 100,000; optimize for engagement rate, not member count
- **Voice of Community**: Treat the community as an ongoing research panel; surface member needs and insights to product and support consistently

## Key Patterns & Frameworks

- **Community Lifecycle Model**: Foundation (0-3 months: setup and seed), Growth (4-9 months: scale programs), Maturity (10+ months: sustain and advocate) — actions differ by phase
- **Engagement Loop Design**: Trigger → Action → Variable Reward → Investment; design participation flows that create habitual return visits
- **Ambassador Program**: Identify and formalize top contributors as community ambassadors; give them recognition, early access, and a direct line to the team
- **Peer Answer Rate Optimization**: Track what percentage of questions get answered by other members before staff intervene; >70% indicates a healthy self-sustaining community
- **Content Seeding**: During foundation phase, pre-populate with high-quality discussions, use cases, and questions to create a sense of activity before organic participation begins
- **Moderation Escalation Matrix**: Tiered response to violations — first offense warning, repeat offense temporary ban, severe violation permanent ban; document consistently
- **Community Health Dashboard**: Weekly review of active members, post volume, peer answer rate, response time, and sentiment to catch decline early
- **Community-Led Support Deflection Tracking**: Measure tickets created by community members vs. non-members; quantify the support cost savings of community engagement
- **User-Generated Content (UGC) Programs**: Structured campaigns that invite members to share tutorials, case studies, or tips; multiplies content output with low platform cost

## Domain Concepts & Terminology

### Community Metrics
- **Monthly Active Members (MAM)**: Unique members who took at least one action (post, comment, vote) in a 30-day window
- **Engagement Rate**: Percentage of total members who are monthly active; target >15%
- **Peer Answer Rate**: Percentage of questions answered by non-staff members before staff respond; target >70%
- **Support Deflection**: Reduction in support ticket volume attributable to community self-service
- **Community NPS**: Net Promoter Score measured within the community; target >50
- **Time to First Response**: Median time before a new post receives a reply; indicator of community health

### Community Structure
- **Forum/Discussion Board**: Threaded conversation space organized by topic categories
- **Sub-Community/Group**: Smaller, topic-specific segment within the broader community
- **Featured Member**: Recognition designation for top contributors
- **Ambassador**: Formally recognized community leader with access to exclusive content or programs
- **User Group**: Geography- or industry-specific sub-group of community members
- **Community Guidelines**: Publicly published norms for acceptable behavior; foundation for moderation

### Engagement Programs
- **Customer Advisory Board (CAB)**: Formal group of select customers who provide strategic feedback to product and leadership
- **Beta Program**: Early access program where engaged community members test pre-release features
- **Spotlight Series**: Regular feature highlighting a member's use case or success story
- **Ask Me Anything (AMA)**: Scheduled live session where company experts answer community questions
- **Challenge/Contest**: Time-bounded competition designed to drive participation and UGC creation

### Moderation
- **Community Guidelines**: Published rules defining acceptable behavior and consequences for violations
- **Moderation Queue**: List of flagged content awaiting review before publication or action
- **Shadow Ban**: Restricting a member's visibility without notifying them; used sparingly
- **Escalation Path**: Process for handing moderation decisions to legal or leadership when content is high-stakes

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- **Broadcasting Without Listening**: Using the community as a one-way announcement channel; members disengage when they feel they're talking to a billboard
- **Inconsistent Moderation**: Enforcing guidelines selectively or unevenly; perceived favoritism destroys psychological safety and trust
- **Ignoring Power Users**: Failing to recognize or nurture top contributors; they become disenchanted and take their activity elsewhere
- **Community as Support Overflow**: Treating the community primarily as a place to deflect tickets rather than as a strategic asset; members notice and resent the dynamic
- **Metrics Without Meaning**: Optimizing for raw member count or post volume without measuring engagement quality or peer answer rate
- **Content Desert**: Launching a community and failing to seed it with quality discussion before inviting members; first impressions of emptiness drive immediate churn
- **Reactive-Only Moderation**: Waiting for reports before addressing problematic behavior; proactive monitoring catches issues before they damage community culture

## Quality Indicators

- **Engagement Rate >15%**: Monthly active members as a percentage of total members
- **Peer Answer Rate >70%**: Most questions answered by community members, not staff
- **Ambassador Program Cohort**: Active, growing group of recognized contributors driving disproportionate value
- **Support Deflection Trending Up**: Measurable, documented reduction in tickets from community-engaged users
- **Community NPS >50**: Members are promoters of the community experience itself
- **Response Time <4 Hours**: Questions receive a response quickly enough to feel like a live conversation
- **Zero Unresolved Moderation Escalations**: No pending high-severity content issues older than 24 hours

## Collaboration Touchpoints

- **With Customer Advocacy Manager**: Identify ambassador candidates and case study subjects from the most active and successful community members
- **With Customer Success Manager**: Share community insights about adoption challenges and feature confusion; CSM can leverage community peers as social proof
- **With Product**: Channel community feedback and feature requests directly to product; community is the richest qualitative input source available
- **With Support Operations Manager**: Coordinate on deflection measurement methodology; align community and support on shared KB content
