# Sales Training Frameworks

## Onboarding Curriculum

**Week 1-2**: Foundation
- Company overview
- Product fundamentals
- Sales process
- CRM and tools

**Week 3-4**: Skills
- Industry landscape
- Competitive intelligence
- Discovery skills
- Demo delivery

**Week 5-8**: Practice
- Advanced product
- Objection handling
- Negotiation
- Role-plays and shadowing

## Coaching Models

### GROW Model
- **Goal**: What do you want to achieve?
- **Reality**: What's the current situation?
- **Options**: What could you do?
- **Way Forward**: What will you do?

### Call Review Framework
1. **Prep**: Review call plan
2. **Execution**: Assess performance
3. **Feedback**: Strengths and gaps
4. **Practice**: Role-play improvement
5. **Action**: Apply in next call
