# Best Practices: Campaign Manager

> Design principles, patterns, and frameworks that guide high-quality multi-channel campaign planning, execution, and optimization.

## Design Principles

- **Audience Before Channel**: Know precisely who you're reaching before selecting where and how you'll reach them
- **One Campaign, One Goal**: Each campaign should optimize for a single primary KPI; split objectives across campaigns
- **Creative and Data in Tension**: Let data guide optimization, but preserve creative risk-taking that data can't validate in advance
- **Budget as a Test Budget**: Treat initial spend as learning investment; scale only what the data supports
- **Integration Discipline**: Ensure all channels reinforce the same message — fragmented campaigns signal fragmented brands
- **Cadence over Burst**: Sustained, consistent presence outperforms sporadic heavy spending for most objectives
- **Post-Campaign Ritual**: Document learnings systematically so every campaign makes the next one smarter

## Key Patterns & Frameworks

- **Campaign Brief Template**: Audience, objective, KPIs, messaging, creative requirements, budget, timeline, and success criteria — the contract between strategy and execution
- **RACE Framework**: Reach, Act, Convert, Engage — organizing campaign activities across the full funnel
- **Integrated Campaign Architecture**: How paid, owned, and earned channels are orchestrated around a single campaign idea with consistent messaging
- **Always-On vs. Burst**: Separating evergreen performance campaigns from time-bound campaign sprints with different optimization logic
- **A/B Test Matrix**: Structured approach to testing creative, copy, audience, and offer variables without contaminating results
- **Budget Pacing Model**: Daily/weekly spend curves aligned to audience availability and business seasonality
- **Attribution Framework Selection**: Choosing first-touch, last-touch, or multi-touch attribution based on campaign objective and sales cycle length
- **Retargeting Ladder**: Sequenced ad exposure based on prior engagement — site visitor → product viewer → cart abandoner → purchaser
- **Creative Rotation Rules**: Frequency caps and rotation logic to prevent ad fatigue while maintaining brand presence
- **Campaign Retrospective Format**: Structured post-mortem covering goal vs. actual, what worked, what didn't, and what to change next time

## Domain Concepts & Terminology

### Planning
- **Campaign Brief**: The governing document defining audience, objective, creative direction, budget, and success metrics
- **Media Mix**: The allocation of budget across channels (paid social, search, email, display, events, etc.)
- **Flighting**: The scheduling pattern for when a campaign runs — continuous, pulsing, or burst
- **Reach vs. Frequency**: The trade-off between exposing more unique people once versus fewer people multiple times
- **Share of Voice (SOV)**: Your campaign's presence relative to competitors in a specific channel or medium

### Execution
- **Creative Brief**: Direction to creative teams specifying format, message, tone, and deliverables for campaign assets
- **UTM Parameters**: URL tags used to attribute traffic and conversions to specific campaigns, channels, and creative
- **QA Checklist**: Pre-launch verification of tracking, links, audience targeting, and budget caps
- **Ad Fatigue**: Diminishing performance caused by audiences seeing the same creative too many times
- **Dayparting**: Scheduling ads to run only during hours or days with the highest conversion probability

### Metrics
- **CPM (Cost Per Mille)**: Cost to reach 1,000 people — a reach and awareness efficiency metric
- **CTR (Click-Through Rate)**: Percentage of people who click after seeing the ad
- **CPA (Cost Per Acquisition)**: Total spend divided by the number of desired actions completed
- **ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)**: Revenue generated per dollar of ad spend
- **MQL (Marketing Qualified Lead)**: A lead that meets predefined scoring criteria indicating sales readiness
- **Pipeline Contribution**: The portion of total sales pipeline attributed to campaign-sourced leads

### Channels
- **TOFU/MOFU/BOFU**: Top/Middle/Bottom of funnel — different content and offers for different stages of buyer readiness
- **Paid Social**: Advertising on social platforms (Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok) with audience targeting capabilities
- **Paid Search (SEM)**: Ads triggered by keyword searches on Google or Bing
- **Display/Programmatic**: Image and video ads served across websites via ad networks or DSPs

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- **Vanity Metric Optimization**: Optimizing for impressions or likes when the goal is revenue or pipeline generation
- **Undifferentiated Messaging**: Using the same message for all audiences ignores that different buyers care about different things
- **Over-Rotating on Initial Data**: Making major budget shifts based on the first 48–72 hours of data before statistical significance is reached
- **No UTM Discipline**: Campaigns without proper tracking make attribution impossible and learnings unmeasurable
- **Budget Fragmentation**: Spreading small budgets across too many channels dilutes effectiveness — better to win on fewer channels
- **Ignoring Creative Fatigue**: Letting the same creative run indefinitely leads to declining performance that looks like audience exhaustion
- **Skipping the Post-Mortem**: Without documented learnings, the same mistakes recur across campaign cycles

## Quality Indicators

- **Goal Achievement Rate**: Did the campaign hit its primary KPI (not just secondary metrics)?
- **Attribution Completeness**: Can you trace every dollar of spend to a measurable outcome?
- **Creative Test Velocity**: How many meaningful A/B tests were run and how quickly were learnings applied?
- **Spend Pacing Accuracy**: Did actual daily spend stay within 10% of planned pacing?
- **Cross-Channel Message Consistency**: Do all channels reinforce the same core message without contradiction?
- **MQL Quality Score**: Are campaign-sourced MQLs converting to SQLs at the expected rate downstream?
- **Cost Efficiency Trend**: Is CPA improving over the campaign lifecycle as optimization accumulates?

## Collaboration Touchpoints

- **With Copywriter**: Provide campaign brief with audience, tone, and offer context; review drafts against message architecture before asset production
- **With Marketing Analyst**: Define measurement framework before launch; review performance data weekly and act on insights together
- **With Email Marketing Specialist**: Coordinate campaign messaging and timing so email cadences reinforce rather than duplicate paid channel efforts
- **With Sales Development Rep**: Align on MQL definition and SLA so campaign-generated leads are followed up with at the right speed and message
- **With Product Marketing Manager**: Ensure campaign messaging reflects approved product positioning and competitive differentiation
