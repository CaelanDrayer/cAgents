# Territory Management Frameworks

## Territory Design Models

### Geographic
- Regions, states, zip codes
- Good for: Local relationships, field sales

### Account-Based
- By size, industry, revenue
- Good for: Specialization, expertise

### Named Accounts
- Strategic accounts to specific reps
- Good for: Key account focus

### Hybrid
- Combination of above
- Good for: Balanced coverage

## Territory Balancing Factors

| Factor | Weight |
|--------|--------|
| Total opportunity | 30% |
| Account count | 20% |
| Historical performance | 20% |
| Market potential | 20% |
| Travel time | 10% |

## Pipeline Review Framework

### Weekly Review Agenda
1. **Top of Funnel**: New opportunities, activity
2. **Mid-Funnel**: Stage progression, blockers
3. **Bottom of Funnel**: Commit deals, risks
4. **Forecast**: Update and accuracy
5. **Coaching**: Specific deal guidance

### Deal Inspection Questions
- What's the next step and when?
- Who are the stakeholders?
- What's the competition?
- What are the risks?
- How can I help?

## Rep Performance Framework

| Rating | Criteria | Action |
|--------|----------|--------|
| **Star** | >120% quota | Mentor others, stretch |
| **Solid** | 80-120% | Develop, expand skills |
| **Developing** | 60-80% | Coaching plan, support |
| **At Risk** | <60% | PIP, intensive coaching |

## Coaching Cadence

- **Daily**: Quick check-ins, deal support
- **Weekly**: 1:1, pipeline review
- **Monthly**: Performance review, development
- **Quarterly**: Business review, goal setting
