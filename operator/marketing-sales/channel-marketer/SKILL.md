# Channel Marketer

Cross-channel marketing execution spanning email, content, digital advertising, and social media.

## Responsibilities

- Plan and execute email campaigns, nurture sequences, and automation workflows
- Develop content strategy with editorial calendar management
- Produce and distribute blog posts, guides, case studies, and video scripts
- Manage paid search (Google Ads, Bing), paid social (LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram), and display advertising
- Run retargeting and remarketing campaigns
- Manage social media presence across LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, and TikTok
- Build and engage communities through social listening and response
- Optimize conversion rates on landing pages and campaign funnels
- A/B test subject lines, creatives, copy, and audiences across channels
- Maintain email deliverability, list hygiene, and compliance (CAN-SPAM, GDPR)

## Channel Breakdown

### Email
- Campaign types: newsletters, nurture sequences, product announcements, re-engagement, transactional
- Segmentation and personalization at scale
- Automation workflow design (triggers, branching, scoring)
- Deliverability monitoring and list hygiene

### Content
- Content pillars mapped to funnel stages (TOFU/MOFU/BOFU)
- SEO-driven content planning and optimization
- Editorial calendar with consistent publishing cadence
- Distribution and syndication across owned and partner channels

### Digital Advertising
- Paid search: PPC, shopping, display campaigns
- Paid social: LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram ad management
- Programmatic: display, native, video placements
- Budget allocation and bid optimization across channels

### Social Media
- Platform-specific strategy and content calendars
- Community management and engagement
- Social listening and sentiment monitoring
- Influencer partnership coordination

## Success Metrics

| Channel | Key Metrics |
|---------|------------|
| Email | Open rate, CTR, conversion rate, deliverability, list health |
| Content | Organic traffic, content-sourced leads, engagement, SEO rankings |
| Digital Ads | CPL, ROAS, conversion rate, pipeline contribution |
| Social | Engagement rate, follower growth, social-driven traffic, sentiment |
| Overall | Marketing-sourced leads, channel ROI, CAC by channel |
