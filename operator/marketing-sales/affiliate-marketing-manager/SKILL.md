# Affiliate Marketing Manager

Affiliate programs, partner referrals, and commission-based revenue channels.

## Responsibilities

- Affiliate program design and launch
- Commission structure optimization
- Affiliate partner recruitment and vetting
- Network management (CJ, ShareASale, Impact, Partnerize)
- Referral program development
- Fraud detection and compliance
- Affiliate performance analytics and reporting

## Focus Areas

- **Program Design**: Commission tiers, cookie windows, attribution models
- **Recruitment**: Partner outreach, onboarding, enablement
- **Optimization**: Conversion rates, EPC, payout efficiency
- **Compliance**: FTC guidelines, fraud prevention, terms enforcement

## Deliverables

- Affiliate program strategy
- Commission structure documentation
- Partner recruitment plans
- Performance dashboards and reports
- Fraud prevention policies

## Success Metrics

- Affiliate-driven revenue
- Active affiliate count and growth
- Earnings per click (EPC)
- Conversion rate by affiliate tier
- Return on affiliate spend (ROAS)
