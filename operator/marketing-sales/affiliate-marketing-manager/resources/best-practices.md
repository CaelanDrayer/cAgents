# Best Practices: Affiliate Marketing Manager

> Design principles, patterns, and frameworks that guide high-quality affiliate program management and partner revenue work.

## Design Principles

- **Partner Success First**: Affiliates perform best when they have the tools, content, and support to succeed — prioritize their experience
- **Commission Alignment**: Structure payouts so affiliate incentives align with your most valuable customer actions
- **Quality Over Quantity**: A small network of high-converting partners outperforms hundreds of inactive ones
- **Transparency Builds Trust**: Clear reporting, prompt payments, and honest communication retain top affiliates
- **Attribution Precision**: Invest in accurate tracking so every partner is fairly credited for their contribution
- **Compliance as Foundation**: FTC disclosures and network terms aren't optional — enforce them proactively
- **Segment and Specialize**: Different affiliate types (content, coupon, influencer, B2B) require different strategies and terms

## Key Patterns & Frameworks

- **Affiliate Tiering**: Segment partners into tiers (e.g., Bronze/Silver/Gold/Platinum) based on performance to allocate resources and offer escalating benefits
- **Commission Architecture**: Design base rates, bonuses, and performance escalators that reward desired behaviors (new customers vs. returning, high-LTV segments)
- **Recruitment Funnel**: Systematic process for identifying, vetting, onboarding, and activating new affiliate partners — from prospect list to first conversion
- **Affiliate Activation Sequence**: Email + resource workflow that moves newly approved affiliates from registration to first active campaign within 30 days
- **Sub-Affiliate Network Management**: Managing relationships with affiliate networks that aggregate smaller publishers — balancing network fees against reach
- **Coupon/Code Discipline**: Policy governing when coupon codes are issued and how they're tracked to prevent cannibalization of organic conversions
- **Content Affiliate Playbook**: Briefing process for review, comparison, and how-to affiliates with messaging guidelines, approved claims, and asset packages
- **Attribution Windows**: Setting first-touch, last-touch, or hybrid credit windows that fairly compensate partners in multi-step buyer journeys
- **Fraud Detection Protocol**: Monitoring for click stuffing, cookie dropping, and fake leads — and the escalation process when violations are detected
- **Performance Review Cadence**: Monthly and quarterly reviews with top affiliates to share data, align on goals, and plan promotional periods

## Domain Concepts & Terminology

### Program Structure
- **Affiliate Network**: Third-party platform (ShareASale, CJ, Impact) that connects advertisers with publishers and handles tracking/payments
- **In-House Program**: Self-managed affiliate program using first-party tracking software (PartnerStack, Rewardful)
- **Publisher**: The affiliate — a website, influencer, app, or media property that promotes your offer
- **Advertiser**: The brand running the affiliate program
- **Sub-Affiliate**: An affiliate who recruits their own network of publishers (multi-level structure)

### Commission Models
- **CPA (Cost Per Acquisition)**: Pay only when a sale or qualified lead is generated
- **CPS (Cost Per Sale)**: Commission as a percentage of the transaction value
- **CPL (Cost Per Lead)**: Fixed fee for each qualified lead delivered
- **Recurring Commission**: Ongoing percentage paid for subscription renewals driven by the affiliate
- **Tiered Commission**: Higher rates unlock as affiliates hit volume or revenue thresholds

### Tracking & Attribution
- **Cookie Window**: Duration after a click during which a conversion is attributed to the affiliate
- **EPC (Earnings Per Click)**: Total commissions divided by clicks — a key affiliate performance benchmark
- **Conversion Rate**: Percentage of referred visitors who complete the target action
- **Reversal Rate**: Percentage of commissions reversed due to returns, fraud, or chargebacks
- **Pixel Tracking**: Conversion tracking via a JavaScript pixel on the thank-you page

### Compliance
- **FTC Disclosure**: Legal requirement for affiliates to disclose their commercial relationship with the brand
- **Network Terms of Service**: Rules governing acceptable promotional methods on affiliate networks
- **Brand Safety Guidelines**: Restrictions on where and how affiliates may use brand assets

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- **Set-and-Forget Management**: Affiliate programs decay without active management; top partners leave for better-supported programs
- **Race-to-Bottom Commissions**: Competing solely on commission rate attracts low-quality, transient affiliates rather than committed partners
- **Ignoring Attribution Overlap**: Failing to analyze how affiliates interact with other channels leads to over-paying for conversions you would have gotten anyway
- **Weak Onboarding**: Approving affiliates without an activation sequence results in most never running their first campaign
- **Allowing Undisclosed Promotions**: Liability for FTC non-compliance rests with the advertiser, not the affiliate
- **No Fraud Monitoring**: Sophisticated click fraud can drain budget and inflate apparent program performance
- **Neglecting Mid-Tier Partners**: Focusing only on top performers misses the compounding value of activating the middle of the curve

## Quality Indicators

- **Active Affiliate Rate**: Percentage of approved affiliates who generated at least one conversion in the last 30 days (healthy: >25%)
- **EPC vs. Benchmark**: Comparing your program's EPC against network averages signals relative attractiveness to new publishers
- **Reversal Rate**: Should stay below 5–10%; spikes indicate fraud or product/service quality issues
- **New Partner Activation Rate**: Percentage of newly approved affiliates who convert within 60 days
- **Revenue Concentration Risk**: If >60% of affiliate revenue comes from one partner, the program is over-concentrated
- **Cost Per Acquisition vs. Other Channels**: Affiliate CPA should benchmark favorably against paid search and display
- **Compliance Audit Score**: Regular checks for FTC disclosures and brand guideline adherence across active affiliates

## Collaboration Touchpoints

- **With Campaign Manager**: Coordinate promotional calendar so affiliate offers align with broader campaign timing and messaging
- **With Partnership Marketing Manager**: Identify co-marketing opportunities with strategic affiliates that could deepen to formal partnerships
- **With Revenue Operations Manager**: Ensure affiliate attribution data is properly integrated into the revenue reporting stack
- **With Copywriter**: Develop approved email and ad copy templates that affiliates can customize while staying on-brand
- **With Marketing Analyst**: Build affiliate performance dashboards that track EPC, conversion rate, and CAC contribution by partner tier
