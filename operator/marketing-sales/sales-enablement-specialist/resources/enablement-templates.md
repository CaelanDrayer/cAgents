# Sales Enablement Templates

## Sales Playbook Template

```markdown
# Playbook: [Industry/Persona/Use Case]

## Overview
- Target: [Who this is for]
- Use case: [Problem we solve]
- Differentiators: [Why us]

## Discovery Questions
1. [Question about current state]
2. [Question about pain points]
3. [Question about decision process]

## Talk Track
- Opening: [Hook]
- Value prop: [Key message]
- Proof: [Evidence/case study]
- CTA: [Next step]

## Objection Handling
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Too expensive" | [Response] |
| "Not a priority" | [Response] |
| "We have a solution" | [Response] |

## Resources
- Case study: [Link]
- One-pager: [Link]
- Demo: [Link]
```

## Competitive Battlecard

```markdown
# Battlecard: [Competitor]

## Quick Facts
- Positioning: [How they position]
- Pricing: [Model and range]
- Strengths: [What they do well]
- Weaknesses: [Our opportunity]

## Win Against
| When They Say | You Say |
|---------------|---------|
| [Claim] | [Counter] |

## Landmines
- [Feature/capability to highlight]
- [Question to ask that exposes weakness]

## Proof Points
- [Customer win story]
- [Comparison data]
```

## Content Library Structure

### By Stage
- Awareness: Blog posts, infographics
- Consideration: Guides, webinars
- Decision: Case studies, ROI calcs

### By Role
- Champion: Technical content
- Decision Maker: Business case
- End User: Demo, training

## Certification Framework

| Level | Requirements | Assessment |
|-------|--------------|------------|
| **Foundation** | Complete onboarding | Quiz, roleplay |
| **Proficient** | 3 months, 3 deals | Manager eval |
| **Expert** | 6 months, top performer | Peer teach |
