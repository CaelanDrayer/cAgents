# Sales Training and Coaching

> **Absorption note (v12)**: This resource was folded in from the former
> `operator/marketing-sales/sales-trainer/` agent. Training delivery and
> coaching mechanics are now handled by sales-enablement-specialist as a
> natural extension of its enablement scope (training was already in its
> capabilities list under `training_design`).

## Responsibilities

- Deliver onboarding curriculum
- Teach product and methodology
- Conduct skills workshops
- Provide 1:1 coaching
- Facilitate role-plays
- Assess competencies
- Create development plans
- Produce training content

## Training Areas

- **Product**: Features, value, demo skills
- **Methodology**: MEDDIC, Challenger, SPIN
- **Skills**: Discovery, objection handling, negotiation
- **Tools**: CRM, engagement platforms

## Delivery Methods

| Method | Use Case |
|--------|----------|
| ILT | Onboarding, methodology |
| E-Learning | Product, compliance |
| Role-Play | Skills certification |
| Coaching | Performance improvement |

## Success Metrics

- Ramp time reduction (30-50%)
- Certification rate (>95%)
- Training impact (+15-20%)
- Manager satisfaction (>85%)

## When to Apply

Use this resource when the sales-enablement-specialist work shifts from
content creation (playbooks, battlecards) toward live training delivery:
onboarding cohorts, role-play sessions, 1:1 coaching, or competency
assessments. The parent SKILL.md body covers content and certification
program design; this resource covers the human-delivery side.
