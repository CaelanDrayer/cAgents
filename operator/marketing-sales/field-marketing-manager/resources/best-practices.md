# Best Practices: Field Marketing Manager

> Design principles, patterns, and frameworks that guide high-quality regional marketing, territory support, and local pipeline generation work.

## Design Principles

- **Regional Relevance Beats Global Scale**: Local market knowledge — industry concentration, competitive presence, customer references — creates advantages that centralized programs can't replicate
- **Sales Territory Alignment First**: Field marketing only works when programs are explicitly designed to serve defined sales territories and their specific pipeline gaps
- **Local Presence Builds Trust**: In-person community presence — local events, meetups, industry associations — creates relationship density that digital channels cannot
- **Pipeline Coverage as the Mandate**: Field marketing exists to fill pipeline gaps in specific geos — every activity should be evaluated on its contribution to regional pipeline
- **Localize, Don't Recreate**: Adapt centralized content and campaigns for regional relevance rather than building from scratch; standardize what can be standardized
- **Territory Feedback Loops**: Field marketers are the intelligence channel from regional markets back to central marketing; systematize that feedback
- **Measure Locally, Report Globally**: Regional programs need local attribution; roll up to global reporting but maintain granularity at the territory level

## Key Patterns & Frameworks

- **Territory Marketing Plan**: Annual plan developed with the regional sales leader defining target segments, key accounts, program calendar, budget allocation, and pipeline targets for each territory
- **ABM for Regional Markets**: Identifying the top 20–30 target accounts in each territory and deploying account-specific programs (executive roundtables, personalized direct mail, co-sell with local partners)
- **Local Event Formats by Account Stage**: Cold accounts → educational roundtable; warm accounts → executive dinner; late-stage accounts → customer reference site visit or exclusive briefing
- **Regional Partner Co-Marketing**: Activating channel partners in each territory for co-hosted events, joint webinars, and co-branded local advertising
- **Field Marketing Calendar Sync**: Monthly alignment with regional sales manager on upcoming pipeline coverage needs, key account targets, and event attendance priorities
- **Local Sponsorship ROI Filter**: Evaluating regional conference and association sponsorships on audience ICP match, pipeline generation potential, and competitive presence before committing
- **Regional Reference Customer Network**: Maintaining a current map of willing reference customers by territory so sales can quickly access local proof points
- **Direct Mail for ABM Accounts**: Personalized physical mailers to top-tier target accounts in each territory — higher engagement than digital for executive audiences
- **Regional Content Localization**: Adapting case studies, testimonials, and event content to feature local customers and relevant regional industry dynamics
- **Field Marketing ROI Dashboard**: Pipeline sourced by program type, region, and time period — with campaign costs — so leadership can see territorial marketing efficiency

## Domain Concepts & Terminology

### Regional Programs
- **Field Marketing**: Marketing programs designed to generate pipeline and brand presence in specific geographic or vertical markets
- **Regional Event**: Small-format, territory-specific event (executive dinner, workshop, user group) targeting accounts in a defined area
- **Executive Roundtable**: Invitation-only discussion format for VP and C-level prospects around a curated business topic — high-touch, low-funnel event type
- **Roadshow**: Series of the same event format executed sequentially across multiple cities
- **Local Sponsorship**: Investing in regional industry events, professional associations, or community organizations for brand visibility and relationship building

### Territory Alignment
- **Territory**: A defined geographic area or vertical segment assigned to a specific sales rep or team
- **Pipeline Gap**: The difference between current pipeline coverage and the coverage needed to achieve quota — what field marketing is hired to close
- **Named Account List**: The specific companies that a regional sales rep or team is assigned to pursue in their territory
- **Sales-Marketing SLA**: Agreement defining how field marketing programs feed into the sales motion — lead routing, follow-up timing, feedback requirements

### Measurement
- **Regional Pipeline Sourced**: Opportunities created as a result of field marketing programs in a specific territory
- **Field Marketing ROI**: Regional program costs vs. attributed pipeline and closed revenue in the same territory
- **Event Attendance Quality**: Percentage of event attendees who match the ICP and target account list — quantity alone is insufficient
- **Account Penetration Rate**: Percentage of target account list with at least one engaged contact from field marketing activity

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- **Field Marketing Without Territory Alignment**: Running regional programs without explicit alignment to sales territory targets generates pipeline in the wrong segments
- **Over-Indexing on Large Conferences**: Sponsoring expensive industry events without ABM-level follow-through for target accounts wastes budget on logo visibility without pipeline impact
- **No Local Reference Customers**: Prospects often want to speak with peers in their region or industry — lacking local references is a sales friction point that field marketing can systematically address
- **Central Program Without Local Adaptation**: Deploying centralized campaign assets without localization ignores regional industry dynamics, competitive presence, and cultural context
- **Event Attendance Without Capture**: Sponsoring events or attending trade shows without a systematic badge-scan or contact capture process generates no measurable pipeline
- **Field Marketing in a Vacuum**: Operating regional programs without weekly communication with the regional sales manager leads to misaligned priorities and wasted effort
- **Single-Program Dependency**: Relying on one annual event for the majority of regional pipeline creates enormous concentration risk

## Quality Indicators

- **Regional Pipeline Coverage**: Does field marketing-sourced pipeline cover the required multiple of the regional quota?
- **Target Account Engagement Rate**: What percentage of named accounts in each territory have attended a field event or engaged with a regional program?
- **Field Event Attendance Quality**: Percentage of attendees from ICP accounts and target account lists
- **Pipeline-to-Spend Ratio**: Regional pipeline generated per dollar of field marketing budget — measured by territory
- **Sales-Marketing Meeting Cadence**: Is the field marketer meeting weekly with the regional sales lead to align on priorities?
- **Local Reference Customer Pool**: Does every territory have at least 3–5 willing, current reference customers available for sales calls?
- **Program-to-Pipeline Attribution Rate**: What percentage of field-sourced MQLs have explicit attribution to a specific field marketing activity?

## Collaboration Touchpoints

- **With Territory Manager**: Define territory marketing plans together; territory manager identifies the accounts and segments, field marketer designs the program mix and calendar
- **With Events Coordinator**: Field events are execution-heavy; coordinate logistics support while maintaining ownership of audience targeting and messaging strategy
- **With Campaign Manager**: Regional programs should ladder up to centralized campaign themes — align on messaging and timing to reinforce rather than contradict central marketing
- **With Channel Partner Manager**: Local channel partners are field marketing force multipliers — co-hosted events and joint campaigns extend reach without proportional budget increase
- **With Demand Generation Manager**: Field-sourced leads must flow into the right nurture sequences with appropriate regional context — establish lead routing protocols together
