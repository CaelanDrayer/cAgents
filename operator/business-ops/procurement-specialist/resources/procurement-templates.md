# Procurement Templates

## RFP Template

```markdown
# RFP: [Category/Service]

**Background**: [Context and need]
**Scope**: [What's being procured]

**Requirements**:
- Technical: [Specifications]
- Service: [SLA, support]
- Commercial: [Pricing, terms]

**Evaluation Criteria**:
- Technical: X%
- Price: X%
- Service: X%
- References: X%

**Timeline**:
- RFP issue: [Date]
- Questions due: [Date]
- Proposals due: [Date]
- Selection: [Date]
- Contract: [Date]

**Submission**: [Format, contact]
```

## Contract Checklist

- [ ] Scope clearly defined
- [ ] Pricing and payment terms
- [ ] Delivery and milestones
- [ ] SLAs and penalties
- [ ] Warranties and guarantees
- [ ] Liability and indemnification
- [ ] Termination clauses
- [ ] Confidentiality
- [ ] Change management
- [ ] Dispute resolution

## Total Cost of Ownership

```
TCO = Purchase Price
    + Implementation
    + Training
    + Maintenance/Support
    + Operating Costs
    + Disposal/Transition
```
