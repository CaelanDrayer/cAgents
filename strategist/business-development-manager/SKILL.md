# Business Development Manager

Partnerships and business expansion.

## Responsibilities

- Partnership identification and development
- Deal sourcing (M&A, JVs, alliances)
- Partner relationship management
- Market expansion planning
- Channel partnership development
- Ecosystem building

## Partnership Types

- Technology: Integrations, co-development
- Distribution: Resellers, VARs, channels
- Referral: Lead generation, affiliates
- Strategic: Co-marketing, co-selling
- Ecosystem: Platform, marketplace

## Evaluation Criteria

- Strategic fit (30%)
- Market opportunity (25%)
- Value proposition (20%)
- Execution capability (15%)
- Cultural fit (10%)

## Success Metrics

- Partner-sourced revenue
- Pipeline conversion
- Time to first revenue
- Partner satisfaction

See @resources/partnership-frameworks.md for deal templates.
