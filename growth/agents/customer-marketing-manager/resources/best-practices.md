# Best Practices: Customer Marketing Manager

> Design principles, patterns, and frameworks that guide high-quality customer advocacy, expansion, and lifecycle marketing work.

## Design Principles

- **Existing Customers Are Your Best Marketing**: Advocacy programs generate higher-quality leads at lower cost than any paid channel
- **Earn the Ask**: Request testimonials and referrals after delivering genuine value, not at contract signing
- **Segment by Health, Not Just Tier**: Customer marketing programs should differentiate based on engagement and satisfaction, not just contract size
- **Expansion as a Service**: Upsells and cross-sells must feel like the account team finding more ways to help — not a revenue grab
- **Reference Readiness as a Program**: Building a reference pool is an ongoing operational program, not a reactive favor-asking process
- **Customer Voice in Product Feedback**: Customer marketing is a channel for product insights — systematize the loop
- **Celebrate Milestones**: Acknowledging customer achievements (onboarding completion, first value realized, renewal anniversaries) builds loyalty at low cost

## Key Patterns & Frameworks

- **Customer Lifecycle Stages**: Onboarding → Adoption → Value Realization → Renewal → Expansion → Advocacy — each stage requires different marketing content and touchpoints
- **Net Promoter Score (NPS) Action Protocol**: Promoters → immediate advocacy program invitation; Passives → value-add content and check-in; Detractors → customer success escalation, not marketing outreach
- **Case Study Production Process**: Customer identification → internal alignment → customer approval → interview → draft → review cycle → final approval → distribution plan
- **Advocacy Tiering**: Casual advocates (G2 reviews), active advocates (webinar speakers, reference calls), strategic advocates (co-authored content, joint press releases) — match program investment to advocacy commitment
- **Expansion Trigger Events**: Usage spikes, new team members added, new use cases requested, department expansions — trigger-based outreach that feels relevant not random
- **Customer Community Flywheel**: Community drives engagement → engagement generates product feedback → feedback improves product → improved product drives satisfaction → satisfaction drives advocacy → advocacy attracts new customers
- **Lifecycle Email Architecture**: Milestone-based automated emails (first login, first value metric, 30/60/90 days, renewal notice) with branch logic based on health signals
- **Reference Request Management**: Tracking reference availability, preference for reference types, recency of requests, and rotation so no customer is over-used
- **Win-Back Campaign Sequence**: For churned customers, time-gated reactivation sequence featuring new capabilities, case studies, and trial offers
- **Voice of Customer (VoC) Program**: Systematic collection and distribution of customer quotes, feedback, and insights for use in marketing, product, and sales

## Domain Concepts & Terminology

### Customer Advocacy
- **Customer Reference**: A customer willing to speak with prospects on your behalf via phone, email, or in-person
- **Case Study**: A structured narrative documenting a customer's challenge, solution, and measurable results
- **Testimonial**: A short customer quote or endorsement for use in marketing materials
- **User Group / Customer Advisory Board (CAB)**: Structured program bringing customers together for product feedback, peer learning, and brand engagement
- **Co-Marketing**: Joint campaigns or content produced with a customer, benefiting both brands

### Expansion Marketing
- **Upsell**: Selling a higher-tier product or additional seats to an existing customer
- **Cross-sell**: Introducing an existing customer to a complementary product or module
- **Expansion Revenue**: Revenue growth from existing customers beyond their original contract value
- **Net Revenue Retention (NRR)**: Revenue from existing customers (including expansions, minus churn) as a percentage of prior period revenue — the key metric for expansion health
- **Land and Expand**: GTM motion of acquiring customers at a small initial footprint and growing revenue over time through expansion

### Customer Health
- **Health Score**: A composite metric combining product usage, support tickets, NPS, and engagement signals to predict churn or expansion probability
- **Churn Risk**: Signal that a customer is at risk of not renewing their contract
- **Renewal Rate**: Percentage of customers and/or revenue that renews at contract end
- **Time to Value (TTV)**: How quickly a customer achieves their first meaningful outcome from the product — shorter TTV correlates with higher retention
- **Adoption Metrics**: Product usage data showing whether customers are using key features that correlate with renewal and expansion

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- **Spray-and-Pray Advocacy Asks**: Emailing all customers with a generic testimonial request ignores relationship stage and customer health
- **Neglecting Detractors**: Routing NPS detractors to marketing campaigns instead of customer success escalation worsens churn risk
- **Reference Overuse**: Asking the same reference customers repeatedly damages goodwill and eventually loses the reference entirely
- **Case Study Staleness**: Maintaining a library of 3-year-old case studies with outdated metrics undermines sales credibility
- **Treating Expansion as Separate from Retention**: Customers who aren't getting value won't expand; expansion programs must be built on retention health
- **No Attribution for Customer Marketing**: If customer marketing activities don't have a pipeline attribution model, the program budget is perpetually at risk
- **Community Without Curation**: Launching a customer community without dedicated moderation and content generates low engagement and negative sentiment

## Quality Indicators

- **Advocacy Program Participation Rate**: Percentage of eligible customers enrolled in at least one advocacy activity
- **Case Study Pipeline**: Number of approved, current case studies available for sales use by vertical and use case
- **Reference Call Availability**: Can the team fulfill reference requests within 48 hours for any major deal stage?
- **Customer-Sourced Revenue Contribution**: Percentage of new revenue influenced by customer advocacy (referrals, case studies, peer reviews)
- **Net Revenue Retention (NRR)**: The primary metric showing whether customer marketing is protecting and growing the base
- **Expansion Campaign Conversion Rate**: Percentage of customers who respond to expansion-triggered campaigns with qualifying conversations
- **Community Engagement Rate**: Monthly active members as a percentage of total community members

## Collaboration Touchpoints

- **With Customer Success Manager**: Customer health data drives marketing program eligibility and timing; daily collaboration on which customers to activate for advocacy vs. stabilize first
- **With Campaign Manager**: Customer marketing campaigns (expansion, re-engagement, advocacy) must be coordinated with broader campaign calendar and messaging
- **With Product Marketing Manager**: Case studies and customer proof points must align with product messaging priorities; co-develop launch proof points with new customers
- **With Sales Analyst**: Customer expansion signals and health data must feed into pipeline reporting to give sales visibility into expansion opportunities
- **With Copywriter**: Case studies, testimonials, and expansion emails require copy that reflects both the customer's voice and the brand's positioning
