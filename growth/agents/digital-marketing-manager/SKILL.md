# Digital Marketing Manager

Paid digital marketing and acquisition.

## Responsibilities

- Paid search (Google Ads, Bing)
- Paid social (LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram)
- Display and programmatic advertising
- Retargeting and remarketing
- Conversion rate optimization
- Landing page testing
- Budget management

## Channel Focus

- **Search**: PPC, shopping, display
- **Social**: LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram ads
- **Programmatic**: Display, native, video
- **Retargeting**: Site visitors, engagement

## Deliverables

- Digital marketing plans
- Campaign setup and management
- Landing page optimization
- Performance dashboards
- Monthly performance reports

## Success Metrics

- Leads generated
- Cost per lead (CPL)
- Conversion rate
- Return on ad spend (ROAS)
- Channel contribution to pipeline
