# Best Practices: Brand Manager

> Design principles, patterns, and frameworks that guide high-quality brand strategy, identity, and consistency work.

## Design Principles

- **Brand as Promise**: Every touchpoint is a micro-commitment to the audience — consistency builds trust over time
- **Emotional Before Rational**: Brands win on feeling first; features and specs come second
- **Positioning is Subtraction**: A strong brand is defined as much by what it refuses to be as by what it claims to be
- **Voice is Non-Negotiable**: Tone of voice must remain consistent even as visuals evolve across campaigns
- **Category Thinking**: The most effective brands define or redefine their category rather than competing in someone else's
- **Internal Brand First**: Employees must live the brand before customers can feel it
- **Measure Perception, Not Just Performance**: Brand equity metrics matter as much as conversion metrics

## Key Patterns & Frameworks

- **Brand Positioning Statement**: "[Brand] is the [category] that [differentiated benefit] for [target audience] because [reason to believe]" — the single-sentence strategic foundation
- **12 Brand Archetypes (Jung)**: Hero, Outlaw, Explorer, Sage, Innocent, Jester, Creator, Ruler, Caregiver, Everyman, Lover, Magician — choosing a primary and secondary archetype creates personality coherence
- **Brand Pyramid**: From functional attributes at the base through emotional benefits, brand personality, and brand essence at the apex — ensures messaging is grounded in real value
- **Ries & Trout Positioning**: Occupy a distinct position in the prospect's mind by being first, or by repositioning the competition
- **Category Design**: Don't compete — create a new category where you can be the defining brand (e.g., Salesforce and CRM-in-the-cloud)
- **Brand Voice Spectrum**: Define tone across four dimensions (formal↔casual, serious↔playful, respectful↔irreverent, matter-of-fact↔enthusiastic) with examples of on-brand vs. off-brand copy
- **Brand Audit Framework**: Systematic review of all brand touchpoints (digital, physical, verbal, behavioral) to identify consistency gaps
- **Message Architecture**: Hierarchy from master value proposition → pillar messages by segment → proof points — ensures all content is traceable to the core brand idea
- **Competitive Perceptual Map**: Plot your brand and competitors on two axes representing the key buying criteria to identify white space
- **Brand Health Tracking**: NPS, aided/unaided awareness, brand association surveys, and share-of-voice metrics measured on a regular cadence

## Domain Concepts & Terminology

### Brand Identity
- **Brand Identity System**: The collection of visual and verbal elements that make the brand recognizable — logo, color palette, typography, photography style, voice
- **Brand Guidelines / Style Guide**: The authoritative document specifying how brand elements may be used
- **Visual Hierarchy**: The arrangement of design elements to communicate relative importance
- **Brand Architecture**: How a company organizes its portfolio of brands (house of brands, branded house, hybrid)
- **Sub-Brand**: A brand nested within a parent brand, sharing some equity while maintaining distinct identity

### Brand Strategy
- **Positioning**: How a brand occupies a distinct, meaningful place in the target audience's mind relative to alternatives
- **Differentiation**: The specific, defensible attributes that make your brand preferable over alternatives
- **Brand Equity**: The commercial value derived from consumer perception of the brand rather than the product itself
- **Brand Essence**: The single core idea at the heart of the brand (e.g., Nike = authentic athletic performance)
- **Reason to Believe (RTB)**: The substantiating evidence that makes your brand promise credible

### Measurement
- **Brand Awareness (Aided/Unaided)**: Whether target customers can identify the brand with or without prompting
- **Brand Recall**: Ability to retrieve the brand from memory when prompted by a product category
- **Brand Association**: The attributes, feelings, and ideas customers link to your brand
- **Share of Voice (SOV)**: Your brand's presence in media relative to competitors
- **Net Promoter Score (NPS)**: Proxy for brand loyalty and advocacy

### Content & Voice
- **Tone of Voice**: The consistent personality expressed through written and verbal communication
- **Brand Story**: The narrative explaining why the brand exists and what it stands for
- **Tagline**: A short phrase that captures the brand's promise or personality
- **Messaging Pillars**: The 3–5 key themes that all brand communication returns to

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- **Logo-Centric Thinking**: Treating the logo as the brand rather than a symbol of the total brand experience
- **Inconsistent Voice Across Channels**: Using formal language in email but casual language on social destroys personality coherence
- **Feature-Led Positioning**: Positioning on product features rather than outcomes and emotional benefits is easily copied and doesn't build loyalty
- **Over-Extension**: Stretching brand into categories or audiences where the association doesn't hold weakens the core positioning
- **Chasing Competitor Aesthetics**: Refreshing brand to look like the current category leader is a follower move that rarely builds differentiation
- **Neglecting Brand Internally**: Employees who don't understand or believe in the brand will underdeliver on it in every customer interaction
- **Short-Term Campaign Drift**: Running campaigns whose tone or visual style contradicts brand guidelines for a temporary boost erodes brand equity

## Quality Indicators

- **Unaided Brand Recall Rate**: Are target customers mentioning your brand spontaneously in the product category?
- **Brand Consistency Score**: Percentage of sampled touchpoints that meet visual and verbal brand standards
- **Share of Voice Trend**: Is brand presence in earned and paid media growing relative to key competitors?
- **Net Promoter Score Movement**: Is NPS trending up over time as a signal of growing brand advocacy?
- **Message Pull-Through Rate**: What percentage of customer-facing content correctly uses the approved message hierarchy?
- **Brand Association Alignment**: Do customer-cited brand associations match the intended positioning attributes?
- **Employee Brand Understanding**: Can a sample of employees articulate the brand promise and positioning?

## Collaboration Touchpoints

- **With Creative Director**: Brand strategy provides the strategic brief; Creative Director translates it into visual and campaign execution — ensure positioning is not lost in execution
- **With Copywriter**: Brand voice guidelines must be specific enough to produce consistent output; joint review of key copy ensures adherence
- **With Marketing Strategist**: Brand positioning informs channel selection, audience targeting, and message frameworks — align before campaign planning
- **With Product Marketing Manager**: Product messaging must be grounded in overall brand positioning; flag when product claims contradict brand personality
- **With PR Specialist**: Media narratives must align with brand story and positioning; brief PR on key messages before media outreach
