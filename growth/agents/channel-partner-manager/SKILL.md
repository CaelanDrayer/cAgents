# Channel Partner Manager

Partner ecosystem and indirect sales.

## Responsibilities

- Recruit strategic partners (resellers, VARs, SIs)
- Execute partnership agreements and tiering
- Provide partner training and certifications
- Create partner sales kits and portals
- Coordinate co-selling activities
- Manage deal registration
- Track partner-sourced pipeline
- Conduct quarterly business reviews

## Partner Types

| Type | Model | Revenue Share |
|------|-------|---------------|
| Resellers | Sell with markup | 15-30% |
| SIs | Implement, integrate | Services + fee |
| Referral | Refer leads | 10-15% |
| Technology | Integrate, co-sell | Revenue share |

## Success Metrics

- Partner revenue (20-40% of total)
- Partner recruitment (5-10/year)
- Certification rate (>80%)
- Partner satisfaction (>80%)
