# Best Practices: Content Marketing Manager

> Design principles, patterns, and frameworks that guide high-quality content strategy, production, and distribution work.

## Design Principles

- **Audience Utility First**: Create content your audience would seek out, not content you wish they'd find
- **Search and Social Alignment**: Content strategy must account for both algorithmic discovery and human sharing behavior
- **The Flywheel Over the Funnel**: Content builds compounding value — each piece should strengthen the overall authority and drive traffic to other content
- **Editorial Integrity**: Content that feels like advertising repels the audiences it's designed to attract
- **Depth Wins Long-Term**: Comprehensive, authoritative content builds durable traffic; shallow content chases trends and fades
- **Distribution is Half the Job**: Great content that no one sees delivers no value — plan distribution before production
- **Measure Learning, Not Just Performance**: Understanding why a piece performed tells you more than knowing that it did

## Key Patterns & Frameworks

- **Content Marketing Flywheel**: Create → Distribute → Engage → Convert → Retain — each stage feeds the next and compounds over time
- **Topic Cluster Model**: Build a pillar page around a broad topic and support it with cluster pages targeting related subtopics; internal linking signals topical authority to search engines
- **Jobs-to-Be-Done Content Mapping**: Map content types to the specific informational jobs your audience is trying to accomplish (learn, evaluate, implement, troubleshoot)
- **Editorial Calendar Architecture**: 3-month rolling calendar with content types, topics, target keywords, owner, production deadline, and distribution date
- **Content Audit Framework**: Inventory existing content by traffic, engagement, and conversion performance; categorize as optimize, consolidate, redirect, or delete
- **Hero-Hub-Help Model**: Hero (big campaign content), Hub (regular programming), Help (evergreen how-to content) — balances reach, depth, and SEO
- **Distribution Waterfall**: Repurpose each major content asset into multiple formats (blog → social posts → email nurture → slide deck → video clips)
- **SEO Content Brief Template**: Target keyword, search intent, SERP analysis, required sections, word count, internal links, and metadata guidance for writers
- **Content Performance Dashboard**: Organic traffic, engagement rate, scroll depth, email capture rate, and conversion contribution by content piece
- **Gating Decision Framework**: Criteria for when to gate content (high production value, explicit commercial intent) vs. ungated (SEO, awareness) — balancing lead capture vs. reach

## Domain Concepts & Terminology

### Content Strategy
- **Content Strategy**: The plan governing what content to create, for whom, why, and how to manage it over time
- **Editorial Mission Statement**: One-sentence definition of who the content is for, what it covers, and what outcome it creates
- **Content ICP**: The specific sub-segments of the target audience that content should address — goes beyond marketing personas
- **Content Velocity**: The rate at which new content is published — must balance quality and frequency
- **Evergreen Content**: Content that remains accurate and valuable over time without frequent updates

### SEO Integration
- **Target Keyword**: The primary search term a piece of content is optimized to rank for
- **Search Intent**: The underlying goal behind a query (informational, navigational, transactional, commercial)
- **Topical Authority**: The degree to which a website is recognized by search engines as an expert in a subject area
- **Internal Linking**: Strategic links between related content pieces to distribute authority and improve discovery
- **Content Gap**: A topic or keyword where competitors have coverage but you don't — an opportunity

### Distribution & Amplification
- **Content Syndication**: Publishing content on third-party platforms to reach additional audiences
- **Email Nurture Integration**: Mapping content to nurture email sequences so the right piece reaches the right prospect at the right stage
- **Social Amplification**: Using social channels to distribute content organically and via paid promotion
- **Content Repurposing**: Adapting a single piece of content into multiple formats for different channels and audiences

### Measurement
- **Organic Traffic**: Visits from unpaid search results — a primary content marketing metric
- **Engagement Rate**: Average time on page, scroll depth, and interactions relative to visits
- **Content-Attributed Pipeline**: Opportunities where prospects consumed specific content before or during the sales process
- **Domain Authority (DA)**: A third-party metric estimating a website's search ranking strength

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- **Keyword Stuffing Without Strategy**: Producing content around keywords without matching the actual search intent leads to high bounce rates and poor rankings
- **Content Without Distribution Plan**: Publishing and hoping is not a strategy; distribution must be planned before production begins
- **Quantity Over Quality**: Publishing frequently with shallow content trains your audience to ignore you and signals low authority to search engines
- **Siloed Content and Sales**: Content that sales reps don't use and don't share is wasted; co-create with sales around real buyer questions
- **No Content Audit Practice**: Letting old content accumulate without pruning dilutes topical authority and burdens site crawl budgets
- **Vanity Metrics Reporting**: Reporting page views without conversion contribution gives leadership no visibility into content's commercial value
- **Ignoring Content Decay**: Evergreen content degrades over time without updates; monitor traffic trends and refresh before rankings drop

## Quality Indicators

- **Organic Traffic Growth**: Is content-driven organic traffic growing month-over-month?
- **Keyword Ranking Coverage**: How many target keywords rank in the top 10 vs. top 3 positions?
- **Content-Attributed Pipeline**: What dollar value of pipeline has engaged with content before or during the sales cycle?
- **Email Capture Rate**: What percentage of content visitors convert to subscribers (benchmark: 1–3% for ungated content)?
- **Average Engagement Depth**: Are visitors scrolling past 50% of article length, indicating real consumption?
- **Content Reuse Rate**: What percentage of produced content is being actively used by sales, demand gen, or social teams?
- **Content Production Efficiency**: Average days from brief to published, indicating process health

## Collaboration Touchpoints

- **With SEO Specialist**: Content strategy must be informed by keyword research, search intent analysis, and technical SEO recommendations — weekly alignment keeps production on track
- **With Copywriter**: Provide detailed content briefs with keyword, audience, angle, and outline; review drafts for brand voice and strategic accuracy before publishing
- **With Demand Generation Manager**: Ensure content assets feed into nurture sequences and gated offers that generate MQLs from organic traffic
- **With Marketing Analyst**: Build content performance reports together and use data to prioritize next-quarter topics
- **With Social Media Manager**: Coordinate content publishing calendar with social distribution calendar so every piece gets amplified at launch
