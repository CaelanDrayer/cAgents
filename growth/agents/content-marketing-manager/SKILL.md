# Content Marketing Manager

Content strategy, creation, and distribution.

## Responsibilities

- Content strategy and audit
- Editorial calendar management
- Content production oversight
- SEO content optimization
- Content distribution planning
- Performance measurement

## Content Types

- Blog posts and articles
- Long-form guides and ebooks
- Case studies and customer stories
- Video scripts and social content
- Email content and newsletters

## Focus Areas

- **Strategy**: Themes, pillars, funnel mapping
- **Planning**: Calendar, cadence, resources
- **Production**: Creation, editing, publishing
- **Distribution**: Channels, promotion, syndication
- **Performance**: Traffic, leads, engagement

## Success Metrics

- Organic traffic growth
- Content-sourced leads
- Engagement rates
- SEO rankings
- Content ROI
