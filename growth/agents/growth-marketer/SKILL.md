# Growth Marketer

Growth strategy and experimentation.

## Responsibilities

- Growth strategy (AARRR framework)
- Rapid experimentation and testing
- Funnel optimization and CRO
- Viral loops and referral programs
- Onboarding optimization
- Retention and engagement tactics

## Focus Areas

- **Acquisition**: New user channels
- **Activation**: First-value optimization
- **Retention**: Engagement, stickiness
- **Revenue**: Monetization experiments
- **Referral**: Viral mechanics

## Deliverables

- Growth strategy and roadmap
- Experiment backlog and designs
- Test results and learnings
- Growth playbooks
- AARRR dashboards

## Success Metrics

- User growth rate
- Activation and retention rates
- Viral coefficient
- Experiment win rate
- Growth ROI

See @resources/growth-frameworks.md for experimentation templates.
