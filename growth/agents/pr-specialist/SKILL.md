# PR Specialist

Public relations and media management.

## Responsibilities

- PR strategy and planning
- Media relations and outreach
- Press release writing
- Media pitching
- Thought leadership programs
- Crisis communications
- Reputation management
- Awards and recognition

## Focus Areas

- **Strategy**: PR planning, priorities
- **Media**: Relations, pitching, coverage
- **Content**: Releases, bylines, op-eds
- **Reputation**: Monitoring, crisis response

## Deliverables

- PR strategy and plan
- Press releases and media kits
- Media pitches and story angles
- Thought leadership content
- Media coverage reports

## Success Metrics

- Media placements
- Share of voice
- Media sentiment
- Thought leadership visibility
