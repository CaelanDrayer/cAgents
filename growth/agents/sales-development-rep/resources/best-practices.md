# Best Practices: Sales Development Rep

> Design principles, patterns, and frameworks that guide high-quality outbound prospecting, cold outreach, and top-of-funnel pipeline generation work.

## Design Principles

- **Personalization is Not Optional**: Generic cold outreach generates sub-1% reply rates; relevant, researched messages generate 5–15% — the investment in personalization pays
- **Multi-Channel, Multi-Touch Cadences Win**: Relying on email alone misses prospects who respond better to phone or LinkedIn; structured multi-channel cadences increase connection rates
- **Research Before Outreach**: 10 minutes of research on a prospect's company, role, and likely pain points converts a cold message into a warm one
- **Volume Enables Pattern Recognition**: High call and email volumes generate the data needed to identify what works; low-volume SDRs can't distinguish signal from noise
- **Qualification Saves Everyone's Time**: Booking low-quality meetings to hit a metric quota wastes AE time and trains buyers that your meetings aren't worth attending
- **Rejection is Data**: "Not interested" responses reveal objection patterns that improve future messaging when analyzed systematically
- **Persistence with Dignity**: Following up 6–10 times is necessary and professional; following up 20 times without a response is harassment

## Key Patterns & Frameworks

- **ICP-Driven Prospecting**: Prospecting list = companies matching ICP firmographics (size, industry, revenue, technology stack) × contacts matching buyer persona (title, function, seniority) — quality list is the foundation
- **10-Touch Multi-Channel Cadence**: Day 1 (email #1 + LinkedIn connect) → Day 3 (call + voicemail) → Day 5 (email #2) → Day 7 (LinkedIn message) → Day 9 (call) → Day 11 (email #3 + value asset) → Day 14 (call) → Day 17 (email #4) → Day 21 (breakup email) — spread across email, phone, and social
- **BASHO Email Framework**: Personalization hook (specific company or role insight) → connect to challenge → credibility statement → CTA (one low-friction ask) — the anatomy of a high-converting cold email
- **Permission-Based Cold Calling Opener**: "I know this is a cold call — would you be willing to give me 27 seconds to tell you why I'm calling?" — disarming, respectful, and higher-converting than fake familiarity
- **Trigger Event Prospecting**: Monitoring for funding rounds, executive hires, company expansions, product launches, and job postings as signals of active spending and change — timing outreach to relevant moments increases relevance
- **Voicemail Formula**: Name + company + value hook + callback number (twice) + email follow-up notice — all under 20 seconds
- **Email Subject Line A/B Testing**: Running split tests on subject line formulas (question vs. statement, specific vs. curiosity-gap, personalized vs. generic) to identify highest-open-rate approaches for each segment
- **CRM Call Logging Protocol**: Log outcome (connected, voicemail, no answer, wrong person), next step, and key insight from every call within 30 minutes — prevents data decay
- **Objection Flip Technique**: Acknowledge → Reframe → Ask — "Totally understand. Many of the [role]s I talk to felt the same way before they saw [specific result]. Would you be open to 15 minutes to see if it applies here?"
- **Monthly Cadence Retrospective**: Analyzing reply rate, meeting booked rate, and disqualification rate by segment, message, and channel — identifies what to iterate on next month

## Domain Concepts & Terminology

### Prospecting
- **Outbound Prospecting**: Proactively reaching out to potential buyers who haven't expressed interest — the SDR's primary motion
- **Cold Outreach**: First contact with a prospect who has had no prior relationship with the company
- **Warm Outreach**: First contact where there's a shared connection, trigger event, or prior company touch (ad engagement, content download)
- **Cadence**: A structured, multi-touch outreach sequence with defined channels, message types, and timing for each touch
- **Personalization Hook**: A specific, researched detail about the prospect's company, role, or situation that signals the message isn't templated

### Qualification
- **BANT**: Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline — basic qualification criteria to assess whether a prospect is a viable opportunity
- **ICP Match**: The degree to which a prospect company matches the firmographic profile of your best customers
- **Discovery**: The process of uncovering a prospect's pain, priorities, and decision-making process — typically happens in an initial meeting booked by the SDR
- **Meeting Quality**: Whether the booked meeting converts to a next step in the AE's pipeline — the SDR's primary quality metric
- **SQL (Sales Qualified Lead)**: A prospect who has met minimum qualification criteria and is ready for deeper sales engagement

### Metrics
- **Connect Rate**: Percentage of dials that result in a live conversation — below 5% signals list quality or timing issues
- **Reply Rate**: Percentage of cold emails that receive a response (positive or negative) — benchmark: 3–8% for well-personalized sequences
- **Meeting Booked Rate**: Percentage of prospects in a cadence who book a meeting — benchmark: 1–3% for cold outbound
- **Meeting Held Rate**: Percentage of booked meetings where the prospect actually shows up — below 70% indicates qualification issues
- **Pipeline Sourced**: Total dollar value of opportunities created from SDR-booked meetings

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- **Template Blasting Without Personalization**: Sending identical emails to hundreds of prospects generates sub-1% response rates and accelerates unsubscribes
- **Booking Unqualified Meetings**: Booking meetings with prospects who clearly don't fit the ICP wastes AE time, degrades SDR credibility, and pollutes the pipeline
- **Giving Up After 2 Touches**: Research consistently shows most responses come after touch 4–7; abandoning cadences early means abandoning the majority of your addressable market
- **Feature-Led Opening**: Leading with product features instead of the buyer's likely problem signals the call is about you, not about them
- **No Call Objective**: Picking up the phone without a specific, answerable objective ("my goal is to book a 20-minute discovery call with the VP of Sales") leads to unfocused conversations
- **Ignoring LinkedIn as a Channel**: Email-only outreach misses decision-makers who are highly active on LinkedIn and unresponsive to email
- **No Sequence Iteration**: Running the same cadence for 6 months without testing alternative messages or sequences prevents the learning that improves performance

## Quality Indicators

- **Meeting Booked Rate**: The primary SDR performance metric — percentage of prospects entering a cadence who book a qualified meeting
- **Meeting Quality Rate**: What percentage of SDR-booked meetings convert to active pipeline opportunities at the AE level?
- **Activity Volume per Day**: Calls made, emails sent, LinkedIn touches — the inputs that drive the output metrics
- **Connect Rate Trend**: Is the percentage of dials resulting in conversations stable, improving, or declining?
- **ICP Match Rate**: Percentage of booked meetings where the prospect meets ICP criteria — a quality control metric
- **Sequence Reply Rate**: Overall positive reply rate across all cadence touchpoints — improving trend indicates message optimization is working
- **Pipeline Source Attribution**: Dollar value of open pipeline attributable to SDR-sourced meetings

## Collaboration Touchpoints

- **With Account Executive**: The SDR-AE relationship determines meeting quality; debrief together weekly on which discovery conversations are converting and why; AE feedback improves SDR qualification
- **With Demand Generation Manager**: SDRs should follow up on inbound leads from marketing programs and provide feedback on lead quality to improve targeting
- **With Sales Enablement Specialist**: Objection handling scripts, competitive positioning, and cadence templates should come from enablement — flag what's working and what's outdated in the field
- **With Marketing Analyst**: SDR outreach response rates and meeting-to-pipeline conversion by segment provide important signal for marketing campaign targeting refinement
- **With Sales Ops Specialist**: CRM workflows, sequence tools (Outreach, Salesloft), and activity tracking dashboards are built by ops — escalate friction in tools that slows outreach velocity
