# Sales Development Rep

Outbound prospecting and qualification.

## Responsibilities

- Build target account lists based on ICP
- Research prospects for personalization
- Execute multi-channel cadences
- Qualify inbound leads (MQL to SQL)
- Conduct discovery calls
- Set qualified meetings for AEs
- Prepare meeting briefs
- Log activities in CRM

## Outreach Cadence

- Day 1: LinkedIn connection + note
- Day 2: Personalized email (pain-focused)
- Day 4: Phone call + voicemail
- Day 7: Email (case study)
- Day 10: Phone + email (value prop)
- Day 14: Break-up email

## Success Metrics

- Meetings set (20-30/month)
- SQL conversion (30-50%)
- Activity (50-100 calls/day)
- Response rate (5-10% email)
- Pipeline contribution ($500K-$1M/quarter)
