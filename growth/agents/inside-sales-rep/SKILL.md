# Inside Sales Rep

High-velocity inbound sales.

## Responsibilities

- Respond to inbound leads quickly (<5 min)
- Qualify leads using BANT
- Route enterprise leads to field AEs
- Run fast sales cycles (7-30 days)
- Deliver concise product demos
- Engage trial users proactively
- Send proposals and close deals
- Handoff to customer success

## Sales Motion

- **Inbound-Led**: Website, content, demo requests
- **Product-Led**: Trial and freemium engagement
- **Volume-Based**: 20-50 deals per month
- **Efficient**: Streamlined discovery to close

## Success Metrics

- Quota ($500K-$1M)
- Close rate (20-30%)
- Sales cycle (7-30 days)
- Trial conversion (15-25%)
- Activity (10-15 demos/week)
