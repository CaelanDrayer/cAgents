# Best Practices: Inside Sales Rep

> Design principles, patterns, and frameworks that guide high-quality inbound sales qualification, outbound prospecting, and high-velocity deal management work.

## Design Principles

- **Speed to Lead is a Competitive Differentiator**: Research shows lead conversion probability drops by 10× after the first 5 minutes — response time is a primary performance lever
- **Qualify Relentlessly**: Time spent on prospects who will never buy is time stolen from those who will — qualify early and often
- **Personalization at Scale**: Templated outreach with tailored personalization signals (company news, relevant trigger, shared connection) outperforms both pure templates and fully custom emails
- **Activity Drives Pipeline**: In high-velocity sales, consistent daily activity (calls, emails, LinkedIn touches) is the primary controllable variable — track and protect it
- **Objection Handling is a Skill**: Common objections are predictable and should be prepared for; improvisational objection responses underperform prepared ones
- **CRM Discipline is Self-Interest**: Accurate CRM records make you better at your job — they're not administrative overhead, they're your memory and your pipeline visibility
- **Learn from Every Conversation**: Each call reveals information about the buyer, the market, and your pitch — capture and apply those learnings

## Key Patterns & Frameworks

- **SPIN Selling for Discovery**: Situation questions (current state) → Problem questions (pain exploration) → Implication questions (consequences of the pain) → Need-Payoff questions (value of solving it) — structures conversations to build buyer conviction
- **BANT Qualification**: Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline — rapid first-call qualification framework to identify low-probability prospects early
- **Inbound Lead Response Sequence**: Immediate email (within 5 minutes of form submission) → First call attempt (within 10 minutes) → Second call + voicemail (same day) → Follow-up email (Day 2) → Third call + email (Day 4) → Final attempt (Day 7)
- **Outbound Cadence (10-Touch)**: Day 1 (email) → Day 2 (LinkedIn connect) → Day 3 (call) → Day 5 (email + value) → Day 7 (call) → Day 9 (LinkedIn message) → Day 11 (email) → Day 14 (call + voicemail) → Day 17 (email) → Day 21 (breakup email)
- **Pre-Call Research Ritual**: 5-minute minimum research on company news, prospect LinkedIn, and CRM history before any outbound call — personalization requires preparation
- **Trial Closing Questions**: "Based on what we've discussed, does this solve the problem you're trying to solve?" — used to test buying readiness without formal closing pressure
- **Common Objection Playbook**: Prepared responses for the top 5–7 objections (price, timing, current solution, decision authority, competition) reviewed and refined regularly
- **CRM Update Protocol**: Update opportunity stage, next steps, and last-touch date within 1 hour of every significant prospect interaction
- **Voicemail Formula**: State name and company → one-sentence value hook relevant to their role → clear callback number (twice) → promise of a follow-up email — under 20 seconds
- **Email P.S. Line**: The postscript is the most-read element of business emails after the subject line; use it for the key value statement or social proof

## Domain Concepts & Terminology

### Sales Velocity
- **Sales Velocity**: Revenue generated per day = (# Opportunities × Average Deal Size × Win Rate) ÷ Sales Cycle Length — increase any input to increase velocity
- **Response Time SLA**: The agreed maximum time between lead assignment and first outreach — typically 5 minutes for hot inbound leads
- **Touches per Lead**: The number of outreach attempts made before a lead is marked disqualified or moved to long-term nurture
- **Conversion Rate by Stage**: Close rate from MQL to SAL, SAL to demo, demo to proposal, proposal to close — diagnosing which stage to optimize
- **Quota Attainment**: Percentage of revenue target achieved — the primary performance metric

### Qualification
- **BANT**: Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline — basic qualification filter for rapid lead triage
- **MEDDIC**: Metrics, Economic Buyer, Decision Criteria, Decision Process, Identify Pain, Champion — deeper qualification for higher-value deals
- **ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)**: The firmographic and behavioral attributes of your highest-converting, highest-retaining customer segments
- **Disqualification Criteria**: Explicit conditions under which a lead should be marked not-a-fit and routed to marketing for nurture
- **Champion**: The internal advocate who believes in your solution and will navigate the buying process on your behalf

### Communication
- **Cadence**: The structured sequence of multi-channel touchpoints used for outbound prospecting
- **Personalization Hook**: The specific, researched detail in an outreach message that signals you know something about the prospect's situation
- **Objection**: A stated reason why the prospect cannot or will not move forward — to be distinguished from a question (information gap)
- **Discovery Call**: The structured conversation to understand the prospect's pain, desired outcomes, current approach, and buying process
- **Demo**: Product demonstration tailored to the prospect's specific pain points — should show only what's relevant

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- **Slow Lead Response**: Every minute of delay after a lead submits a form reduces the probability of connection — slow response is a primary conversion killer
- **Generic Outreach**: Templates with no personalization signal that you didn't research the prospect and generate immediate credibility loss
- **Pitching Before Discovering**: Jumping to product features before understanding the prospect's pain leads to irrelevant demos that don't convert
- **Giving Up After 2 Touches**: Research shows most connections require 6–10 touches; abandoning cadences early means missing prospects who would have converted
- **Ignoring CRM Updates**: Missing next steps, stale close dates, and inaccurate pipeline stages make your manager's forecast unreliable and your own performance invisible
- **Handling Objections with Tactics Instead of Insight**: Using canned objection deflections instead of genuinely engaging with the prospect's concern erodes trust
- **No Post-Call Analysis**: Finishing a call without noting what worked, what questions stalled, and what the prospect cares about prevents learning accumulation

## Quality Indicators

- **Lead Response Time**: Average minutes from lead assignment to first outreach — below 5 minutes for hot inbound
- **Connect Rate**: Percentage of dials that result in a conversation — below 5% suggests list or timing issues
- **Discovery-to-Demo Conversion Rate**: Percentage of discovery calls that schedule a product demo — below 30% suggests qualification or pitch effectiveness issues
- **Demo-to-Proposal Rate**: Percentage of demos that advance to formal proposal — measures demo quality and qualification depth
- **Cadence Completion Rate**: Percentage of leads receiving the full outreach cadence before disqualification — measures follow-through discipline
- **Daily Activity Tracking**: Calls made, emails sent, LinkedIn touches, connects, and meetings booked per day — the leading indicators of pipeline
- **Win Rate**: Percentage of opportunities closed as won — primary lagging indicator of selling effectiveness

## Collaboration Touchpoints

- **With Account Executive**: Hand off qualified opportunities with full discovery context, key stakeholder map, and competitive dynamics — a poor handoff loses the work done in qualification
- **With Sales Development Rep**: For inbound-only inside sales roles, SDRs may provide outbound-sourced leads; maintain clear qualification criteria and rejection feedback to improve SDR targeting
- **With Demand Generation Manager**: Provide weekly feedback on lead quality, common disqualification reasons, and messaging that resonates — this data improves future campaign targeting
- **With Sales Enablement Specialist**: Request updated objection handling playbooks, competitive battle cards, and discovery question guides — use them, then provide feedback on what's working
- **With Sales Ops Specialist**: CRM workflows, lead routing rules, and performance dashboards are built by ops — flag any friction in the tools that slows the sales motion
