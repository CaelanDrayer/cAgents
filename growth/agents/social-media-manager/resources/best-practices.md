# Best Practices: Social Media Manager

> Design principles, patterns, and frameworks that guide high-quality social media strategy, community management, and content performance work.

## Design Principles

- **Platform Fit Before Content Volume**: Different platforms serve different audiences and communication styles — strategy should start with where the audience actually is
- **Community Before Broadcasting**: Social media is a conversation channel, not an additional broadcast medium — listening and responding are as important as publishing
- **Consistency Beats Perfection**: Publishing reliably at a sustainable cadence builds audience trust more than sporadic high-quality bursts
- **Organic Reach is Earned**: Algorithms favor content that generates genuine engagement — build for real response, not metric gaming
- **Brand Voice Must Be Human**: Social content that sounds like a press release fails on every platform; the voice should feel like a person, not a corporation
- **Real-Time Responsiveness is Expected**: Audiences expect brands to respond to comments, DMs, and mentions within hours, not days
- **Data Guides Content Mix, Instinct Guides Voice**: Use performance data to optimize format, timing, and topic mix; use brand intuition to maintain the voice and personality within that mix

## Key Patterns & Frameworks

- **Platform Strategy Matrix**: For each platform (LinkedIn, Instagram, X/Twitter, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook) — audience profile, content format preferences, optimal posting frequency, primary brand objective — determines where to invest and how
- **Content Calendar Architecture**: Monthly themes → weekly content mix (educational, promotional, social proof, entertaining, engaging) → daily posting schedule with platform-specific variants
- **3-2-1 Content Ratio**: 3 educational/value posts : 2 social proof/storytelling posts : 1 direct promotional post — prevents social feed from feeling like an ad channel
- **Community Management Response Framework**: Tier 1 (positive comments → acknowledge and engage), Tier 2 (questions → answer helpfully with brand voice), Tier 3 (complaints → acknowledge publicly + move to DM), Tier 4 (trolls/abuse → hide/report with no engagement)
- **Hashtag Research and Governance**: Mix branded hashtags (owned), community hashtags (niche, high-engagement), and discovery hashtags (broad reach) — monitor performance and rotate non-performing tags
- **Social Listening Protocol**: Daily monitoring for brand mentions (tagged and untagged) + competitor mentions + category keywords → weekly report to relevant teams → crisis alert triggers
- **User-Generated Content (UGC) Program**: Incentivizing and systematically collecting customer content → moderation review → permission request → repurposing in brand channels — builds authenticity and content volume
- **Social Proof Amplification**: Case studies, customer quotes, reviews, and testimonials formatted for each social platform's visual and text constraints
- **Crisis Response Playbook**: Pre-approved response templates by crisis type, escalation criteria, spokesperson designation, and silence protocol (when not to engage)
- **Social Analytics Reporting Stack**: Reach and impressions, engagement rate, follower growth, link clicks, social listening sentiment, and share of voice — measured weekly with month-over-month trend

## Domain Concepts & Terminology

### Platform-Specific
- **Organic Reach**: The number of unique users who see a post without paid promotion — declining on most platforms due to algorithmic feed filters
- **Algorithm**: The platform's content ranking system — prioritizes content based on engagement signals, relationship strength, and content type preferences
- **Engagement Rate**: (Likes + Comments + Shares + Saves) ÷ Impressions — measures content resonance relative to reach
- **Impressions**: Total number of times content was displayed, including multiple views by the same user
- **Reach**: The number of unique users who saw the content at least once
- **Share of Voice**: Your brand's social media mentions as a percentage of all brand mentions in your category

### Content Types
- **Organic Post**: Non-paid content published to a brand's social media profile
- **UGC (User-Generated Content)**: Content created by customers, fans, or followers featuring your brand
- **Social Proof**: Social media content featuring customer testimonials, reviews, and success stories
- **Thought Leadership Post**: Long-form or detailed content sharing expertise, opinion, or insight on an industry topic
- **Carousel**: A multi-image or multi-slide post format available on Instagram and LinkedIn — higher engagement rate than single images for educational content

### Community Management
- **Brand Mention**: Any reference to your brand on social media, whether tagged or untagged
- **Social Listening**: Monitoring social media for brand mentions, competitor mentions, and industry conversations
- **Community Management**: Actively engaging with audience comments, messages, and mentions to build relationships
- **Sentiment Analysis**: Classifying social mentions as positive, neutral, or negative to track brand perception trends
- **Crisis Monitoring**: Tracking escalating negative mentions or viral complaints before they reach critical mass

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- **Every Platform, Thin Everywhere**: Creating accounts on every platform without the resources to maintain consistent quality leads to abandoned accounts that damage brand perception
- **Content Calendar Without Content Strategy**: A calendar full of posting dates is not a strategy; without audience insight and content purpose, the calendar just creates pressure to publish without direction
- **Ignoring Comments and DMs**: A brand that publishes without responding signals it doesn't care about its community — response rate and time are brand signals
- **Buying Followers**: Fake followers inflate vanity metrics while destroying engagement rate calculations and providing zero business value
- **Promotional Overload**: A social feed that's more than 20–30% promotional content trains audiences to tune out and decline to follow
- **No Crisis Protocol**: Being unprepared for a viral complaint or PR crisis on social media means the first response is improvised under pressure — the worst conditions for strategic communication
- **Chasing Every Trend**: Forcing the brand into every meme format or viral moment dilutes brand identity; only engage trends that authentically fit the brand voice

## Quality Indicators

- **Engagement Rate by Platform**: Compare against platform-specific benchmarks — LinkedIn (1–3%), Instagram (2–5%), X/Twitter (<1%) — significant underperformance signals content resonance issues
- **Follower Growth Rate**: Month-over-month organic follower growth — accelerating growth indicates content is driving discoverability
- **Response Rate and Time**: Percentage of comments and DMs responded to, and average response time — community management quality metric
- **Share of Voice Trend**: Is your brand's social presence growing relative to competitors?
- **Link Click Rate**: Percentage of social impressions that generate clicks to owned content or conversion destinations
- **Social Listening Sentiment Score**: Ratio of positive to negative mentions — trend matters more than absolute score
- **UGC Volume**: Number of pieces of user-generated content discovered per month — signal of community brand affinity

## Collaboration Touchpoints

- **With Campaign Manager**: Social content calendar must align with broader campaign timing and messaging; receive campaign briefs before content planning begins to ensure social supports rather than contradicts campaign objectives
- **With Copywriter**: Social-specific copy adaptations require understanding of platform voice conventions and character limits; provide copy brief with platform context and tone direction
- **With Influencer Marketing Specialist**: Brand-owned social channels and influencer channels should coordinate amplification strategies — influencer post timing and brand social posts should reinforce each other
- **With Brand Manager**: Brand voice guidelines must be specific enough to produce consistent social content across multiple social writers; escalate any edge cases where trending content format conflicts with brand standards
- **With PR Specialist**: Crisis communications must be coordinated between earned media (PR) and owned social (social manager) — unified messages and timing prevent contradictory public statements
