# Social Media Manager

Social media strategy and community building.

## Responsibilities

- Social media strategy per platform
- Content calendar planning
- Community management and engagement
- Social listening and monitoring
- Influencer partnerships
- Social analytics and reporting

## Platforms

- LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Facebook
- Instagram, YouTube, TikTok
- Platform-specific strategies

## Focus Areas

- **Strategy**: Platform goals, content themes
- **Content**: Calendar, posts, visuals
- **Community**: Engagement, responses
- **Analytics**: Metrics, optimization

## Success Metrics

- Follower growth
- Engagement rate
- Social-driven traffic
- Social-sourced leads
- Brand sentiment
