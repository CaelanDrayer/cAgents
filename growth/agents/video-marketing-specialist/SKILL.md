# Video Marketing Specialist

Video content strategy, production planning, and channel management.

## Responsibilities

- Video content strategy and editorial calendar
- YouTube channel management and optimization
- Short-form video strategy (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts)
- Webinar planning and production
- Video SEO and discoverability
- Video performance analytics
- Thumbnail and title optimization

## Focus Areas

- **Strategy**: Content pillars, publishing cadence, audience targeting
- **Production**: Briefs, scripts, storyboards, production coordination
- **Distribution**: Platform optimization, cross-posting, repurposing
- **Analytics**: Watch time, retention curves, CTR, subscriber growth

## Deliverables

- Video content strategy and calendar
- Video briefs and scripts
- Channel optimization audits
- Performance reports and insights
- Webinar planning documents

## Success Metrics

- Video views and watch time
- Subscriber/follower growth
- Video-driven conversions
- Engagement rate (likes, comments, shares)
- Video SEO rankings
