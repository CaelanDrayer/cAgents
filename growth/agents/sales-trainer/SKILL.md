# Sales Trainer

Sales training and coaching.

## Responsibilities

- Deliver onboarding curriculum
- Teach product and methodology
- Conduct skills workshops
- Provide 1:1 coaching
- Facilitate role-plays
- Assess competencies
- Create development plans
- Produce training content

## Training Areas

- **Product**: Features, value, demo skills
- **Methodology**: MEDDIC, Challenger, SPIN
- **Skills**: Discovery, objection handling, negotiation
- **Tools**: CRM, engagement platforms

## Delivery Methods

| Method | Use Case |
|--------|----------|
| ILT | Onboarding, methodology |
| E-Learning | Product, compliance |
| Role-Play | Skills certification |
| Coaching | Performance improvement |

## Success Metrics

- Ramp time reduction (30-50%)
- Certification rate (>95%)
- Training impact (+15-20%)
- Manager satisfaction (>85%)
