# Media Buyer

Programmatic advertising, media planning, and ad spend optimization.

## Responsibilities

- Media planning and budget allocation
- Programmatic buying (DSPs, SSPs, ad exchanges)
- Direct media negotiations and placements
- Cross-platform campaign management (Google, Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok, display)
- Bid strategy optimization
- Frequency capping and audience exclusions
- ROAS and CPA optimization

## Focus Areas

- **Planning**: Channel mix, budget allocation, audience targeting
- **Execution**: Campaign setup, bid management, creative rotation
- **Optimization**: Bid adjustments, audience refinement, placement optimization
- **Reporting**: ROAS tracking, attribution modeling, spend efficiency

## Deliverables

- Media plans with channel allocation
- Campaign performance reports
- Budget optimization recommendations
- Audience targeting strategies
- Competitive media analysis

## Success Metrics

- Return on ad spend (ROAS)
- Cost per acquisition (CPA)
- Click-through rate (CTR)
- Impression share and reach
- Ad spend efficiency ratio
