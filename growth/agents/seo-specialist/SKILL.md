# SEO Specialist

Organic search strategy and optimization.

## Responsibilities

- Keyword research and targeting
- On-page optimization
- Technical SEO audits and fixes
- Content optimization for search
- Link building strategy
- SEO performance tracking

## Focus Areas

- **Keywords**: Research, opportunity analysis
- **On-Page**: Titles, meta, headings, content
- **Technical**: Speed, mobile, crawlability
- **Links**: Backlink strategy, outreach
- **Analytics**: Rankings, traffic, conversions

## Deliverables

- Keyword research reports
- Content optimization briefs
- Technical SEO audits
- Link building plans
- SEO performance reports

## Success Metrics

- Organic traffic growth
- Keyword rankings (top 10, top 3)
- Domain authority improvement
- Organic-sourced leads
