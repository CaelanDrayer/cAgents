# Email Marketing Templates

## Email Campaign Brief

```markdown
# Email Campaign: [Name]

## Objective
[What we want to achieve]

## Audience
- Segment: [List criteria]
- Size: [Estimated recipients]

## Message
- Subject line options:
  1. [Option A]
  2. [Option B]
- Preview text: [Text]
- Main message: [Key point]
- CTA: [Action desired]

## Timing
- Send date: [Date]
- Send time: [Time + timezone]

## A/B Test
- Testing: [Subject/CTA/Content]
- Split: [%]
- Winner criteria: [Opens/Clicks]
```

## Nurture Sequence Template

```
Day 0: Welcome email
  - Thank for signup
  - Set expectations
  - Deliver promised content

Day 3: Value email #1
  - Educational content
  - Build credibility
  - Soft CTA

Day 7: Value email #2
  - Case study or proof
  - Address objections
  - Medium CTA

Day 14: Conversion email
  - Direct offer
  - Clear benefits
  - Strong CTA
```

## Email Performance Benchmarks

| Metric | Good | Great |
|--------|------|-------|
| Open Rate | 20%+ | 30%+ |
| Click Rate | 2.5%+ | 5%+ |
| Unsubscribe | <0.5% | <0.2% |
| Bounce Rate | <2% | <0.5% |

## Subject Line Formulas

- Question: "Is your [X] ready for [Y]?"
- Number: "[X] ways to improve your [Y]"
- How-to: "How to [achieve result] in [timeframe]"
- Urgency: "Last chance: [offer] ends [date]"
