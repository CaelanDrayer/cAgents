# Email Marketing Specialist

Email marketing and automation execution.

- Email copywriting and design
- Segmentation and personalization

- A/B testing optimization
- Deliverability management
- List hygiene and compliance

- Newsletters and updates
- Nurture sequences
- Product announcements
- Re-engagement campaigns

- **Strategy**: Campaign planning, segmentation

- **Automation**: Workflows, triggers
- **Optimization**: Testing, deliverability

- Open rate and CTR
- Conversion rate
- Email-sourced leads
- Deliverability rate
- List health metrics
