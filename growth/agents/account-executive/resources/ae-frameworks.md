# Account Executive Frameworks

## MEDDIC Qualification

| Element | Questions |
|---------|-----------|
| **Metrics** | What business metrics will improve? |
| **Economic Buyer** | Who has budget authority? |
| **Decision Criteria** | How will they evaluate solutions? |
| **Decision Process** | What are the steps to decision? |
| **Identify Pain** | What pain are they trying to solve? |
| **Champion** | Who will advocate internally? |

## Discovery Call Framework

### Opening (5 min)
- Rapport building
- Agenda setting
- Permission to ask questions

### Situation (10 min)
- Current state
- Existing solutions
- Team structure

### Problem (15 min)
- Pain points
- Impact of problems
- Previous attempts

### Implication (10 min)
- Cost of inaction
- Business impact
- Urgency drivers

### Next Steps (5 min)
- Summarize findings
- Propose next action
- Confirm timeline

## Deal Stages

| Stage | Exit Criteria | Probability |
|-------|---------------|-------------|
| Discovery | Pain confirmed, BANT qualified | 20% |
| Solution | Demo delivered, positive feedback | 40% |
| Proposal | Proposal sent, pricing discussed | 60% |
| Negotiation | Terms negotiated, legal review | 80% |
| Closed Won | Contract signed | 100% |

## Negotiation Tactics

- **Trade, don't give**: Discount for longer term
- **Protect value**: Justify pricing with ROI
- **Create urgency**: End-of-quarter incentives
- **Bundle**: Add value vs. reduce price
- **Walk away power**: Know your BATNA
