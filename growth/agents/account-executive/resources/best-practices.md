# Best Practices: Account Executive

> Design principles, patterns, and frameworks that guide high-quality full-cycle sales and deal closing work.

## Design Principles

- **Problem-First Selling**: Understand the prospect's business pain before presenting any solution
- **Qualification Discipline**: Only invest time in opportunities that meet minimum viability criteria
- **Multi-Threading**: Build relationships with multiple stakeholders to avoid single-point-of-failure deals
- **Value Articulation**: Always connect product capabilities to measurable business outcomes
- **Buying Process Alignment**: Sync your sales process to how the buyer actually makes decisions
- **Honest Forecasting**: Represent pipeline accurately—inflated forecasts destroy planning and trust
- **Speed as Respect**: Fast follow-ups signal professionalism and seriousness

## Key Patterns & Frameworks

- **MEDDPICC**: Metrics, Economic Buyer, Decision Criteria, Decision Process, Identify Pain, Champion, Competition — comprehensive qualification framework to assess deal health and guide next steps
- **Challenger Sale**: Lead with insight, teach the prospect something new about their business, tailor the message to their role, and take control of the conversation
- **SPIN Selling**: Situation, Problem, Implication, Need-Payoff — question-based discovery that helps prospects articulate their own pain and value
- **Sandler Submarine**: Pain → Budget → Decision — a consultative framework that qualifies deals before investing proposal effort
- **The Demo Sandwich**: Open with business context and goals, show only the relevant features, close with next-steps confirmation
- **Command of the Message**: Ensure every conversation reinforces the same value framework, differentiators, and proof points
- **Mutual Action Plan (MAP)**: A shared document outlining the steps both buyer and seller must take to reach a decision by a target date
- **Economic Buyer Access**: Strategies for gaining access to the person who actually controls budget and signs checks
- **Champion Development**: Identifying, nurturing, and equipping internal advocates who sell on your behalf when you're not in the room
- **Competitive Displacement Motion**: Framework for attacking incumbent vendors — identify dissatisfaction, quantify switching cost vs. pain, co-create the business case

## Domain Concepts & Terminology

### Pipeline Management
- **Stage Gates**: Specific exit criteria that must be met before moving a deal to the next pipeline stage
- **Coverage Ratio**: Total pipeline value divided by quota target (typically 3–5×)
- **Weighted Pipeline**: Probability-adjusted deal value used for revenue forecasting
- **Velocity**: How quickly deals move through the pipeline (deal size × win rate ÷ sales cycle length)
- **Pipeline Hygiene**: Regular review and purge of stale, misqualified, or inaccurate opportunities

### Deal Qualification
- **BANT**: Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline — basic qualification filter
- **ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)**: Firmographic and behavioral attributes of your best-fit customers
- **Champion**: Internal stakeholder who advocates for your solution and has organizational credibility
- **Economic Buyer**: The individual with final budget authority, often different from the champion
- **Technical Buyer**: The evaluator assessing product fit and integration requirements

### Sales Process
- **Discovery Call**: Structured conversation to uncover pain, desired outcomes, and buying process
- **POC/Pilot**: Proof of concept or pilot engagement used to validate technical and business fit
- **Business Case**: Quantified ROI analysis built collaboratively with the champion
- **Executive Alignment**: Connecting seller leadership with buyer leadership to strengthen the deal
- **Objection Handling**: Structured responses that acknowledge, clarify, and reframe concerns

### Commercial
- **ARR/ACV**: Annual Recurring Revenue / Annual Contract Value — primary SaaS revenue metrics
- **Expansion Revenue**: Upsells and cross-sells within existing accounts
- **Discount Authority**: The maximum discount a rep can offer without manager approval
- **Legal Red Lines**: Non-negotiable contract terms that require legal escalation

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- **Premature Demo**: Showing the product before understanding the prospect's pain leads to irrelevant demos and lost credibility
- **Single-Threaded Deals**: Relying on one stakeholder means a job change, vacation, or politics can kill the deal overnight
- **Happy Ears**: Misinterpreting buying signals because you want the deal to close; validate with specific next-step commitments
- **Discount Creep**: Offering discounts as a default close tactic trains buyers to expect them and erodes deal value
- **Pipeline Stuffing**: Loading unqualified opportunities to hit coverage targets obscures real forecast risk
- **Skipping Mutual Action Plans**: Without shared milestones, deals drift and close dates slip
- **Feature Dumping**: Presenting every capability instead of only what addresses the prospect's specific pain

## Quality Indicators

- **Discovery Depth**: Can you articulate the prospect's specific pain, impact, and urgency in their own words?
- **Stakeholder Map Completeness**: Have you identified and engaged all key decision influencers?
- **Next-Step Confirmation**: Does every meeting end with a concrete, calendar-confirmed next step?
- **Business Case Strength**: Is the ROI model built on the prospect's own data, not generic benchmarks?
- **Forecast Accuracy**: Is your called commit within 10% of actual closed revenue at quarter-end?
- **Deal Velocity**: Are deals progressing through stages at or faster than historical average cycle time?
- **Win/Loss Pattern**: Can you identify recurring win and loss themes to adjust your approach?

## Collaboration Touchpoints

- **With Sales Engineer**: Align on technical discovery questions before calls; debrief after demos to address open concerns and plan POC scope
- **With Proposal Specialist**: Provide deal context, competitive dynamics, and champion priorities before proposal kick-off to ensure document resonance
- **With Sales Strategist**: Escalate pattern-level insights about ICP fit, competitive positioning gaps, or pricing objections that recur across deals
- **With Revenue Operations**: Maintain CRM accuracy so forecasting models and territory planning reflect reality
- **With Customer Success**: Execute warm handoffs with full context on the customer's stated goals to set the relationship up for expansion
