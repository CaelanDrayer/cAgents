# Influencer Marketing Templates

## Influencer Evaluation Criteria

| Criteria | Weight | Score (1-10) |
|----------|--------|--------------|
| **Relevance** | 30% | Audience fit |
| **Reach** | 20% | Follower count |
| **Engagement** | 25% | Engagement rate |
| **Authenticity** | 15% | Content quality |
| **Brand Safety** | 10% | Past behavior |

## Influencer Tiers

| Tier | Followers | Typical Rate | Best For |
|------|-----------|--------------|----------|
| **Nano** | 1-10K | $50-500 | Authenticity |
| **Micro** | 10-100K | $500-5K | Engagement |
| **Mid** | 100K-500K | $5K-25K | Reach + engagement |
| **Macro** | 500K-1M | $25K-100K | Reach |
| **Mega** | 1M+ | $100K+ | Mass awareness |

## Campaign Brief Template

```markdown
# Influencer Campaign Brief: [Name]

## Campaign Overview
- Objective: [Awareness/Engagement/Conversion]
- Timeline: [Dates]
- Budget: $[Amount]

## Target Influencers
- Tier: [Micro/Mid/Macro]
- Niche: [Industry/Topic]
- Platform: [Instagram/TikTok/YouTube]
- Count: [Number of influencers]

## Content Guidelines
- Required: [Brand mention, hashtag, link]
- Tone: [Authentic, educational, entertaining]
- Dos: [List]
- Don'ts: [List]

## Deliverables
- [X] posts on [platform]
- Due: [Date]
- Approval process: [Steps]

## Compensation
- Payment: $[Amount]
- Additional: [Product, affiliate, etc.]
```

## ROI Calculation

```
Influencer ROI = (Revenue Attributed - Campaign Cost) / Campaign Cost

CPE (Cost Per Engagement) = Campaign Cost / Total Engagements

CPM (Cost Per 1000 Impressions) = (Campaign Cost / Impressions) x 1000
```

## Contract Essentials

- Deliverables and timeline
- Compensation and payment terms
- Usage rights and exclusivity
- FTC disclosure requirements
- Approval process
- Cancellation terms
