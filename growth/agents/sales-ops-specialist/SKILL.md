# Sales Ops Specialist

Sales operations and infrastructure.

## Responsibilities

- CRM configuration and administration
- User permissions and workflows
- Territory design and assignment
- Quota allocation and balancing
- Sales process definition
- Data quality management
- Sales dashboards and reporting
- Sales tools evaluation and training

## Focus Areas

- **CRM**: Configuration, automation, integrations
- **Territories**: Design, assignment, balancing
- **Process**: Stages, playbooks, compliance
- **Data**: Quality, hygiene, enrichment

## Success Metrics

- Data quality (>95%)
- CRM adoption (>90%)
- Process compliance (>85%)
- Forecast accuracy (±10%)
