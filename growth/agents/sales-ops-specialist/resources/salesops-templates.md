# Sales Operations Templates

## Territory Design Models

**Geographic**: By region, state, city, zip
**Account-Based**: By company size, industry, revenue
**Named Accounts**: Strategic accounts to specific reps
**Hybrid**: Combination of above

### Balancing Factors
- Opportunity size
- Account count
- Market potential
- Travel time
- Historical performance

## Quota Allocation Template

```markdown
# Quota Allocation FY[Year]

## Company Target: $[Amount]

## Allocation by Region
| Region | Headcount | Quota | % of Total |
|--------|-----------|-------|------------|
| West | 10 | $5M | 25% |
| East | 12 | $6M | 30% |
| Central | 8 | $4M | 20% |
| Strategic | 5 | $5M | 25% |

## Allocation Methodology
- Base: Historical performance (60%)
- Growth: Market potential (30%)
- Strategic: New initiatives (10%)
```

## Sales Process Stages

| Stage | Exit Criteria | Probability |
|-------|---------------|-------------|
| **Prospect** | First meeting scheduled | 10% |
| **Discovery** | Needs confirmed, champion identified | 20% |
| **Solution** | Solution presented, positive feedback | 40% |
| **Proposal** | Proposal delivered, pricing discussed | 60% |
| **Negotiation** | Terms negotiated, legal review | 80% |
| **Closed Won** | Contract signed | 100% |

## Data Quality Standards

### Required Fields
- Account: Company name, industry, size, owner
- Contact: Name, email, title, phone
- Opportunity: Stage, amount, close date, next step

### Validation Rules
- Email format validation
- Phone format standardization
- Duplicate detection
- Stage progression rules

## CRM Hygiene Checklist

- [ ] Duplicate accounts merged
- [ ] Stale opportunities closed
- [ ] Missing required fields flagged
- [ ] Inactive users deactivated
- [ ] Custom fields reviewed
