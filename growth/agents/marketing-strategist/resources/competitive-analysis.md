# Competitive Analysis

Reference for conducting competitive intelligence and analysis.

## Analysis Framework

### 1. Competitor Identification

**Direct Competitors**: Same product/service, same market
**Indirect Competitors**: Different product, same need solved
**Aspirational Competitors**: Where you want to be positioned

### 2. Data Collection Sources

- **Public**: Website, pricing pages, blog, social media, job postings
- **Product**: Free trials, demos, feature comparison
- **Financial**: Annual reports, SEC filings, funding announcements
- **Reviews**: G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, app stores
- **Social**: LinkedIn employee count, Glassdoor reviews
- **SEO**: Keyword rankings, domain authority, backlink profile

### 3. Analysis Dimensions

| Dimension | What to Assess |
|-----------|---------------|
| Product | Features, quality, technology, roadmap |
| Pricing | Model, tiers, discounts, value perception |
| Positioning | Messaging, target audience, brand voice |
| Distribution | Channels, partnerships, market coverage |
| Marketing | Content strategy, ad spend, events |
| Team | Key hires, leadership, growth signals |

## Win/Loss Analysis

### Data Points to Capture
- **Win reason**: Feature, price, relationship, brand, integration
- **Loss reason**: Feature gap, price, competitor strength, timing
- **Competitive mention rate**: How often each competitor appears in deals
- **Win rate by competitor**: Head-to-head performance

### Interview Questions
1. What alternatives did you evaluate?
2. What was the deciding factor?
3. What nearly made you choose differently?
4. How did our pricing compare?
5. What feature gaps concerned you?
