# Go-to-Market Planning

Reference for developing and executing go-to-market strategies.

## GTM Planning Framework

### Phase 1: Market Readiness

**Market Validation Checklist**:
- [ ] Target market defined and sized (TAM/SAM/SOM)
- [ ] Customer problem validated (interviews, surveys, usage data)
- [ ] Competitive landscape mapped
- [ ] Pricing model validated
- [ ] Distribution channels identified

### Phase 2: Positioning & Messaging

**Positioning Template**:
```
For [target customer]
Who [statement of need or opportunity]
[Product name] is a [product category]
That [key benefit / reason to buy]
Unlike [competitive alternative]
Our product [key differentiator]
```

**Message Testing Matrix**:
| Message | Audience Segment | Channel | Metric | Result |
|---------|-----------------|---------|--------|--------|
| Value prop A | Enterprise | Email | Open rate | |
| Value prop B | SMB | Social | Engagement | |
| Pain point focus | Technical | Content | Time on page | |

### Phase 3: Channel Strategy

**Channel Prioritization**:
| Channel | Reach | Cost | Time to Impact | Priority |
|---------|-------|------|----------------|----------|
| Content/SEO | High | Low | 3-6 months | Foundation |
| Paid search | Medium | High | Immediate | Quick wins |
| Email/nurture | Medium | Low | 1-3 months | Conversion |
| Social organic | Low-Med | Low | 3-6 months | Brand |
| Partnerships | Variable | Medium | 3-6 months | Scale |
| Events/webinars | Low | Medium | 1-2 months | Pipeline |

### Phase 4: Launch Execution

**Launch Timeline Template**:

| Week | Activity | Owner |
|------|----------|-------|
| T-8 | Finalize messaging, create assets | Marketing |
| T-6 | Sales enablement, training | Sales + Marketing |
| T-4 | PR outreach, analyst briefings | Comms |
| T-2 | Beta customer testimonials | Customer Success |
| T-1 | Pre-launch teaser campaign | Marketing |
| Launch | Press release, social blitz, email blast | All |
| T+1 | Monitor metrics, gather feedback | Marketing |
| T+2 | Optimize based on data | Marketing |
| T+4 | Post-launch review | All |

### Phase 5: Measurement

**GTM Metrics Dashboard**:
- **Awareness**: Website traffic, social mentions, press coverage
- **Interest**: Content downloads, webinar signups, demo requests
- **Consideration**: Free trial starts, sales conversations, RFP responses
- **Decision**: Pipeline created, win rate, deal velocity
- **Adoption**: Activation rate, feature usage, time-to-value

## Launch Types

| Launch Type | Scope | Activities |
|-------------|-------|-----------|
| Tier 1 (Major) | New product, major rebrand | Full PR, analyst, sales, marketing push |
| Tier 2 (Significant) | Major feature, new market | Blog, email, webinar, sales enablement |
| Tier 3 (Minor) | Feature update, improvement | Blog post, in-app notification, changelog |
