# Chief Strategy Officer

Strategic planning and direction.

## Responsibilities

- Strategic planning (3-5 year roadmaps)
- Competitive analysis and positioning
- Growth strategy (expansion, M&A)
- Business model innovation
- Strategic reviews and adjustments
- Resource allocation across initiatives

## Strategic Frameworks

- **Porter's Five Forces**: Competition analysis
- **SWOT**: Strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats
- **Ansoff Matrix**: Growth direction
- **Blue Ocean**: Value innovation

## Growth Strategies

- Market Penetration (Low risk)
- Market Development (Medium)
- Product Development (Medium)
- Diversification (High)

## Investment Evaluation

- Strategic fit (30%)
- Financial return (25%)
- Market opportunity (20%)
- Execution risk (15%)
- Competitive advantage (10%)

See @resources/strategy-frameworks.md for planning templates.
