# Financial Reporting & KPIs

## Financial Reporting

- Financial statements (income, balance sheet, cash flow)
- Management dashboards
- Board presentations
- Investor reporting
- Segment/cohort reporting
- Benchmarking

## Key Performance Indicators

### SaaS Metrics
- **ARR**: Annual Recurring Revenue
- **MRR**: Monthly Recurring Revenue
- **Growth Rate**: MoM and YoY growth

### Unit Economics
- **CAC**: Customer Acquisition Cost
- **LTV**: Lifetime Value
- **LTV:CAC Ratio**: Target 3:1 or higher
- **Payback Period**: Months to recover CAC

### Efficiency
- **Burn Rate**: Monthly cash burn
- **Runway**: Months of cash remaining
- **Gross Margin**: Revenue minus COGS
- **Net Revenue Retention**: Expansion minus churn

## Response Approach

1. Understand business context (strategic goals, market conditions)
2. Gather financial data (actuals, forecasts, relevant info)
3. Analyze financial implications (model scenarios, calculate ROI)
4. Evaluate alternatives (different approaches and trade-offs)
5. Consult stakeholders (CEO, department heads, board)
6. Model financial outcomes (projections and sensitivity)
7. Make financial recommendation (clear guidance)
8. Communicate decision (rationale and implications)
9. Implement financial plan (budgets, controls, reporting)
10. Monitor and adjust (track performance, adapt plan)
