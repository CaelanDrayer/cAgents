# Engineering Culture Building

Creating and nurturing high-performance engineering culture.

## Culture Foundations

### Core Engineering Values

**Ownership:**
- Own your code end-to-end
- Take responsibility for outcomes
- Fix what you break
- Think beyond your task

**Craftsmanship:**
- Write code you're proud of
- Test thoroughly
- Document for others
- Continuously improve

**Collaboration:**
- Help teammates succeed
- Share knowledge freely
- Give constructive feedback
- Celebrate team wins

**Curiosity:**
- Always be learning
- Question assumptions
- Experiment and iterate
- Embrace new technologies

**Integrity:**
- Be honest about estimates
- Admit mistakes early
- Follow through on commitments
- Speak up about concerns

## Psychological Safety

### What It Means
Team members feel safe to:
- Ask questions
- Admit mistakes
- Propose ideas
- Challenge decisions
- Take risks

### Building Safety

**As a Leader:**
- Admit your own mistakes
- Ask genuine questions
- Thank people for raising issues
- Never punish learning failures
- Encourage diverse perspectives

**Team Practices:**
- Blameless post-mortems
- No stupid questions policy
- Safe space for feedback
- Anonymous suggestion box
- Regular 1:1s

### Signs of Low Safety
- People don't speak up in meetings
- Only senior people give opinions
- Mistakes are hidden
- Blame culture after incidents
- Ideas only come from leadership

## Learning & Development

### Learning Time

**Allocation:**
- 10% time for learning (4 hours/week)
- 20% time for innovation (if mature)
- Learning budget per engineer

**Options:**
- Online courses (Pluralsight, Udemy)
- Conference attendance
- Book clubs
- Certification programs
- Internal training

### Knowledge Sharing

**Tech Talks (Weekly/Bi-weekly):**
- 30-60 min presentations
- Engineers present to peers
- Mix of internal and external topics
- Record for async viewing

**Brown Bags (Lunch & Learn):**
- Informal learning sessions
- Demos of new tools/techniques
- Cross-team sharing

**Documentation:**
- Engineering wiki
- ADRs (Architecture Decision Records)
- Runbooks and how-tos
- Onboarding guides

### Mentorship Program

**Structure:**
- Pair senior with junior
- Regular 1:1s (bi-weekly)
- Goal setting together
- Shadowing opportunities

**Mentor Responsibilities:**
- Career guidance
- Technical coaching
- Organizational navigation
- Advocate for mentee

## Recognition & Rewards

### Recognition Types

**Peer Recognition:**
- Shoutouts in team meetings
- Kudos channel in Slack
- Peer bonus programs
- Team appreciation walls

**Manager Recognition:**
- 1:1 acknowledgment
- Written praise (email/Slack)
- Public recognition in all-hands
- Spot bonuses

**Formal Recognition:**
- Quarterly awards
- Annual bonuses
- Promotion recognition
- Title/level advancement

### What to Recognize
- Going above and beyond
- Helping teammates
- Solving hard problems
- Taking initiative
- Living company values
- Learning and growing

### Recognition Tips
- Be specific (what they did)
- Be timely (soon after event)
- Be genuine (mean it)
- Be public (when appropriate)
- Be proportionate (match achievement)

## Team Engagement

### Engagement Drivers

**Meaningful Work:**
- Clear purpose and impact
- Connection to users/customers
- Challenging problems
- Autonomy in approach

**Growth Opportunities:**
- Learning new skills
- Career advancement
- Increasing responsibility
- Exposure to new areas

**Positive Environment:**
- Supportive teammates
- Good manager relationship
- Work-life balance
- Fun and camaraderie

### Measuring Engagement

**Surveys (Quarterly):**
- eNPS (employee Net Promoter Score)
- Engagement index questions
- Open-ended feedback

**Indicators:**
- Retention rate
- Referral rate
- Meeting participation
- Voluntary contributions

### Improving Engagement

**Low engagement? Check:**
- Is work meaningful?
- Do people have growth paths?
- Is management effective?
- Are there team conflicts?
- Is workload sustainable?

## Team Building

### Regular Activities

**Weekly:**
- Team coffee chat
- Social Slack channel
- Virtual water cooler

**Monthly:**
- Team lunch/happy hour
- Game sessions
- Show and tell

**Quarterly:**
- Offsite/team event
- Hackathon
- Celebration/milestone

### Remote Team Building

**Virtual Events:**
- Online games (Jackbox, Among Us)
- Virtual escape rooms
- Trivia nights
- Cooking/cocktail classes

**Async Bonding:**
- Random coffee pairings
- Pet/family photo sharing
- Hobby channels
- Birthday celebrations

### Onboarding for Culture

**First Week:**
- Welcome message from VP
- Lunch with team
- Buddy assigned
- Culture overview session

**First Month:**
- 1:1s with key people
- Attend team events
- Participate in tech talk
- Complete onboarding project

**First Quarter:**
- Mentorship pairing
- First code in production
- Culture feedback session
- Role clarity confirmed

## Communication Practices

### Engineering All-Hands (Monthly)

**Agenda:**
- Business update (5 min)
- Product highlights (10 min)
- Engineering updates (15 min)
- Recognition (5 min)
- Q&A (10 min)

### Skip-Level 1:1s (Monthly)

**Purpose:**
- Direct feedback channel
- Early warning on issues
- Career development
- Culture health check

**Questions to Ask:**
- How is the team doing?
- What's working well?
- What could be better?
- How can I help you?

### Transparency

**Share Openly:**
- Company strategy and goals
- Engineering priorities
- Key metrics and progress
- Challenges and setbacks

**Create Forums:**
- AMA (Ask Me Anything) sessions
- Engineering newsletter
- Slack channels for updates
- Open door policy
