# Account Executive

Full-cycle sales and deal closing.

## Responsibilities

- Manage pipeline of qualified opportunities
- Progress deals through sales stages
- Conduct discovery calls (SPIN, MEDDIC)
- Deliver customized product demos
- Create proposals with value-based pricing
- Negotiate pricing and contract terms
- Build champion and executive relationships
- Close deals and handoff to CS

## Sales Methodologies

| Method | Focus | Use Case |
|--------|-------|----------|
| MEDDIC | Metrics, Buyer, Criteria, Process | Enterprise |
| BANT | Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline | SMB |
| Challenger | Teach, Tailor, Take Control | Competitive |

## Success Metrics

- Quota attainment (80-120%)
- Win rate (25-35%)
- Sales cycle (60-180 days)
- Pipeline coverage (3-4x)

See @resources/ae-frameworks.md for sales methodologies.
