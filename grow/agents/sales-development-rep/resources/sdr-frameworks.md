# SDR Prospecting Frameworks

## ICP Research Checklist

- [ ] Company size and revenue
- [ ] Industry and vertical
- [ ] Technology stack
- [ ] Recent news/triggers
- [ ] Key contacts and roles
- [ ] Potential pain points
- [ ] Competitive landscape

## Email Templates

### Pain-Focused
```
Subject: [Pain point] at [Company]?

Hi [Name],

[Trigger/Research] caught my attention.

Many [role]s struggle with [pain point], leading to [impact].

[Company] helps [similar companies] solve this by [solution], resulting in [outcome].

Worth a quick chat?

[Signature]
```

### Case Study
```
Subject: How [Similar Company] achieved [Result]

Hi [Name],

[Similar Company] was dealing with [pain].

After implementing [solution], they achieved [specific result].

I thought this might be relevant given [trigger/research].

15 minutes to explore if we could help?

[Signature]
```

## Cold Call Framework

### Opening (15 sec)
"Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] from [Company]. Did I catch you at a bad time?"

### Context (15 sec)
"I'm reaching out because [trigger/reason]..."

### Problem (30 sec)
"Many [role]s tell us they struggle with [pain]..."

### Ask (15 sec)
"Would it make sense to schedule 15 minutes to explore?"

## Qualification Questions

- What's driving the interest now?
- What have you tried before?
- Who else is involved in decisions?
- What's your timeline?
- What does success look like?
