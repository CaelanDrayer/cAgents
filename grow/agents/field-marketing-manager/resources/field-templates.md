## Local Event Planning

### Executive Dinner
- Format: Intimate dinner, 12-15 execs
- Venue: Upscale restaurant
- Content: Thought leadership discussion
- Goal: Relationship building
- Budget: $4-6K

### Roundtable
- Format: Discussion session, 20-25 attendees
- Venue: Hotel meeting room
- Content: Industry topic, peer discussion
- Goal: Lead generation, thought leadership
- Budget: $2-4K

### Workshop
- Format: Hands-on session, 15-30 attendees
- Venue: Company office or co-working space
- Content: Product training or best practices
- Goal: Engagement, adoption
- Budget: $1-2K
