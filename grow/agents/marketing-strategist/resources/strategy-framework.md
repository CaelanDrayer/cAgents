# Marketing Strategy Framework

Reference for developing comprehensive marketing strategies.

## Strategy Development Process

### 1. Situation Analysis
- **Market Assessment**: Size, growth rate, trends, segmentation
- **SWOT Analysis**: Strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats
- **Competitive Landscape**: Direct/indirect competitors, positioning, share
- **Customer Analysis**: Segments, needs, pain points, buying journey

### 2. Strategic Objectives
- Define SMART goals (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound)
- Align with business KPIs (revenue, growth, retention)
- Set leading and lagging indicators

### 3. Target Audience
- **Persona Development**: Demographics, psychographics, behaviors
- **Jobs-to-be-Done**: Functional, emotional, social outcomes
- **Segmentation**: By value, behavior, lifecycle stage, or need

### 4. Positioning & Messaging
- **Value Proposition Canvas**: Fit between product and customer segment
- **Positioning Statement**: For [target], [product] is the [category] that [differentiator] because [reason to believe]
- **Messaging Hierarchy**: Brand promise -> pillars -> proof points

### 5. Channel Strategy
- **Owned**: Website, blog, email, app
- **Earned**: PR, reviews, word-of-mouth, SEO
- **Paid**: Search ads, social ads, display, sponsorships
- **Shared**: Social media, community, partnerships

## Strategy Frameworks

### Porter's Five Forces
| Force | Assessment Questions |
|-------|---------------------|
| Competitive Rivalry | How many competitors? Differentiation level? |
| Supplier Power | How many suppliers? Switching costs? |
| Buyer Power | How price-sensitive? Alternatives available? |
| Threat of Substitution | How easy to switch? Substitute quality? |
| Threat of New Entry | Barriers to entry? Capital requirements? |

### Ansoff Growth Matrix
| | Existing Products | New Products |
|---|---|---|
| **Existing Markets** | Market Penetration | Product Development |
| **New Markets** | Market Development | Diversification |

### STP Framework
1. **Segment**: Divide market by meaningful criteria
2. **Target**: Evaluate and select segments to serve
3. **Position**: Create distinct value proposition per segment

## Success Metrics

- **Awareness**: Brand recall, share of voice, impressions
- **Consideration**: Traffic, engagement, content consumption
- **Conversion**: Leads, MQLs, SQLs, pipeline, revenue
- **Retention**: NPS, churn rate, LTV, expansion revenue
