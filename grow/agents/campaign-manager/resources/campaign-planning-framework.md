# Campaign Planning Framework

Comprehensive framework for planning, structuring, and launching campaigns across channels.

## Campaign Brief Structure

Every campaign begins with a structured brief. The campaign-manager must ensure all sections are completed before moving to execution.

### Required Brief Fields

| Field | Description | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| Campaign Name | Descriptive identifier | "Q2 Product Launch - Enterprise" |
| Objective | Primary measurable goal | "Generate 500 MQLs in 6 weeks" |
| Target Audience | Specific segment(s) | "Enterprise CTOs, 500+ employees" |
| Channels | Distribution channels | "LinkedIn Ads, Email, Webinar" |
| Budget | Total and per-channel allocation | "$25,000 total ($10K ads, $8K content, $7K events)" |
| Timeline | Start, milestones, end | "Mar 1 - Apr 15, mid-campaign review Mar 22" |
| Success Metrics | KPIs with targets | "CPA < $50, CTR > 2.5%, Conv Rate > 3%" |
| Dependencies | Cross-team needs | "Creative assets by Feb 20, landing page by Feb 25" |

### Brief Approval Checklist

Before a brief is considered approved, verify:

- [ ] Objective is SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound)
- [ ] Budget has been allocated and approved by finance
- [ ] Target audience is defined with specificity (not just "developers")
- [ ] At least 2 channels identified with rationale for each
- [ ] Timeline includes buffer for creative review and approvals
- [ ] Success metrics have baseline comparisons
- [ ] Dependencies mapped with owners and due dates

## Campaign Types and Planning Patterns

### Awareness Campaigns

**Goal**: Brand visibility, reach, impressions
**Typical Channels**: Display ads, social media, PR, content syndication
**Planning Duration**: 2-3 weeks
**Key Questions**:
- What is the current brand awareness baseline?
- Which channels reach our audience most efficiently?
- What creative themes resonate with the target segment?

**Budget Allocation Pattern**:
- 50% paid media (display, social ads)
- 25% content creation (blog posts, infographics, video)
- 15% distribution and syndication
- 10% measurement and tooling

### Lead Generation Campaigns

**Goal**: Qualified leads, pipeline contribution
**Typical Channels**: Search ads, email nurture, webinars, gated content
**Planning Duration**: 3-4 weeks
**Key Questions**:
- What is the current lead-to-MQL conversion rate?
- What content offers have the highest conversion history?
- What is the acceptable cost per lead for this segment?

**Budget Allocation Pattern**:
- 40% paid acquisition (search, social lead gen)
- 25% content and asset creation
- 20% email and nurture automation
- 15% landing pages and conversion optimization

### Product Launch Campaigns

**Goal**: Adoption, signups, revenue from new product/feature
**Typical Channels**: Multi-channel coordinated (email, ads, PR, sales enablement)
**Planning Duration**: 4-6 weeks
**Key Questions**:
- What is the go-to-market positioning?
- Which existing customers are early adopter candidates?
- What competitive messaging needs to be addressed?

**Budget Allocation Pattern**:
- 30% paid media (multi-channel)
- 25% content and creative assets
- 20% events and webinars
- 15% sales enablement materials
- 10% PR and analyst relations

## Timeline Templates

### 6-Week Standard Campaign

| Week | Phase | Activities |
|------|-------|-----------|
| 1 | Planning | Brief, audience research, channel selection |
| 2 | Creative | Asset creation, copy development, landing pages |
| 3 | Setup | Ad platforms, email sequences, tracking |
| 4 | Launch | Go live, initial monitoring, quick adjustments |
| 5 | Optimize | A/B test results, budget reallocation, targeting refinement |
| 6 | Wrap-up | Performance analysis, reporting, learnings |

### 2-Week Sprint Campaign

| Week | Phase | Activities |
|------|-------|-----------|
| 1 (Mon-Wed) | Plan + Create | Brief, assets, setup in parallel |
| 1 (Thu-Fri) | Launch | Go live, verify tracking |
| 2 (Mon-Wed) | Optimize | Adjust targeting, budgets, creative |
| 2 (Thu-Fri) | Report | Results, recommendations, next steps |

## Channel Selection Matrix

Use this matrix when deciding which channels to include in a campaign:

| Channel | Best For | Min Budget | Lead Time | Measurement Ease |
|---------|----------|-----------|-----------|-----------------|
| Search Ads | Intent-based demand | $2,000/mo | 1 week | High |
| LinkedIn Ads | B2B targeting | $3,000/mo | 1 week | High |
| Email | Nurture, retention | $500/mo | 3 days | High |
| Webinars | Education, demos | $1,000/event | 3 weeks | Medium |
| Content Syndication | Reach, awareness | $2,000/mo | 2 weeks | Medium |
| Display Ads | Retargeting, awareness | $1,500/mo | 1 week | Medium |
| Social Organic | Community, engagement | $0 | Ongoing | Low |
| PR | Credibility, awareness | $5,000/mo | 4 weeks | Low |

## Cross-Functional Coordination

### Stakeholder Map

| Team | Involvement | Key Handoff |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| Creative | Asset production | Creative brief -> finished assets |
| Content | Copy, blog posts | Content calendar -> published pieces |
| Sales | Lead follow-up | MQL handoff criteria -> qualified leads |
| Product | Messaging alignment | Feature specs -> positioning |
| Analytics | Tracking setup | Measurement plan -> dashboards |
| Finance | Budget approval | Budget request -> allocation |

### Communication Cadence

- **Daily**: Campaign performance check (automated dashboard)
- **Twice Weekly**: Creative/content sync (15 min standup)
- **Weekly**: Full campaign review with stakeholders (30 min)
- **Post-Campaign**: Retrospective with all stakeholders (60 min)

## Risk Assessment

Before launching, assess these common campaign risks:

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|-----------|
| Creative delays | High | Medium | Build 3-day buffer in timeline |
| Low initial performance | Medium | Medium | Pre-plan optimization levers |
| Budget overrun | Low | High | Set daily spend caps, monitor weekly |
| Tracking failure | Medium | High | QA tracking before launch |
| Audience fatigue | Medium | Medium | Frequency caps, creative rotation |
| Competitive interference | Low | Medium | Monitor competitor activity weekly |
