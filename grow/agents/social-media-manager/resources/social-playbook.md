# Social Media Playbook

## Platform Guidelines

### LinkedIn
- **Best for**: B2B, thought leadership, recruiting
- **Posting frequency**: 1-2x daily
- **Content types**: Articles, insights, company updates
- **Engagement**: Professional, value-focused

### Twitter/X
- **Best for**: News, engagement, customer service
- **Posting frequency**: 3-5x daily
- **Content types**: Quick takes, threads, links
- **Engagement**: Timely, conversational

### Instagram
- **Best for**: Brand, culture, visual storytelling
- **Posting frequency**: 1x daily, stories multiple
- **Content types**: Photos, reels, stories
- **Engagement**: Visual, authentic

## Engagement Guidelines

### Response Times
- Mentions: < 2 hours
- DMs: < 4 hours
- Comments: < 24 hours

### Response Tone
- Professional yet personable
- Helpful and solution-oriented
- On-brand voice
