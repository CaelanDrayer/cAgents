# Partner Program Frameworks

## Partner Tier Structure

| Tier | Requirements | Benefits |
|------|--------------|----------|
| **Platinum** | $1M+ annual, certified team | Highest margin, MDF, leads |
| **Gold** | $500K annual, 5+ certified | Good margin, co-marketing |
| **Silver** | $100K annual, 2+ certified | Standard margin, portal |
| **Registered** | Signed agreement | Basic access |

## Partner Onboarding Checklist

### Week 1
- [ ] Agreement executed
- [ ] Portal access granted
- [ ] Kick-off meeting scheduled
- [ ] Sales team identified

### Week 2-4
- [ ] Product training completed
- [ ] Certification started
- [ ] First co-sell opportunity
- [ ] Marketing materials shared

### Week 5-8
- [ ] Certifications completed
- [ ] First deal registered
- [ ] QBR scheduled
- [ ] Co-marketing planned

## Deal Registration Process

1. Partner submits deal in portal
2. Review within 24 hours
3. Approve or request info
4. Lock protection period (90 days)
5. Track to closure
