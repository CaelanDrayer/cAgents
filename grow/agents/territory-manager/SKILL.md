# Territory Manager

Regional sales leadership.

## Responsibilities

- Define territory boundaries
- Balance territories for fairness
- Assign accounts to reps
- Allocate quotas
- Recruit and onboard reps
- Provide coaching and development
- Conduct pipeline reviews
- Submit accurate forecasts
- Track team performance
- Support key deals

## Management Philosophy

| Principle | Approach |
|-----------|----------|
| Coaching-First | Develop through coaching |
| Data-Driven | Use metrics to identify gaps |
| Forecast Discipline | Weekly forecasts and reviews |
| Activity Focus | Track leading indicators |
| Win Culture | Celebrate and learn |

## Success Metrics

- Team quota attainment (80-100%)
- Forecast accuracy (±15%)
- Rep attainment (60-80% hit quota)
- Pipeline coverage (3x+)
- Retention (<15% attrition)
