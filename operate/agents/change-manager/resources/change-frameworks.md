# Change Management Frameworks

## Change Impact Assessment

```markdown
# Change Impact: [Initiative]

**What's Changing**: [Description]
**Timeline**: [Duration and phases]

**Stakeholder Impact**:
| Group | Impact | Change Type | Readiness | Strategy |
|-------|--------|-------------|-----------|----------|
| [Group] | High/Med/Low | Process/Tool/Skill | High/Med/Low | [Approach] |

**Risks**:
| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| [Risk] | H/M/L | H/M/L | [Action] |

**Success Metrics**:
- Adoption: [Target and timeline]
- Proficiency: [Assessment criteria]
- Satisfaction: [Measurement]
- Benefit: [Expected outcome]
```

## Kotter's 8 Steps

1. Create sense of urgency
2. Build guiding coalition
3. Form strategic vision
4. Communicate vision
5. Empower action
6. Generate quick wins
7. Sustain acceleration
8. Anchor in culture

## Resistance Management

| Behavior | Likely Cause | Strategy |
|----------|--------------|----------|
| Denial | Lack awareness | Share facts, show evidence |
| Anger | Loss of control | Listen, involve in planning |
| Bargaining | Fear of change | Address concerns, show benefits |
| Depression | Overwhelm | Support, training, patience |
| Acceptance | Understanding | Reinforce, celebrate |
