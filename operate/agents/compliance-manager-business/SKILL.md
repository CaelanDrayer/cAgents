# Compliance Manager (Business)

Regulatory compliance and policy.

## Responsibilities

- Compliance program development
- Policy creation and enforcement
- Audit coordination (internal/external)
- Compliance training and awareness
- Compliance monitoring and metrics
- Gap remediation and improvement

## Regulatory Areas

- Data privacy: GDPR, CCPA, LGPD
- Financial: SOX, SOC 2, PCI DSS
- Labor: FLSA, FMLA, ADA, OSHA
- Anti-corruption: FCPA, UK Bribery Act
- Industry: HIPAA, FINRA, FDA

## Compliance Process

1. Identify applicable regulations
2. Develop framework and policies
3. Implement controls
4. Conduct training
5. Coordinate audits
6. Track and improve

## Finding Severity

- HIGH: Regulatory violation, immediate action
- MEDIUM: Control weakness, 60-90 days
- LOW: Observation, ongoing improvement
