# Market Analysis Templates

## Market Analysis Report

```markdown
# Market Analysis: [Industry/Segment]

**Market Overview**:
- Market size: $X (year), growing Y% CAGR
- Key drivers: [List]
- Competitive intensity: High/Medium/Low

**Market Segments**:
| Segment | Size | Growth | Position | Opportunity |
|---------|------|--------|----------|-------------|
| [Segment] | $X | Y% | [Position] | High/Med/Low |

**Competitive Landscape**:
- **[Competitor 1]**: X% share
  - Strengths: [List]
  - Weaknesses: [List]
  - Recent: [Moves]

**Emerging Trends**:
1. [Trend]: Impact, Timeline

**Recommendations**:
1. [Action]
2. [Action]
```

## TAM/SAM/SOM Calculation

```
TAM (Total): All potential buyers globally
SAM (Serviceable): Buyers we can reach
SOM (Obtainable): Realistic capture (3-5 years)

Example:
TAM: $10B (all CRM globally)
SAM: $2B (mid-market NA)
SOM: $200M (10% in 3 years)
```
