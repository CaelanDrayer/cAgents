## Relationship Recovery

### At-Risk Indicators
- Declining engagement
- Negative feedback
- Reduced communication
- Missed commitments
- Executive changes

### Recovery Playbook
```markdown
## Phase 1: Assessment (Week 1)
- Analyze relationship history
- Identify root causes
- Gather internal feedback

## Phase 2: Outreach (Week 2)
- Executive-to-executive conversation
- Acknowledge concerns
- Propose recovery plan

## Phase 3: Action (Weeks 3-8)
- Implement quick wins
- Address root causes
- Regular progress updates

## Phase 4: Stabilization (Ongoing)
- Rebuild trust through consistency
- Increase engagement
- Monitor health closely
```
