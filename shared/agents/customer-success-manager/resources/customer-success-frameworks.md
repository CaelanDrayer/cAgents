# Customer Success Frameworks

## Customer Onboarding Journey

### Onboarding Phases
1. **Kickoff**: Welcome, introductions, expectations
2. **Setup**: Technical implementation, configuration
3. **Training**: User enablement, admin training
4. **Go-Live**: Launch support, early adoption
5. **Optimization**: Expand usage, drive value

## Customer Health Score

### Health Indicators
| Indicator | Weight | Healthy | At-Risk | Critical |
|-----------|--------|---------|---------|----------|
| Product Usage | 30% | >80% DAU | 40-80% | <40% |
| Feature Adoption | 20% | >5 features | 2-5 | <2 |
| Support Tickets | 15% | <2/month | 2-5 | >5 |
| NPS Score | 15% | >50 | 0-50 | <0 |
| Engagement | 20% | Weekly | Monthly | None |

### Health Actions
- **Healthy (80-100)**: Identify expansion opportunities
- **At-Risk (50-79)**: Proactive outreach, address concerns
- **Critical (<50)**: Executive escalation, recovery plan

## Adoption Playbook

### Low Adoption Indicators
- Login frequency declining
- Key features unused
- Training incomplete
- Support tickets increasing

### Intervention Strategies
1. Executive alignment call
2. Adoption workshop
3. Success review meeting
4. Additional training
5. Use case discovery

## Value Realization

## Churn Prevention

### Early Warning Signs
- Declining usage
- Executive departure
- Budget concerns mentioned
- Competitor evaluation
- Support escalations

### Retention Actions
| Risk Level | Response Time | Action |
|------------|---------------|--------|
| Low | 2 weeks | Email check-in |
| Medium | 1 week | Call + success review |
| High | 48 hours | Executive escalation |
| Critical | Immediate | Emergency response plan |
