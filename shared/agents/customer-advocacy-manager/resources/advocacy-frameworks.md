# Customer Advocacy Frameworks

## Advocacy Program Structure

### Program Elements
1. **Reference Program**: Referenceable customers
2. **Case Studies**: Written success stories
3. **Testimonials**: Quotes and reviews
4. **Advisory Board**: Customer council
5. **Community**: User forums, events
6. **Champions**: Internal advocates

## Reference Customer Criteria

### Qualifications
- Strong results/ROI achieved
- Positive relationship
- Willingness to participate
- Relevant to target segments
- Brand recognition value

### Tiering
| Tier | Participation | Benefits |
|------|---------------|----------|
| Platinum | All activities | Exclusive access, recognition |
| Gold | References + case study | Early access, events |
| Silver | References only | Thank you rewards |

## Case Study Template

```markdown
# [Customer Name]: [Headline Result]

## Challenge
[Business problem they faced]

## Solution
[How they used your product/service]

## Results
- [Metric 1]: X% improvement
- [Metric 2]: $X saved/earned
- [Metric 3]: Qualitative outcome

## Quote
> "[Powerful customer quote]"
> — [Name], [Title], [Company]

## About [Customer]
[Brief company description]
```

## Testimonial Collection

### Request Email
```
Subject: Quick favor - share your experience?

Hi [Name],

We're thrilled that [result they achieved]! Would you be willing to share a brief quote about your experience?

Just 2-3 sentences about:
- The challenge you faced
- How we helped
- The result

I can draft something for your review if that's easier.

Thanks!
```

### Quote Format
> "[Impact statement]"
> — [Name], [Title], [Company]

## Customer Advisory Board

### Structure
- 10-15 members
- Mix of segments/sizes
- Quarterly meetings (2 virtual, 1 in-person)
- 2-year terms

### Meeting Agenda
1. Business update (20 min)
2. Product roadmap preview (30 min)
3. Strategic discussion topic (45 min)
4. Open feedback (20 min)
5. Networking (30 min)

### Member Benefits
- Product influence
- Exclusive previews
- Executive networking
- Recognition
- Event invitations

## Community Building

### Engagement Activities
- User forums / Slack community
- Webinars and events
- User groups (local/virtual)
- Content contributions
- Beta programs

### Health Metrics
- Active members
- Engagement rate
- Content contributions
- Member satisfaction
- Referral generation

## Measuring Advocacy ROI

| Metric | Measurement |
|--------|-------------|
| Reference calls completed | Count/quarter |
| Case studies published | Count/year |
| Testimonials collected | Count |
| Community size | Members |
| Influenced pipeline | $$ attributed |
| Win rate with references | % comparison |
