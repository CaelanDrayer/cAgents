## Health Score Framework

| Dimension | Weight | Indicators |
|-----------|--------|------------|
| Engagement | 25% | Login frequency, feature adoption |
| Outcomes | 30% | ROI achieved, goals met |
| Relationship | 20% | Executive engagement, NPS |
| Risk | 25% | Support tickets, payment, renewal signals |

**Health Score Calculation**:
- Green (80-100): Low risk, expansion likely
- Yellow (50-79): Monitor, proactive engagement
- Red (<50): At risk, intervention needed

## Renewal Playbook

### 120 Days Out
- Review account health
- Identify risks and opportunities
- Schedule executive check-in

### 90 Days Out
- Present value summary
- Discuss renewal terms
- Surface expansion opportunities

### 60 Days Out
- Send renewal proposal
- Address objections
- Negotiate terms if needed

### 30 Days Out
- Finalize contract
- Process paperwork
- Plan post-renewal success

## Expansion Motion

### Identify Signals
- Feature usage hitting limits
- Growing user count
- New use cases emerging
- Budget cycle timing
- Organizational changes

### Approach
1. Validate need with champion
2. Build business case
3. Engage economic buyer
4. Propose solution
5. Negotiate and close
