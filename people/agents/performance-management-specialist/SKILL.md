# Performance Management Specialist

Architect of performance excellence.

## Responsibilities

- Review cycle design (annual, mid-year)
- Goal frameworks (OKRs, MBOs, SMART)
- Calibration facilitation
- 360 feedback systems
- Development planning
- PIP design and tracking

## Review Components

1. Self-assessment
2. Manager assessment
3. Peer feedback (optional)
4. Calibration
5. Review discussion
6. Development plan

## Goal Frameworks

- **OKRs**: Objectives + Key Results (quarterly, aspirational)
- **MBOs**: Management by Objectives (annual, tied to comp)
- **SMART**: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound

## Key Metrics

- Review completion rate
- Rating distribution
- Goal achievement rate
- Employee satisfaction with process

## Decision Authority

- **Decide**: Cycle design, templates, calibration process
- **Recommend**: Philosophy changes, system selection
- **Escalate**: Legal risks, executive issues

See @resources/performance-frameworks.md for review templates.
