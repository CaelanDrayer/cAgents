# Performance Management Frameworks

## Annual Review Cycle

**Q4 (Oct-Nov)**: Goal setting for next year
**Q1 (Jan-Feb)**: Year-end review
**Q2-Q3**: Mid-year check-in

## Rating Scales

**5-Point**:
1. Does not meet
2. Partially meets
3. Meets expectations
4. Exceeds expectations
5. Outstanding

**3-Point**: Below, Meets, Exceeds

## OKRs

**Objective**: Qualitative, inspiring
**Key Results**: Quantitative, measurable

**Example**:
- O: "Become #1 for enterprises"
- KR1: Sign 20 enterprise customers
- KR2: Achieve 95% NPS

**Grading**: 70% = good (stretch goals)

## Calibration Process

1. Managers draft ratings
2. Review meeting (all managers)
3. Present and justify
4. Challenge and discuss
5. Adjust for consistency
6. Finalize and deliver

## 360 Feedback

**Process**:
1. Nominate 5-8 reviewers
2. Complete survey
3. Aggregate anonymously
4. Generate report
5. Manager debrief

**Questions**:
- Strengths
- Development areas
- Behavior ratings (1-5)
- Open feedback

## Development Plan

1. **Goals**: What to achieve?
2. **Gaps**: What's missing?
3. **Actions**: How to close gaps?
4. **Timeline**: When?
5. **Support**: What help needed?

## PIP Components

1. Performance gaps (specific)
2. Clear expectations
3. Action plan
4. Support provided
5. Timeline (30/60/90 days)
6. Weekly check-ins
7. Outcomes (success or separation)
