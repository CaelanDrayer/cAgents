# Employee Lifecycle Management

Detailed guidance for managing the complete employee journey from attraction through alumni engagement.

## Lifecycle Stages Overview

The employee lifecycle has seven stages. Each stage has distinct HR responsibilities, key metrics, and handoff points.

```
Attract -> Recruit -> Onboard -> Develop -> Retain -> Transition -> Alumni
```

| Stage | HR Focus | Primary Owner | Key Metric |
|-------|----------|---------------|------------|
| Attract | Employer brand, EVP | Talent Acquisition | Application rate |
| Recruit | Sourcing, selection | Talent Acquisition | Time to fill, quality of hire |
| Onboard | Integration, ramp-up | HR Manager | 90-day retention, time to productivity |
| Develop | Growth, performance | HR Manager + Manager | Promotion rate, skill growth |
| Retain | Engagement, satisfaction | HR Manager | Turnover rate, eNPS |
| Transition | Role changes, exits | HR Manager | Internal mobility rate |
| Alumni | Ongoing relationship | HR/Recruiting | Rehire rate, referral rate |

## Stage 1: Attract

### Employer Value Proposition (EVP)

An EVP should address five pillars:

1. **Compensation**: Base pay, bonuses, equity, total rewards
2. **Benefits**: Health, wellness, retirement, perks
3. **Career**: Growth paths, learning opportunities, mentorship
4. **Culture**: Values, work environment, team dynamics
5. **Purpose**: Company mission, social impact, meaningful work

### Employer Brand Health Check

Assess these annually:

| Dimension | How to Measure | Action if Low |
|-----------|---------------|---------------|
| Glassdoor rating | Check current rating | Address top complaints |
| Application volume | Applications per posting | Improve job descriptions, expand sourcing |
| Career page traffic | Web analytics | Refresh content, add employee stories |
| Social media engagement | Followers, interactions | Increase employee advocacy content |
| Offer acceptance rate | Accepted / Extended | Improve candidate experience, comp review |

## Stage 2: Recruit

### Structured Hiring Process

**Step 1: Role Definition**
- Job description with clear requirements (must-have vs nice-to-have)
- Compensation band approved by compensation team
- Interview panel selected and trained
- Scorecard created with evaluation criteria

**Step 2: Sourcing Strategy**
- Internal candidates first (post internally for 5 days minimum)
- Employee referral program activation
- External sourcing channels based on role type
- Diversity sourcing goals defined

**Step 3: Interview Process**

| Round | Format | Focus | Duration |
|-------|--------|-------|----------|
| Screen | Phone/Video | Basic qualifications, motivation | 30 min |
| Technical | Video/In-person | Skills assessment, domain expertise | 60 min |
| Behavioral | Video/In-person | Culture fit, collaboration, leadership | 45 min |
| Final | In-person | Team fit, mutual evaluation | 60 min |

**Step 4: Decision and Offer**
- Debrief within 24 hours of final interview
- Consensus-based decision using scorecard
- Offer within 48 hours of decision
- Candidate follow-up within 2 business days of offer

### Candidate Experience Standards

- Acknowledge every application within 48 hours
- Provide status updates at least every 2 weeks
- Respond to candidate questions within 1 business day
- Notify rejected candidates with respectful, timely communication
- Collect candidate experience feedback via survey

## Stage 3: Onboard

### The 4 C's of Onboarding

| C | Focus | Examples |
|---|-------|---------|
| **Compliance** | Legal and policy requirements | I-9, tax forms, handbook acknowledgment |
| **Clarification** | Role expectations | Job description review, 30/60/90 plan, success metrics |
| **Culture** | Values and norms | Company history, team introductions, cultural norms |
| **Connection** | Relationships | Buddy program, manager 1:1, cross-team introductions |

### Onboarding Effectiveness Measurement

| Metric | When to Measure | Target |
|--------|----------------|--------|
| New hire satisfaction survey | Day 7, Day 30, Day 90 | > 4.0/5.0 |
| Time to first contribution | Tracked by manager | Role-dependent |
| Buddy program engagement | Day 30 survey | > 90% had 3+ meetings |
| Training completion | Day 30 | 100% required, > 80% optional |
| 90-day retention rate | Day 90 | > 95% |
| Manager satisfaction with new hire | Day 90 | > 4.0/5.0 |

## Stage 4: Develop

### Performance Management Cycle

| Activity | Frequency | Participants | Output |
|----------|-----------|-------------|--------|
| Goal setting | Quarterly or semi-annually | Manager + Employee | Documented goals with OKRs |
| Continuous feedback | Ongoing (minimum monthly) | Manager + Employee | 1:1 notes, feedback log |
| Mid-year review | Semi-annually | Manager + Employee | Progress assessment, goal adjustment |
| Annual review | Annually | Manager + Employee + HR | Performance rating, development plan |
| Calibration | Annually | Leadership + HR | Consistent ratings across teams |

### Individual Development Plan (IDP) Template

Each employee should have an IDP that includes:

1. **Current Role Assessment**: Strengths, areas for growth, performance level
2. **Career Aspirations**: Short-term (1 year) and long-term (3-5 year) goals
3. **Skill Gaps**: Identified gaps between current state and career goals
4. **Development Actions**: Specific activities to close gaps

| Development Type | Examples | Time Investment |
|-----------------|----------|-----------------|
| On-the-Job (70%) | Stretch assignments, project leads, cross-functional work | Integrated into daily work |
| Social Learning (20%) | Mentorship, coaching, peer learning, communities of practice | 2-4 hours/month |
| Formal Training (10%) | Courses, certifications, conferences, workshops | 40 hours/year minimum |

### Career Pathways

Define clear pathways for common roles:

```
Individual Contributor Track:
  Junior -> Mid -> Senior -> Staff -> Principal

Management Track:
  Team Lead -> Manager -> Sr Manager -> Director -> VP

Specialist Track:
  Specialist -> Sr Specialist -> Expert -> Fellow
```

Each level should have documented:
- Required competencies and skills
- Expected scope of impact
- Compensation band
- Typical tenure before progression

## Stage 5: Retain

### Retention Risk Assessment

Assess retention risk quarterly for each team:

| Risk Factor | Indicators | Mitigation |
|------------|-----------|-----------|
| Compensation gap | Below market median | Market adjustment, equity refresh |
| Career stagnation | 2+ years in same role, no growth | Development plan, stretch assignment |
| Manager relationship | Low 1:1 satisfaction, skip-level concerns | Manager coaching, potential transfer |
| Workload/burnout | High overtime, PTO not taken, declining output | Workload review, mandatory time off |
| Cultural misalignment | Low engagement scores, reduced participation | Culture discussion, team building |
| External opportunities | LinkedIn activity spike, referral decline | Stay interview, counter-offer strategy |

### Stay Interview Framework

Conduct stay interviews for high performers and high-risk employees:

**Core Questions**:
1. What do you look forward to each day at work?
2. What are you learning here? What do you want to learn?
3. Why do you stay at this company?
4. When was the last time you thought about leaving? What prompted it?
5. What can I do to make your experience better?
6. What would tempt you to leave?
7. What talents are not being used in your current role?

**Follow-Up Protocol**:
- Document responses (confidentially)
- Create action items within 1 week
- Follow up on commitments within 30 days
- Track trends across the team

## Stage 6: Transition

### Internal Mobility Process

| Step | Action | Timeline |
|------|--------|----------|
| 1. Interest | Employee expresses interest in internal move | Anytime |
| 2. Discussion | Manager and HR discuss with employee | Within 1 week |
| 3. Assessment | Skills assessment for target role | Within 2 weeks |
| 4. Application | Formal application to internal posting | Per posting window |
| 5. Interview | Abbreviated interview process | Within 2 weeks |
| 6. Decision | Hiring manager decision with HR input | Within 1 week |
| 7. Transition | Knowledge transfer and role change | 2-4 weeks |

### Exit Interview Analysis

Track and analyze exit interview data for patterns:

| Category | Questions to Track | Action Threshold |
|----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Manager | Rating of direct manager | < 3.5/5.0 average |
| Growth | Perceived career growth opportunities | < 3.0/5.0 average |
| Compensation | Satisfaction with total rewards | < 3.0/5.0 average |
| Culture | Alignment with company values | < 3.5/5.0 average |
| Workload | Work-life balance satisfaction | < 3.0/5.0 average |

Analyze trends quarterly. If any category falls below threshold for a specific team or department, escalate to leadership with recommendations.

## Stage 7: Alumni

### Alumni Engagement Program

Maintain relationships with former employees:

- **Alumni Network**: LinkedIn group or platform for former employees
- **Boomerang Program**: Fast-track rehiring process for former high performers
- **Referral Eligibility**: Former employees can participate in referral program for 12 months
- **Knowledge Base**: Maintain access to non-sensitive company updates and newsletters
- **Events**: Invite alumni to company events and celebrations annually

### Alumni Metrics

| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Rehire rate | > 5% of annual hires | Track former employee rehires |
| Referral rate from alumni | > 10% of referrals | Track referral source |
| Alumni network size | > 60% of voluntary departures | Track opt-in rate |
| Glassdoor review sentiment | > 3.5 stars from former employees | Monitor quarterly |
