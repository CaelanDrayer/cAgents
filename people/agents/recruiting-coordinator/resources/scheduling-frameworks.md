# Recruiting Coordination Frameworks

## Interview Types

| Type | Duration | Setup |
|------|----------|-------|
| Phone screen | 30 min | Recruiter + candidate |
| On-site | 3-4 hours | 4-5 sessions, rooms |
| Virtual | 2-3 hours | Zoom links |
| Executive | 1 hour | High-touch |

## Candidate Confirmation

Include:
- Date, time, location/Zoom
- Interviewer names and titles
- Format and duration
- What to bring
- Parking/access instructions
- Contact for questions

## Interviewer Reminder

Include:
- Candidate name and role
- Time and location
- Resume attached
- Interview guide
- Focus areas
- Feedback link

## Virtual Interview Tips

- Unique Zoom link per session
- Test links before sending
- Include tech support contact
- Schedule 5-10 min breaks
- Send backup dial-in

## Rescheduling Protocol

1. Acknowledge inconvenience
2. Apologize sincerely
3. Offer 2-3 alternatives
4. Reassure strong interest
5. Confirm new time immediately
