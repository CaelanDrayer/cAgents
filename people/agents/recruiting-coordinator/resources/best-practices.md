# Best Practices: Recruiting Coordinator

> Design principles, patterns, and frameworks that guide high-quality recruiting coordination, interview scheduling, and candidate operations work.

## Design Principles

- **Candidate Experience is Determined by Details**: Candidates judge companies by how they're treated in logistics — response time, scheduling ease, preparation quality, and follow-through; every detail the coordinator touches shapes employer perception.
- **Speed Signals Respect**: Slow scheduling is disrespectful of candidate time and signals organizational disorganization; candidate ghosting and offer declines are often caused by slow operational execution, not compensation.
- **Proactive Communication Prevents Friction**: Sending confirmations, prep materials, and day-of reminders before candidates ask for them reduces anxiety and no-shows dramatically.
- **ATS Discipline Enables Team Visibility**: Real-time ATS updates enable recruiters to manage pipelines, make decisions, and provide accurate status updates; coordinator ATS hygiene is a team capability.
- **Standardize the Repeatable**: Interview scheduling, confirmation emails, and feedback request workflows are repeatable; standardize templates and processes to free up cognitive capacity for exception handling.
- **Interviewer Experience Matters**: Coordinators who manage interviewer calendars with respect, provide clear agendas, and send prep materials on time build goodwill that translates to better hiring outcomes.
- **Logistics Accuracy Prevents Last-Minute Crises**: Confirming every detail (video link, room, phone number, time zone) 24 hours in advance prevents the day-of scrambles that damage candidate experience.

## Key Patterns & Frameworks

- **3-Day Scheduling Commitment**: From scheduling request to confirmed interview, target ≤3 business days; response to candidate email within 4 business hours; longer windows signal disorganization.
- **Time Zone Confirmation Protocol**: For every virtual interview, confirm the time zone explicitly in the initial scheduling email and in the calendar invite; time zone errors are among the most common and preventable scheduling failures.
- **Prep Materials Package**: Send to candidate 24 hours before interview — who they'll be meeting (name, title, photo), interview format (behavioral, technical, presentation), how to join, parking/building instructions, and what to bring. Reduces anxiety, improves interview quality.
- **Interviewer Agenda Packets**: For each interview, send the interviewer their assigned competency/questions, the candidate's resume, their 15-minute reminder, and the debrief time slot; interviewers who are prepared produce better assessments.
- **Rescheduling Protocol**: Establish rescheduling SLA (reschedule within 24 hours, maintain same day-of-week when possible, limit to one reschedule per interviewer per candidate); document all rescheduling events in ATS.
- **ATS Stage Management System**: Update candidate stage in ATS at every transition — scheduled, completed, feedback received, decision made; never let ATS fall more than 24 hours behind.
- **Feedback Collection Workflow**: Send feedback form link to interviewers immediately after interview completion; second request at 24 hours if not received; escalate to recruiter at 48 hours; track completion rates by interviewer.
- **Virtual Interview Logistics Checklist**: Video platform link tested, calendar invite sent to candidate and all interviewers, time zone confirmed, technical backup plan (phone number) provided, day-of reminder sent morning of.

## Domain Concepts & Terminology

### Scheduling Operations
- **Interview Panel**: Group of interviewers for a single candidate visit; typically 3-6 interviews for professional roles, sequenced by increasing seniority
- **Back-to-Back Schedule**: Consecutive interviews with different interviewers in a single session; efficient for candidates, requires careful buffer time management
- **Debrief Session**: Post-interview meeting where interviewers compare assessments; coordinator schedules this as part of the interview loop, typically 30-60 minutes
- **Time Zone Math**: Converting interview times across time zones; the most common source of day-of scheduling errors; always express time zone explicitly in all communications
- **Scheduling Software**: Tools that streamline interview scheduling (Calendly, ModernLoop, GoodTime, Prelude); coordinators typically manage these platforms

### Candidate Communications
- **Confirmation Email**: Sent immediately after interview is scheduled; includes date, time (with time zone), format, join instructions, interviewer names, and what to expect
- **Reminder**: Day-before confirmation reiterating logistics and providing last-minute preparation tips; reduces no-shows by 15-20%
- **Disposition Communication**: Notification to candidate of their advancement or rejection; advancing messages from recruiter; declinations can be automated for early stages
- **Candidate Status Update**: Proactive communication during delays in the hiring process; one of the highest-impact candidate experience touchpoints — candidates who know why there's a delay rate their experience significantly higher than those left in silence

### ATS Operations
- **Stage Management**: Moving candidates through ATS pipeline stages (applied → screened → phone interview → on-site → offer → hired/rejected) in real time
- **Candidate Profile**: ATS record containing resume, interview notes, scorecards, communications history, and stage history for each candidate
- **Interview Activity Logging**: Recording interview completion, scorecard receipt, and debrief notes in ATS for pipeline visibility and EEOC compliance
- **Disposition Tracking**: Recording decline decisions in ATS with decline reason codes; required for EEOC adverse action analysis and reporting
- **Reporting**: ATS-generated metrics on pipeline volume, stage conversion, time-at-stage, and diversity data; coordinator maintains data quality that makes reporting valid

### Logistics & Tools
- **Video Interview Platform**: Software for remote interviews (Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Spark Hire); coordinator tests and manages links and licenses
- **Calendar Integration**: ATS and scheduling tool integration with interviewer calendars (Google Calendar, Outlook) to check availability and send invites automatically
- **Room Booking**: Conference room reservation system for in-person interviews; coordinator manages this to ensure availability without conflicts
- **Interview Guide**: Document containing standardized questions for an interviewer aligned to their assigned competency; created by recruiter, distributed by coordinator

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- **Scheduling Via Long Email Chains**: Back-and-forth availability emails with candidates and interviewers — use scheduling software or provide 3 concrete time slot options to minimize rounds.
- **Missing Time Zone Confirmation**: Assuming both parties know the time zone from the calendar invite; explicitly state the time zone in every scheduling email and confirmation.
- **Late Prep Materials**: Sending interview prep materials the morning of the interview; candidates who receive materials 24 hours in advance arrive more prepared and rate their experience significantly higher.
- **Feedback Collection Neglect**: Not following up on missing interviewer feedback; delayed feedback collection delays decisions and signals that recruiter feedback accountability is low.
- **ATS Lagging**: Updating ATS records weekly rather than in real time; prevents recruiter pipeline visibility, delays decision-making, and creates EEOC compliance gaps.
- **No Rescheduling Protocol**: Handling rescheduling requests ad-hoc without standards; leads to extended delays and multiple rounds of availability checking.
- **Overloading Interviewer Schedules**: Scheduling the same interviewer for multiple same-day interviews without adequate buffer time; leads to rushed feedback and interviewer burnout.

## Quality Indicators

- **Time-to-Schedule**: Days from scheduling request to confirmed interview ≤3 business days; response to scheduling request within 4 hours
- **Day-of No-Show Rate**: <3% of scheduled interviews result in no-show or last-minute cancellation; tracked separately for candidates and interviewers
- **Rescheduling Rate**: <15% of interviews require rescheduling after initial confirmation; high rates indicate availability mismatch or communication quality issues
- **Feedback Collection Timeliness**: 85% of interviewer scorecards received within 24 hours of interview completion; 100% within 48 hours
- **Candidate Satisfaction Score**: Candidate NPS for scheduling/coordination experience ≥40; measured in post-interview survey
- **ATS Data Currency**: ≤24 hours lag between event (interview completed, decision made) and ATS stage update; measured in random weekly audit
- **Prep Materials Delivery Rate**: 95%+ of candidates receive confirmation and prep materials ≥24 hours before their interview

## Collaboration Touchpoints

- **With Recruiter**: Primary working relationship; coordinator handles scheduling, communications, and ATS hygiene so recruiter can focus on sourcing, assessing, and closing; real-time communication on pipeline changes is essential.
- **With Talent Acquisition Manager**: TA manager sets scheduling SLAs and prioritization standards; coordinator escalates capacity constraints and process bottlenecks.
- **With Hiring Managers and Interviewers**: Coordinator manages interviewer calendars and prep communications; building good working relationships with frequent interviewers makes scheduling faster and feedback quality higher.
- **With HR Ops Specialist**: ATS configuration and integration issues that affect scheduling workflows require HR ops involvement; coordinator surfaces operational friction points.
- **With Onboarding Specialist**: Offer-to-start coordination bridges recruiting and onboarding; coordinator ensures accurate start date documentation and recruiter handoff completeness.
