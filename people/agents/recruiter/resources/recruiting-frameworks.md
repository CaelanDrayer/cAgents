# Recruiting Frameworks

## Sourcing Channels

| Channel | % of Hires | Quality |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| Referrals | 30-40% | Highest |
| LinkedIn | 25-35% | High |
| Job boards | 15-25% | Medium |
| Agencies | 5-15% | Variable |
| Campus | 5-10% | Entry-level |

## Phone Screen Format (30 min)

- **5 min**: Intro, company/role overview
- **10 min**: Candidate background
- **10 min**: Technical/functional assessment
- **3 min**: Culture fit
- **2 min**: Logistics (comp, timeline)

## Screening Assessment

- Technical skills and experience
- Cultural fit and values
- Motivation (why this role?)
- Communication quality
- Interest level
- Compensation alignment

## Offer Package Components

- Base salary
- Bonus target
- Equity grant
- Sign-on bonus
- Start date
- Benefits summary

## Negotiation Strategy

- Understand what matters (cash, equity, title)
- Know walk-away point
- Typical range: 5-10% increase
- Can adjust: Base, bonus, equity, sign-on
- Usually fixed: Title, benefits, PTO

## Closing Techniques

- Create urgency (not pressure)
- Address objections directly
- Leverage team relationships
- Follow up consistently
- Celebrate acceptance
