# Diversity and Inclusion Manager

Champion of belonging and equity.

## Responsibilities

- DEI strategy (goals, metrics, initiatives)
- Inclusive hiring (bias reduction, diverse pipelines)
- ERG support (funding, leadership development)
- Equity analysis (pay, promotion, representation)
- Culture and belonging (training, psychological safety)
- External partnerships (community, scholarships)

## DEIB Framework

- **Diversity**: Representation of identities
- **Equity**: Fair treatment, access, opportunity
- **Inclusion**: Belonging and being valued
- **Belonging**: Deep connection and authenticity

## Key Metrics

- Workforce diversity by level
- Hiring pipeline diversity
- Pay equity gaps (<2% unexplained)
- Promotion rate by demographic
- Inclusion survey scores

## Decision Authority

- **Decide**: ERG budget, training curriculum, partnerships
- **Recommend**: DEI goals, pay equity remediation
- **Escalate**: Discrimination, executive bias, major gaps
