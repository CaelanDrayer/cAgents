# DEI Frameworks

## Representation Goals

**Metrics to Track**:
- Workforce diversity (% by demographic, level, function)
- Leadership diversity (VP+, Director+, Manager+)
- Pipeline diversity (candidates, offers, hires)
- Retention/turnover by demographic
- Promotion rates by demographic

**Example Goals**:
- "40% women in leadership by 2026"
- "15% Black and Latinx representation by 2027"
- "50% diverse interview slates"

## Inclusive Hiring Practices

### Bias Reduction
- Blind resume screening
- Structured interviews (same questions, rubrics)
- Diverse interview panels
- Inclusive job descriptions
- Salary range transparency

### Diverse Pipelines
- Partner with diversity orgs
- HBCU and HSI campus recruiting
- Diversity referral bonuses
- Diversity-focused job boards
- Remove unnecessary requirements

### Diverse Slates
- Interview at least 1 diverse candidate
- Target 50% diverse slate
- Track and report slate diversity

## Employee Resource Groups

**Common ERGs**:
- Women's network
- Black/African American
- Latinx/Hispanic
- Asian and Pacific Islander
- LGBTQ+ and allies
- Veterans and military
- Disability and neurodiversity
- Parents and caregivers

**ERG Support**:
- Funding ($2-10k/year per ERG)
- Leadership development
- Strategic alignment
- Executive sponsorship
- Event support

## Pay Equity Analysis

**Process**:
1. Analyze by gender and race
2. Control for role, level, location, performance
3. Identify unexplained gaps (>2%)
4. Remediate gaps
5. Publish findings

## Inclusive Leadership Training

**Topics**:
- Unconscious bias awareness
- Microaggressions recognition
- Allyship (amplify, sponsor, share power)
- Psychological safety
- Inclusive meetings

**Allyship Actions**:
- Amplify: Credit underrepresented voices
- Sponsor: Advocate for advancement
- Share power: Create space for others
- Educate: Learn without burdening others
