# Compensation Frameworks

## Compensation Philosophy

**Market Positioning**:
- 50th percentile: Market median
- 75th percentile: Market competitive
- 90th percentile: Market leading

**Pay Mix by Stage**:
- Early startup: Lower base, higher equity
- Growth stage: Competitive base, moderate equity, bonus
- Public company: Market-rate base, RSUs, cash bonus

**Internal Equity**:
- Fair pay relative to peers
- Address compression issues
- Promotion increase: 10-15% typical

## Benchmarking Process

1. Identify comparable companies (size, stage, industry, geo)
2. Match roles to benchmark job descriptions
3. Pull market data (25th, 50th, 75th, 90th percentile)
4. Apply geographic differentials
5. Determine target percentile
6. Build or update salary ranges

**Data Sources**:
- Radford (tech industry standard)
- Mercer, Aon, Willis Towers Watson
- Payscale, Salary.com
- Glassdoor, Levels.fyi (crowdsourced)

## Job Leveling

**IC Track**: IC1 → IC2 → IC3 → Senior → Staff → Principal
**Manager Track**: Manager → Sr Manager → Director → Sr Director → VP → SVP

## Offer Package Design

1. Determine role level
2. Look up salary range
3. Position in range based on:
   - Experience and skills
   - Market competition
   - Internal equity
   - Budget constraints
4. Add bonus target
5. Add equity grant
6. Include benefits summary
7. Get approvals

**Equity Grant Ranges**:
| Level | Equity Range |
|-------|--------------|
| Entry IC | 0.01-0.05% |
| Mid IC | 0.05-0.15% |
| Senior/Manager | 0.10-0.30% |
| Director | 0.20-0.50% |
| VP | 0.30-1.00% |
| C-Level | 0.50-5.00% |

## Pay Equity Analysis

**Internal Equity**:
- Compare pay for same role/level
- Identify unexplained gaps
- Address compression

**External Competitiveness**:
- Compare to market benchmarks
- Identify flight risk roles
- Prioritize adjustments

**Diversity Pay Equity**:
- Analyze by gender, race
- Remediate unexplained gaps
- Publish findings (where required)

## Annual Compensation Cycle

**Q1 - Planning**:
- Update market benchmarks
- Analyze pay equity
- Propose merit budget

**Q2 - Execution**:
- Performance reviews
- Calibration
- Merit increases and promotions
- Equity refreshes

**Q3 - Monitoring**:
- Track offer acceptance
- Monitor turnover
- Update market data

**Q4 - Year-End**:
- Bonus payouts
- Year-end equity grants
- Plan next cycle
