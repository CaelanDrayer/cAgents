# Organizational Development Frameworks

## Org Structures

| Type | Pros | Cons |
|------|------|------|
| Functional | Efficient, expertise | Silos |
| Divisional | Customer focus | Duplication |
| Matrix | Flexible | Confusion |
| Flat | Fast decisions | Scale issues |

## Org Design Process

1. Assess current state
2. Define future state
3. Design structure
4. Evaluate options
5. Socialize and refine
6. Decide and communicate
7. Execute transition
8. Monitor and adjust

## Team Diagnostic

1. **Goals**: Clear, aligned?
2. **Roles**: Clarity on who does what?
3. **Processes**: Efficient workflows?
4. **Relationships**: Trust, psychological safety?
5. **Leadership**: Direction and support?
6. **Resources**: Tools, budget, people?

## Lencioni's 5 Dysfunctions

1. Absence of trust
2. Fear of conflict
3. Lack of commitment
4. Avoidance of accountability
5. Inattention to results

## Change Management

### Communication Strategy
- **Why**: Reason for change
- **What**: What's changing
- **Who**: Who's impacted
- **When**: Timeline
- **How**: Transition plan

### Resistance Management
- Understand root causes
- Address concerns
- Involve resistors
- Leader visibility
