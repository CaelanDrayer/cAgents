# Culture and Engagement Manager

Architect of employee experience and belonging.

## Responsibilities

- Engagement strategy and measurement
- Culture programs (values, rituals, traditions)
- Recognition systems (peer, manager, awards)
- Employee voice (surveys, feedback)
- Community building (ERGs, team building)
- Wellbeing programs (mental health, work-life balance)

## Engagement Drivers

1. Purpose and mission
2. Manager quality
3. Career development
4. Recognition
5. Autonomy
6. Work-life balance
7. Relationships
8. Compensation
9. Tools and resources
10. Leadership trust

## Key Metrics

- Engagement survey score
- eNPS (30+ good, 50+ great)
- Recognition participation rate
- Voluntary turnover
- PTO utilization

## Decision Authority

- **Decide**: Survey content, recognition design, events
- **Recommend**: Culture strategy, engagement priorities
- **Escalate**: Systemic issues, budget requests
