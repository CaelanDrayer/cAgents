# Engagement Frameworks

## Engagement Measurement

**Annual Survey**: 40-60 questions, comprehensive view
**Pulse Surveys**: 5-10 questions, quarterly/monthly
**eNPS**: "How likely to recommend working here?"
- Promoters (9-10), Passives (7-8), Detractors (0-6)
- eNPS = % Promoters - % Detractors

**Focus Groups**: Qualitative insights
**Exit Interviews**: Why people leave

## Survey Action Planning

1. **Share Results**: Transparent communication
2. **Identify Focus Areas**: Company and team priorities
3. **Action Plans**: Managers create with teams
4. **Follow-through**: Track progress, show impact
5. **Accountability**: Hold managers accountable

## Recognition Systems

### Peer Recognition
- Platform: Bonusly, Kudos, Slack tools
- Points/kudos with public message
- Redemption for rewards
- Ongoing, real-time, low friction

### Manager Recognition
- Regular 1:1 appreciation
- Public recognition in meetings
- Written thank-you notes
- Spot bonuses ($500-$2k)

### Milestone Recognition
- Work anniversaries (1, 3, 5, 10 years)
- Life events (weddings, babies)
- Promotions and achievements
- Project completions

### Awards Programs
- Values awards (quarterly/annual)
- Innovation awards
- Peer-nominated awards
- Leadership awards

## Culture Programs

### Company Rituals
- Weekly all-hands
- Quarterly offsites
- Monthly team lunches
- Annual kick-off
- Demo days

### Team Building
- Team offsites
- Social events
- Interest-based groups
- Virtual social (remote)

### Heritage Celebrations
- Heritage months
- Cultural celebrations
- Company milestones
- Personal milestones

## Wellbeing Programs

### Mental Health
- EAP (5-10 sessions)
- Mental health days
- Wellness apps
- Training on burnout

### Work-Life Balance
- Flexible work
- PTO encouragement
- No after-hours email norms
- Meeting-free time

### Burnout Prevention
- Workload management
- Manager training
- Sabbaticals
- Company-wide recharge days

## Remote Culture

**Challenges**:
- Isolation and loneliness
- Communication gaps
- Onboarding difficulty
- Meeting fatigue

**Solutions**:
- Virtual coffee chats
- Async communication
- In-person offsites
- Virtual social events
- Over-communication
