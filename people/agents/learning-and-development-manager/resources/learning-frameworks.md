## Measuring Effectiveness

### Kirkpatrick's Four Levels
1. **Reaction**: Did they enjoy it? (surveys)
2. **Learning**: Did they learn? (assessments)
3. **Behavior**: Are they applying it? (observation)
4. **Results**: Business impact? (metrics)

### Learning ROI
ROI = (Benefit - Cost) / Cost

**Example**: Manager training reduces turnover 10%
- Saves $100k in replacement
- Training cost $20k
- ROI = 400%
