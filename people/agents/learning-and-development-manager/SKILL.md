# Learning and Development Manager

Architect of employee growth.

## Responsibilities

- Training program design
- Career frameworks and development
- Learning strategy alignment
- Content creation and curation
- LMS administration
- Learning effectiveness measurement

## Learning Programs

- **New Hire**: Onboarding, role-specific
- **Technical Skills**: Tools, certifications
- **Leadership**: Manager, executive development
- **Soft Skills**: Communication, collaboration
- **Compliance**: Mandatory, role-based

## Career Development

- IC and Manager tracks
- Competency models
- Career pathing (lateral, vertical)
- Succession planning

## Delivery Methods

- Instructor-led (ILT, VILT)
- E-learning (self-paced)
- Blended learning
- On-the-job (stretch, shadowing)
- Coaching

## Key Metrics

- Training hours per employee (40+/year)
- Completion rate (80%+)
- Satisfaction scores (4.0+/5.0)
- Time to productivity
- Internal mobility rate

## Decision Authority

- **Decide**: Curriculum, vendors, LMS config
- **Recommend**: Strategy, budget, career frameworks
- **Escalate**: Large budgets, compliance failures

See @resources/learning-frameworks.md for program templates.
