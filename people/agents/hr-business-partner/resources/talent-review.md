# Talent Assessment & Review

Reference for talent review processes, calibration, and development planning.

## Talent Review Process

### Annual Cycle

| Quarter | Activity | Output |
|---------|----------|--------|
| Q1 | Goal setting, development plans | Individual OKRs, IDPs |
| Q2 | Mid-year check-in, progress review | Calibrated feedback |
| Q3 | Talent review prep, 9-box assessment | Talent grid, succession plans |
| Q4 | Performance review, compensation cycle | Ratings, comp adjustments |

### Pre-Review Preparation

**Manager Inputs**:
1. Performance rating (Exceeds, Meets, Below Expectations)
2. Potential assessment (High, Medium, Low)
3. Key accomplishments (3-5 with impact metrics)
4. Development areas (2-3 specific and actionable)
5. Flight risk assessment (Low, Medium, High)
6. Succession readiness (for direct reports)

**HRBP Inputs**:
1. Cross-team calibration data
2. Engagement survey results
3. Compensation equity analysis
4. Diversity and inclusion metrics
5. Historical performance trends

## Performance Assessment

### Rating Scale

| Rating | Description | Distribution Target |
|--------|-------------|-------------------|
| Exceptional | Consistently exceeds in all areas, outsized impact | 5-10% |
| Exceeds | Exceeds expectations in most areas | 20-25% |
| Meets | Consistently meets all expectations | 50-60% |
| Developing | Meets some expectations, gaps in others | 10-15% |
| Below | Does not meet expectations | 0-5% |

### Calibration Session Format

**Duration**: 2-3 hours per team (8-15 employees)

**Participants**: Direct manager, skip-level manager, HRBP, peer managers

**Process**:
1. Manager presents each employee (2-3 min each)
2. Peer managers provide cross-functional feedback
3. Discuss and calibrate ratings within the session
4. Identify talent actions (promotions, development, PIPs)
5. HRBP records decisions and rationale

**Calibration Questions**:
- Would you rehire this person today?
- If they left tomorrow, how hard would it be to replace them?
- Are they in the right role? Would they thrive elsewhere?
- What is holding them back from the next level?

## Potential Assessment

### Indicators of High Potential

| Indicator | Evidence |
|-----------|----------|
| Learning agility | Quickly masters new domains, seeks feedback |
| Drive for results | Consistently delivers beyond scope |
| Strategic thinking | Sees big picture, connects dots across teams |
| Influence | Inspires others without authority, builds coalitions |
| Resilience | Recovers from setbacks, maintains composure under pressure |
| Self-awareness | Accurately assesses own strengths and weaknesses |

### Potential vs. Performance Distinction

```
Performance = What they have delivered (past/present)
Potential = What they could deliver in a bigger role (future)

High Performance + High Potential = Future leader (invest heavily)
High Performance + Low Potential = Key contributor (reward, retain)
Low Performance + High Potential = Misaligned (new role, coaching)
Low Performance + Low Potential = Performance manage (PIP or exit)
```

## Flight Risk Assessment

### Risk Indicators

| Signal | Weight | Source |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Below-market compensation | High | Comp analysis |
| Limited growth opportunities | High | Career conversation |
| Manager relationship issues | High | Engagement survey, skip-levels |
| Life changes (relocation, family) | Medium | Manager awareness |
| LinkedIn activity spike | Medium | Observation |
| Engagement score decline | Medium | Survey data |
| Peer departures | Medium | Attrition data |
| Tenure at 2-year or 4-year mark | Low | HR data |

### Retention Actions by Risk Level

| Risk Level | Actions |
|-----------|---------|
| High | Immediate retention conversation, comp review, stretch assignment |
| Medium | Career development discussion, mentorship, recognition |
| Low | Standard development plan, maintain engagement |

## Talent Review Meeting Agenda

1. **Context Setting** (10 min): Business priorities, org changes, team dynamics
2. **Individual Reviews** (2-3 min each): Performance, potential, development, risk
3. **Calibration Discussion** (30 min): Align ratings, debate edge cases
4. **Action Planning** (20 min): Promotions, moves, PIPs, high-potential investments
5. **Summary and Next Steps** (10 min): Document decisions, assign owners
