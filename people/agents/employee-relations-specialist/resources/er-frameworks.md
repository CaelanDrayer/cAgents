# Employee Relations Frameworks

## Investigation Types

- Harassment (sexual, bullying, hostile environment)
- Discrimination (protected class)
- Retaliation (against complainant/whistleblower)
- Policy violations (theft, fraud, conduct)
- Workplace violence or threats

## Investigation Steps

1. **Intake**: Receive complaint, assess severity/urgency
2. **Planning**: Scope, witnesses, interview approach
3. **Interviews**: Complainant, accused, witnesses
4. **Evidence**: Documents, emails, Slack, footage
5. **Analysis**: Credibility, corroboration, policy check
6. **Findings**: Substantiated, unsubstantiated, inconclusive
7. **Remediation**: Discipline, training, policy changes
8. **Documentation**: File, findings memo, actions
9. **Follow-up**: Check in, monitor for retaliation

## Interview Best Practices

- Neutral location, private setting
- Explain purpose and confidentiality limits
- Ask open-ended questions
- Take detailed notes or record
- Ask for evidence
- Don't promise outcomes

## Credibility Assessment

- **Plausibility**: Does it make sense?
- **Consistency**: Consistent across interviews?
- **Corroboration**: Other evidence supports?
- **Motive**: Reason to lie or exaggerate?

## Remediation Actions

| Finding | Actions |
|---------|---------|
| Substantiated harassment | Termination, final warning, training |
| Substantiated violation | Warning, suspension, termination |
| Unsubstantiated | No action, preventive training |
| All cases | Monitor for retaliation |

## Conflict Mediation

**When to Mediate**:
- Interpersonal conflicts
- Team dynamics breakdown
- Communication issues
- NOT for: Harassment, discrimination, severe violations

**Process**:
1. Pre-mediation (meet separately)
2. Ground rules (respect, confidentiality)
3. Opening statements (no interruptions)
4. Discussion (clarify, find common ground)
5. Problem-solving (brainstorm solutions)
6. Agreement (document commitments)
7. Follow-up (2-4 weeks)

## Retention Management

### Stay Interviews
- Proactive with valued employees
- Questions: What keeps you? What would make you leave?
- Frequency: Annual or after key events

### Exit Interviews
- Understand why leaving
- Questions: Why leaving? What could we change?
- Aggregate data, protect individual responses

### Turnover Analysis
- Voluntary vs. involuntary
- Regrettable vs. non-regrettable
- Analyze by manager, department, demographics

## Employment Law Compliance

- **EEOC**: Discrimination protection
- **FLSA**: Overtime, minimum wage
- **FMLA**: Family/medical leave
- **ADA**: Disability accommodations
- **Whistleblower**: SOX, OSHA protections

## Documentation Standards

- Factual, objective, timely
- Avoid speculation
- Include dates, times, witnesses
- Store securely, maintain confidentiality
- Retain 7+ years
