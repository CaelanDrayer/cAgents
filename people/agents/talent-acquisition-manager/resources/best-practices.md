# Best Practices: Talent Acquisition Manager

> Design principles, patterns, and frameworks that guide high-quality talent acquisition strategy, recruiting team leadership, and hiring pipeline management work.

## Design Principles

- **TA is a Revenue Function**: Every unfilled role has a cost (vacant salary budget, lost productivity, delayed projects); frame talent acquisition as a revenue-enabling capability, not a cost center.
- **Strategy Before Tactics**: Channel mix, sourcing strategy, and employer brand investments must be grounded in an assessment of the talent market, role complexity, and organizational needs — not historical habit.
- **Process Quality Drives Outcome Quality**: The recruiting process (how quickly we move, how we assess, how we communicate) determines the quality of people we hire more than the size of the sourcing budget.
- **Hiring Manager Partnership is Non-Negotiable**: TA managers who don't invest in deep hiring manager relationships consistently fail to fill roles with the right candidates; alignment precedes execution.
- **Talent Market Intelligence Improves Decisions**: TA managers who understand supply, demand, compensation trends, and competitive recruiting landscape make better pipeline and offer decisions than those operating on intuition alone.
- **Pipeline Metrics Enable Accountability**: TA teams that track conversion rates, time-to-fill, and quality-of-hire can diagnose and improve performance; teams that don't are perpetually reactive.
- **Employer Brand Compounds Over Time**: Employer reputation (Glassdoor, LinkedIn, referral quality) takes years to build and can be damaged quickly; manage it as a strategic asset, not a marketing afterthought.

## Key Patterns & Frameworks

- **Recruiting Strategy Document**: For each major hiring initiative, produce a one-page strategy covering role prioritization, sourcing channels, timeline, interview process, compensation strategy, and diversity goals — prevents ad-hoc recruiting.
- **Pipeline Health Dashboard**: Real-time view of all open roles by stage — requisitions opened, candidates in pipeline, time-at-stage, conversion rates, and diversity metrics; the TA manager's primary management tool.
- **Hiring Manager Kickoff Protocol**: Structured intake meeting for every new requisition covering job definition, must-have vs. nice-to-have criteria, interview process design, timeline, budget, and diversity expectations.
- **Recruiting Capacity Model**: Mathematical model matching recruiter capacity (reqs per recruiter, hours per req stage) to hiring plan; prevents under-staffing TA and enables headcount planning.
- **Quality-of-Hire Measurement System**: Define role-specific success criteria at hire time, collect 90-day and 1-year performance and retention data, attribute back to sourcing channel and recruiter; the only true measure of recruiting quality.
- **Diverse Sourcing Strategy**: Systematically identify and activate 3-5 non-standard sourcing channels per role type (coding bootcamps, HBCU partnerships, veteran networks, diversity job boards, professional associations) in addition to standard channels.
- **Offer Approval Framework**: Tiered approval thresholds by deviation from midpoint (±10% auto-approve, ±20% manager approval, >20% exec + comp analyst review); balances speed with governance.
- **Recruiter Performance Framework**: Define individual recruiter goals by metrics that matter (offers accepted, quality-of-hire, hiring manager satisfaction, diverse placements) rather than activity metrics alone.

## Domain Concepts & Terminology

### TA Strategy
- **Recruiting Strategy**: Deliberate plan for how to source, attract, assess, and close candidates for a role or set of roles; includes channel mix, timeline, and success criteria
- **Talent Market Analysis**: Assessment of candidate supply (how many qualified candidates exist), demand (how many companies are competing for them), and compensation trends
- **Employer Brand**: Company's reputation as a place to work; reflected in Glassdoor ratings, LinkedIn Follower engagement, employee referral rates, and offer acceptance rates
- **EVP (Employer Value Proposition)**: The set of experiences and benefits that differentiate the company as an employer; the foundation of employer brand messaging
- **Candidate NPS**: Net Promoter Score collected from all candidates (advance and decline) measuring likelihood to recommend the company as an employer; key employer brand health metric

### Pipeline Management
- **Recruiting Funnel**: Top-of-funnel (sourced/applied) → Middle-of-funnel (screened/interviewed) → Bottom-of-funnel (offered/hired); conversion rates at each stage reveal efficiency
- **Requisition Load**: Number of open requisitions assigned to each recruiter; benchmark 15-20 reqs per recruiter (varies by role complexity and sourcing strategy)
- **Pipeline Coverage Ratio**: Number of qualified candidates at a funnel stage divided by number needed to advance to the next stage; healthy ratio = 3:1 for most stages
- **Time-to-Fill**: Days from requisition approval to accepted offer; benchmark 30-45 days for professional roles; longer for executive or specialized technical roles
- **Aging Req**: Requisition open longer than target time-to-fill without a clear path to close; requires strategic review and possible re-scoping
- **Slate**: Set of qualified candidates presented to a hiring manager for consideration; standard practice is 3-5 candidates before moving to interview stage

### Recruiting Operations
- **Recruiter Capacity**: Number of open requisitions a recruiter can work effectively simultaneously; varies by role complexity and sourcing intensity required
- **Sourcing Channels**: Methods for finding candidates — direct sourcing (LinkedIn, Boolean), job boards (Indeed, Glassdoor), employee referrals, agencies, events, university
- **ATS (Applicant Tracking System)**: Software managing end-to-end recruiting workflow; TA manager oversees ATS configuration, reporting, and data quality governance
- **Agency / RPO (Recruiting Process Outsourcing)**: External recruiting vendor; agency provides individual placement, RPO provides end-to-end process support; both have high cost, useful for surge capacity
- **Hiring Manager Satisfaction Score**: Survey measuring hiring manager perception of recruiter quality, process efficiency, and candidate quality; primary internal customer satisfaction metric

### Quality Metrics
- **Quality of Hire**: Composite score measuring new hire performance rating, retention at 90 days and 1 year, and hiring manager satisfaction; the ultimate measure of recruiting effectiveness
- **Interview-to-Offer Ratio**: Number of candidates interviewed to extend one offer; benchmark 3-4:1 for most professional roles; higher ratios may indicate assessment calibration issues
- **Offer Acceptance Rate**: % of extended offers accepted; below 80% indicates compensation misalignment or candidate experience issues
- **Source-of-Hire Attribution**: Which channel produced each hire; enables ROI analysis across sourcing investments
- **Diversity Pipeline Metric**: % of candidates from underrepresented groups at each stage of the funnel; identifies where diverse candidates are dropping out

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- **Spray-and-Pray Sourcing**: Sending high-volume generic InMail outreach to maximize outreach volume; response rates below 5% waste sourcing capacity and damage employer brand.
- **Hiring Manager Deference Without Standards**: Letting hiring managers define unrealistic role requirements, reject qualified candidates without behavioral evidence, or skip structured assessment; TA manager must maintain process standards.
- **Activity-Only Recruiter Metrics**: Evaluating recruiters on applications reviewed, calls made, or reqs worked rather than outcome quality (offers accepted, quality-of-hire); optimizes for activity not results.
- **Neglecting Employer Brand**: Treating employer brand as a marketing function rather than a TA function; TA manager is accountable for the candidate experience that produces the employer brand reputation.
- **No Workforce Planning Integration**: Reactive hiring that waits for requisitions to open rather than proactive pipeline building against a workforce plan; creates perpetual time-to-fill pressure.
- **Metrics Theater**: Reporting time-to-fill and offer acceptance rate but not quality-of-hire; optimizing the metrics you report while ignoring the ones that actually matter.
- **One-Size Sourcing Strategy**: Using the same LinkedIn + referral strategy for all roles regardless of talent scarcity, diversity needs, or seniority level.

## Quality Indicators

- **Team Time-to-Fill**: Organization-wide average time-to-fill at or below 45 days; executive roles within 60-90 days
- **Offer Acceptance Rate**: Team portfolio offer acceptance rate ≥85%; below 80% triggers strategy review
- **Quality-of-Hire Score**: 90-day new hire performance average ≥ "meets expectations" for 85%+ of hires; 1-year retention ≥90% for hires rated "meets or exceeds"
- **Diverse Slate Rate**: 75%+ of final interview rounds include ≥50% candidates from underrepresented groups
- **Hiring Manager Satisfaction**: Average hiring manager NPS ≥40; ≥4.0/5.0 on candidate quality and process efficiency
- **Recruiter Utilization**: Team average of 15-20 active reqs per recruiter; flag anyone above 25 or below 10 for capacity review
- **Employer Brand Health**: Glassdoor rating ≥4.0; offer acceptance rate trend improving; employee referral rate ≥20% of hires

## Collaboration Touchpoints

- **With HR Manager**: TA strategy and recruiter headcount decisions require HR manager alignment; TA manager owns execution, HR manager owns strategic prioritization and budget.
- **With Recruiter**: TA manager coaches and develops recruiters, sets process standards, approves offer exceptions, and removes obstacles; primary accountability for individual recruiter performance.
- **With Recruiting Coordinator**: TA manager ensures coordinator capacity matches recruiter workload; designs coordinator processes and SLAs.
- **With Compensation Analyst**: Offer competitiveness depends on current market data; coordinate on comp ranges for hard-to-fill roles, exception approvals, and equity refresh timing for candidates in process.
- **With Diversity and Inclusion Manager**: Diverse pipeline goals require systematic sourcing channel partnership; co-design diverse sourcing strategy, structured interview standards, and hiring funnel diversity monitoring.
