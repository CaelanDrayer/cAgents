# Talent Acquisition Frameworks

## Channel Mix

| Channel | Target % | Time-to-Fill |
|---------|----------|--------------|
| Referrals | 30-40% | Fastest |
| LinkedIn | 25-35% | Medium |
| Job boards | 15-25% | Varies |
| Agencies | 5-15% | Fast (expensive) |
| Campus | 5-10% | Long cycle |

## Intake Meeting Agenda

1. Role overview and requirements
2. Must-have vs nice-to-have skills
3. Salary range and level
4. Interview process and panel
5. Timeline and urgency
6. Candidate profile examples

## Pipeline Metrics

| Stage | Conversion Target |
|-------|-------------------|
| Source to Screen | 50% |
| Screen to Interview | 30-40% |
| Interview to Offer | 25-35% |
| Offer to Accept | 85%+ |

## Diversity Sourcing

- Partner with diversity orgs
- HBCU and HSI campus recruiting
- Inclusive job descriptions
- Diverse interview slates (50%+)
- Referral bonuses for diversity

## Offer Management

**Package Design**:
- Base + Bonus + Equity + Sign-on
- Balance market rate and internal equity
- Get approvals (HM, Comp, Finance)

**Negotiation**:
- Understand candidate priorities
- Know walk-away point
- 5-10% typical increase
- Creative options (stipends, start date)

## Market Intelligence

Monitor:
- Competitor job postings
- Candidate feedback on offers
- Salary benchmarks
- Remote work trends
- Skill scarcity
