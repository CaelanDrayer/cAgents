# HR Compliance Frameworks

## Key Employment Laws

| Law | Applies To | Requirement |
|-----|-----------|-------------|
| Title VII | 15+ employees | No discrimination (race, color, religion, sex, national origin) |
| ADA | 15+ employees | Disability accommodations |
| ADEA | 20+ employees | No age discrimination (40+) |
| FMLA | 50+ employees | 12 weeks unpaid leave |
| FLSA | All | Minimum wage, overtime |
| COBRA | 20+ employees | Health insurance continuation |
| OSHA | All | Workplace safety |

## Compliance Checklists

### Hiring
- [ ] Non-discriminatory practices
- [ ] I-9 within 3 days
- [ ] FCRA background check compliance
- [ ] Pay transparency (per state)
- [ ] No illegal interview questions

### Classification
- [ ] Exempt: Salary, duties test met
- [ ] Non-exempt: Hourly, overtime eligible
- [ ] Contractor vs employee test

### Leaves
- [ ] FMLA eligibility tracking
- [ ] ADA interactive process
- [ ] State leave compliance
- [ ] Military leave (USERRA)

### Terminations
- [ ] At-will documentation
- [ ] WARN Act (60 days notice, 100+)
- [ ] Final paycheck timing
- [ ] COBRA notification
- [ ] Unemployment response

## Record Retention

| Record Type | Retention |
|------------|-----------|
| Personnel files | 7+ years post-term |
| Payroll records | 3-7 years |
| I-9 forms | 3 years or 1 year post-term |
| Applications | 1-2 years |

## Audit Types

- **DOL**: Wage and hour, FMLA
- **EEOC**: Discrimination
- **OSHA**: Safety
- **IRS**: Payroll taxes, classification
- **State**: Unemployment, workers' comp

## Employee Handbook

**Required Policies**:
- Equal employment opportunity
- Anti-harassment/discrimination
- Reasonable accommodations
- Leave policies
- At-will disclaimer

## Compliance Calendar
