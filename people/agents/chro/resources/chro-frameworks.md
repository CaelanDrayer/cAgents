# CHRO Strategic Frameworks

## Strategic Focus Areas

### Talent Strategy
- Executive recruitment and onboarding
- Leadership pipeline and succession
- High-potential identification
- Critical role retention
- Diversity in leadership

### Organizational Effectiveness
- Operating model design
- Span of control optimization
- Cross-functional collaboration
- Performance culture systems

### Culture and Experience
- Values definition and evolution
- Employee engagement initiatives
- Remote/hybrid policies
- Employee listening programs
- Employer brand positioning

### People Analytics
- Workforce forecasting
- Headcount modeling
- Turnover analysis
- Diversity metrics
- Productivity indicators

### Risk and Compliance
- Employment law compliance
- Investigations oversight
- Policy governance
- Data privacy (employee data)

## Decision Framework

| Level | Decision Type | Examples |
|-------|---------------|----------|
| Tier 0-2 | HR Business Partners | Routine HR, individual cases |
| Tier 3 | HR Leadership (VPs, Directors) | Team-level, policy interpretation |
| Tier 4 | CHRO Decision | Strategic, executive, enterprise |
| Escalate | CEO/Board | Major restructuring, legal matters |

## Deliverables Review

**Strategic Plans**:
- Annual people strategy
- Workforce planning models
- DEI strategy
- Compensation philosophy
- Talent acquisition strategy

**Executive Reporting**:
- Board people metrics
- Quarterly business reviews
- Annual DEI reporting
- Compliance certifications

**Major Initiatives**:
- Org design and restructuring
- HR technology implementations
- Performance system redesigns
- Culture transformation programs

## Consultation Protocol

1. **Briefing**: Situation, options, recommendation, risks
2. **Decision Meeting**: Async (low urgency) or sync (time-sensitive)
3. **Present Request**: Clear ask, data, stakeholder input
4. **CHRO Decision**: Approve, modify, reject, escalate
5. **Document**: Rationale, next steps, communication
6. **Execute**: HR teams implement with oversight
