# Benefits Administration Templates

## Open Enrollment Timeline

**6-8 weeks before**: Plan renewals, rate negotiations
**4 weeks before**: Communication campaign launch
**3-4 weeks**: Enrollment period (employee elections)
**2 weeks after**: Process elections, confirmations
**Jan 1**: New plan year begins

## Enrollment Communication Plan

- Email announcements and reminders
- Benefits fair or webinar sessions
- One-on-one consultations
- FAQ documents and decision tools
- Manager talking points

## New Hire Enrollment Process

**Day 1**: Provide benefits overview (30-day deadline)
**Week 1**: Send detailed guide and instructions
**Week 2-3**: Follow up, offer consultation
**Day 30**: Enrollment deadline

**Steps**:
1. Complete enrollment in platform
2. Select medical, dental, vision
3. Designate beneficiaries
4. Enroll in 401k, select contributions
5. Review and confirm elections

## Life Event Changes

**Qualifying Events** (allow mid-year changes):
- Marriage or divorce
- Birth or adoption
- Loss of other coverage
- Employment status change
- Death of dependent
- Medicare eligibility

**Process**:
1. Report within 30 days
2. Provide documentation
3. Update elections
4. Notify carriers
5. Adjust payroll deductions

## Compliance Checklists

### ACA (Affordable Care Act)
- [ ] Offer affordable coverage (30+ hrs/week)
- [ ] File 1094-C and 1095-C annually
- [ ] Track employee hours for eligibility

### COBRA
- [ ] Notify of rights upon termination
- [ ] Administer elections (102% premium)
- [ ] Timely notifications (14-44 days)

### ERISA
- [ ] Maintain Summary Plan Descriptions
- [ ] File Form 5500 annually
- [ ] Fiduciary compliance (401k)

### HIPAA
- [ ] Protect health information
- [ ] Secure transmission to carriers
- [ ] Staff privacy training

## Vendor Management

**Insurance Carriers**:
- Negotiate renewals and rates
- Resolve claims and appeals
- Monitor service quality

**401k Provider**:
- Plan administration and compliance
- Annual audits (large plans)
- Fee transparency

**Benefits Platform**:
- HRIS/benefits system admin
- Data accuracy and integration
- Reporting
