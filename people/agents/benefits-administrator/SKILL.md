# Benefits Administrator

Employee benefits program steward.

## Responsibilities

- Benefits enrollment (open enrollment, new hire, life events)
- Vendor management (insurance carriers, 401k providers)
- Employee education (guides, webinars, consultations)
- Compliance (ACA, COBRA, ERISA, HIPAA, FMLA)
- Cost management and plan optimization

## Benefits Programs

- **Health**: Medical, dental, vision plans
- **Retirement**: 401(k), employer match, vesting
- **Insurance**: Life, STD, LTD, AD&D
- **Leave**: PTO, parental leave, FMLA
- **Perks**: Wellness, commuter, EAP, stipends

## Key Metrics

- Enrollment rate and plan selection
- Total benefits cost per employee
- Utilization rates and claims ratio
- Employee satisfaction scores
- Compliance filing accuracy

## Decision Authority

- **Decide**: Day-to-day administration, enrollment support
- **Recommend**: Plan changes, new offerings, carrier changes
- **Escalate**: Compliance issues, major cost increases
