# Process Audit Frameworks

## Audit Methodology

### Audit Phases
1. **Planning**: Scope, objectives, risk assessment
2. **Fieldwork**: Testing, evidence gathering, interviews
3. **Reporting**: Findings, recommendations, communication
4. **Follow-up**: Remediation tracking, verification

## Fieldwork

### Control Testing
```yaml

  Control: [Control description]
  Objective: [What should the control do]
  Test Procedure:
    1. [Step 1]
    2. [Step 2]
    3. [Step 3]

  Sample: [Size and selection method]
```

## Finding Classification

### Severity Levels
| Level | Definition | Response Time |
|-------|------------|---------------|
| Critical | Significant risk, material impact | Immediate |
| High | Substantial risk, control gap | 30 days |
| Medium | Moderate risk, improvement needed | 90 days |
| Low | Minor issue, best practice | 180 days |

### Finding Template
```markdown
## Finding: [Title]

**Severity**: High
**Area**: [Process/Department]

### Condition
What was found (the problem)

### Criteria
What should have been (the standard)

### Cause
Why it happened (root cause)

### Effect
What is the risk/impact

### Recommendation
What should be done

### Management Response
[Auditee response and action plan]
```

## Audit Report

### Report Structure
1. **Executive Summary** (1 page)
   - Overall assessment
   - Key findings summary
   - Critical recommendations

2. **Detailed Findings**
   - Each finding with criteria, condition, effect
   - Recommendations
   - Management responses

3. **Appendix**
   - Scope and methodology
   - Documents reviewed
   - Testing results

### Rating Scale
| Rating | Definition |
|--------|------------|
| Satisfactory | Controls effective, minor issues |
| Needs Improvement | Some gaps, moderate risk |
| Unsatisfactory | Significant gaps, high risk |
